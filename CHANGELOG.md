## Unreleased

### Added

- Coupled standard maps in `Chaoslab.Systems` (catalog rows Y3 and Y4, module `coupled-maps`, decisions D103 to D105): `FroeschleMap` and `WrappedFroeschleMap` (the 4D map of Skokos2010 Eq. (16), state `SVector{4}`), `StandardMapChain` and `WrappedStandardMapChain` (N coupled standard maps, 2 <= N <= 100, open ends or ring, any vector state or a fixed-size `SVector` of length 2N, wrapped 4N), the banded tangent `ChainTangent` (isbits, its product with a fixed-size frame stays fixed-size and costs O(N)), in-place forms and banded frame actions that allocate nothing, and `symplectic_residual` for dense matrices. Verified at module-build stage 04: closed forms (K = 0, origin spectra from the chain Laplacian), bitwise decoupled limits against the standard map, 256-bit pairing, the published orbits C and D, ChaosTools, and GPU against CPU for Y3 and Y4 through the tangent stream.
- `GALI` reducer in `Chaoslab.Indicators` (Generalized Alignment Index GALI_k, catalog row I3, module `gali`, decisions D106 to D108): one column-normalising reducer over K columns (2 <= K <= d), last in its chain as the one replacer, reporting GALI_k for every order k = 2..K from one nested modified Gram-Schmidt of the unit columns (each column orthogonalised twice, fixed-size buffers, no library factorisation, no allocation after warm-up), the floor `gali_floor(T, k) = 2 k eps` carried in the output, the compensated log sums of every column (the first two bitwise those of `SALI`) and for K = d the floor-free log form `L` (bitwise the sali `L` at d = 2); `gali_values`, `gali_value`, `gali_floor`, `check_frame(GALI(), W0)`; a zero, subnormal or non-finite column norm is a health failure. Verified at module-build stage 04: closed forms, 256-bit and 512-bit references along Y3 and Y4 orbits, the laws at hyperbolic and stable fixed points and on orbits C and D, ChaosTools `gali` for k = 2, 3, 4, Y4 at N = 100 through the stream, and the local GPU (Float64 kernel; Double64 falls back); spec Amendment D108 corrects the log-form bound (lifted-state factor) and several law constants and data; Amendment D109 forms the CPU-GPU orbit-divergence term for d >= 4 (gated, PASS) and makes `gali_values` and `gali_value` reject d < K, the non-static path type-stable (returns a `Vector`, bitwise the static path, first call 0.12 s at d = 200) and accept non-float element types. Catalog row I3 `verified`; row I4 stays `planned` (no source defines LGALI).
- `Chaoslab.Integrators`, fixed-step integrators for Hamiltonian flows (module `integrator-catalog`, catalog rows S1 to S9, S11 to S13, P1 to P3, G1 to G3, R1; decisions D111 to D114). One generic stepper runs every explicit splitting or composition method as a coefficient table (`SplittingMethod`, isbits, validated sum conditions): `SymplecticEuler`, `Leapfrog`, `McLachlan2`, `Ruth3`, `ForestRuth`, `PEFRL`, `CalvoSanzSerna4`, `Yoshida` (orders 6 and 8), `KahanLi`, `SofroniouSpaletta10`, the composition builders `triple_jump` and `suzuki_jump`, `SABA` and `SBAB` (n = 1 to 10, from Gauss-Legendre and Gauss-Lobatto nodes), `ABA864`, `ABAH864` and the corrected `SABAC`, `SBABC` (`CorrectedMethod`). `Gauss` (s-stage Gauss-Legendre) and `ImplicitMidpoint` with the fixed-point solver of Hairer2008 Sect. 3 and compensated stage and update sums (energy error follows Brouwer's law), a failure status instead of a silent value; `TaoExtended` for nonseparable flows (Tao2016, with Yoshida's exponent 1/(l - 1) for the triple jump); `taylor_reference` in the TaylorIntegration extension (`ChaoslabTaylorExt`, weak dependency). `advance`, `advance_n` (merged kicks, optional compensated update), `advance_to`, `advance_checked`, `tangent_advance` (tangent map by the same splitting), `IntegratorMap` (the one-step map as a Systems map), `advance_ensemble` (one KernelAbstractions kernel over orbits, bitwise across thread counts; CUDA hook `ensemble_backend`), metadata `force_evaluations` (long-run cost per step), `isolated_force_evaluations`, `nominal_order`, `stage_count`, `adjoint_method`. The method's coefficient type, the state's element type and a floating step size must agree (`ArgumentError`). Supported size n <= 25 degrees of freedom with the full tangent frame, n <= 50 for the state (risk K22). Hamiltonian flows in `Chaoslab.Systems` (`AbstractFlow`, `AbstractSeparableFlow`, `HarmonicOscillator`, `Pendulum`, `Kepler`, `HenonHeilesFlow`, `TaoNonseparable`, `CountingFlow`, with drifts, kicks, their Jacobians and exact flows where they exist; systems catalog rows F1, F2 and F4). Verified at module-build stage 04: full-size suite 248 241 assertions, 0 fail; oracles OrdinaryDiffEqSymplecticRK 2.2.3 (one-step maps), TaylorIntegration (reference orbits), REBOUND coefficient arrays (ABA864 b4 confirmed to 2.2e-39) and the OrdinaryDiffEqSDIRK implicit midpoint pass; independent reviews without CRITICAL or HIGH finding. Not run: the GPU device runs (`verify/gpu.jl`), criterion 3(b2) and the independent read of ForestRuth1990 (named in the catalog qualifiers). Catalog rows S10 and R2 stay `planned` (sources not read).

### Fixed
