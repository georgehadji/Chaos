# Bibliography: Corrections to the seed list

Layer 3, canonical. Annotated entries; records in `references.bib`.

- Panichi, Gozdziewski & Turchetti 2017: MNRAS 468, 469 (not CNSNS).
- Lega et al. 2000 LCE paper: authors are Lega, Penna, Froeschle, Celletti (IJBC 10, 2791), not Lega-Guzzo-Froeschle.
- 'Maffione 2011' is three distinct papers: CeMDA 111, 285 (comparison on maps; = arXiv:1108.2196); IJNLM 46, 23 (MEGNO tests); and the 'variational chaos indicators vs ODE integrators' paper is Darriba, Maffione, Cincotta, Giordano, IJBC 22 (2012) 1230033.
- Fouchard et al. 2002 (CeMDA 83, 205) is FLI vs periodic orbits (introduces OFLI), not FLI vs MEGNO; the FLI-MEGNO analytic paper is Mestre, Cincotta & Giordano 2011, MNRAS 414, L100.
- Mogavero & Laskar 'A&A 2022' long-term inner-planet paper is 2021 (A&A 655, A1); the 2022 A&A item is L3 (origin of chaos).
- Barrio 2005 OFLI2 is 'Sensitivity tools vs. Poincare sections', Chaos Solitons Fractals 25, 711.
- Pihajoki: CeMDA 121 (online 2014, vol 2015); Tao 2016: PRE 94, 043303; Rein & Spiegel IAS15: MNRAS 446 (online Nov 2014, vol Jan 2015).
- Skokos 2010 LCE review is Lect. Notes Phys. 790, 63-135 (arXiv:0811.0882).
- Rein, Dey & Tamayo 2025 arXiv:2507.04987 is actually Rein, Brown & Kanda 2025, OJAp 8 (ReinBrownKanda2025).
- Batygin & Laughlin 2008 abstract has no '~1%' figure (that figure is Laskar & Gastineau 2009, quoted by Zeebe 2015).
- Greene & Kim 1989 (Physica D 36, 83) is a metric-tensor paper, not obviously SVD.
- Kovacs arXiv:2607.13629 = Universe 12(7), 203; Hernandez arXiv:2603.24456 = ApJ 1005, 25; Bazzani et al. arXiv:2304.08340 = PRE 107, 064209.
- Crossref record doi:10.1017/s0252921100072572 spells the first author 'Efthymioulos'; corrected to Efthymiopoulos (EfthymiopoulosContopoulosVoglis1999), matching the same author's 1997 record (literature-review run standard-map-stickiness, 2026-10-12).
