# Bibliography: Chaos indicators (variational, Fourier/frequency, other)

Layer 3, canonical. Annotated entries; records in `references.bib`.

- **Benettin1980a** G. Benettin et al. (1980). Lyapunov characteristic exponents for smooth dynamical systems and for Hamiltonian systems; a method for computing all of them. Part 1: Theory. *Meccanica* 15(1), 9-20. doi:10.1007/bf02128236 -- Theory behind the standard (renormalise + Gram-Schmidt) LCE algorithm.
- **Benettin1980b** G. Benettin et al. (1980). Lyapunov characteristic exponents for smooth dynamical systems and for Hamiltonian systems; a method for computing all of them. Part 2: Numerical application. *Meccanica* 15(1), 21-30. doi:10.1007/bf02128237 -- Numerical algorithm for the full LCE spectrum; baseline for mLCE comparisons.
- **Wolf1985** A. Wolf et al. (1985). Determining Lyapunov exponents from a time series. *Physica D* 16(3), 285-317. doi:10.1016/0167-2789(85)90011-9 -- Classic algorithms, incl. equation-based spectrum method and time-series estimate.
- **Skokos2001** Ch. Skokos (2001). Alignment indices: a new, simple method for determining the ordered or chaotic nature of orbits. *Journal of Physics A: Mathematical and General* 34(47), 10029-10043. doi:10.1088/0305-4470/34/47/309 -- Introduces SALI (tested on 2D/4D maps and Hénon-Heiles).
- **Skokos2004** Ch. Skokos et al. (2004). Detecting order and chaos in Hamiltonian systems by the SALI method. *Journal of Physics A: Mathematical and General* 37(24), 6269-6284. doi:10.1088/0305-4470/37/24/006 -- Establishes SALI ~ exp(-(s1-s2)t) for chaotic orbits and a non-zero plateau for regular ones.
- **Skokos2007** Ch. Skokos, T. C. Bountis & Ch. Antonopoulos (2007). Geometrical properties of local dynamics in Hamiltonian systems: the Generalized Alignment Index (GALI) method. *Physica D* 231(1), 30-54. doi:10.1016/j.physd.2007.04.004 -- Defines GALI_k and derives its decay laws for chaotic and regular (torus) motion.
- **Skokos2008** Ch. Skokos, T. C. Bountis & Ch. Antonopoulos (2008). Detecting chaos, determining the dimensions of tori and predicting slow diffusion in Fermi-Pasta-Ulam lattices by the Generalized Alignment Index method. *European Physical Journal Special Topics* 165(1), 5-14. doi:10.1140/epjst/e2008-00844-2 -- GALI in high-dimensional lattices (probably what the requested 'GALI method ... local dynamics, 2008' refers to; that exact title was not found).
- **Skokos2010** Ch. Skokos (2010). The Lyapunov characteristic exponents and their computation. *Lecture Notes in Physics, vol. 790* 790, 63-135. doi:10.1007/978-3-642-04458-8_2 -- Survey of mLCE/full-spectrum algorithms (QR, SVD, discrete/continuous), Oseledec theorem; regular-orbit finite-time mLCE decays ~ ln t / t. (arXiv:0811.0882)
- **SkokosGerlach2010** Ch. Skokos & E. Gerlach (2010). Numerical integration of variational equations. *Physical Review E* 82(3), 036704. doi:10.1103/physreve.82.036704 -- Tangent-map (TM) method for variational equations of symplectic systems; ties integrator choice to indicator cost.
- **Froeschle1997** C. Froeschlé, E. Lega & R. Gonczi (1997). Fast Lyapunov indicators. Application to asteroidal motion. *Celestial Mechanics and Dynamical Astronomy* 67(1), 41-62. doi:10.1023/a:1008276418601 -- Introduces the FLI.
- **FroeschleLega2000** C. Froeschlé & E. Lega (2000). On the structure of symplectic mappings. The fast Lyapunov indicator: a very sensitive tool. *Celestial Mechanics and Dynamical Astronomy* 78(1-4), 167-195. doi:10.1023/a:1011141018230 -- FLI on symplectic maps (resonance/Arnold-web structure).
- **Fouchard2002** M. Fouchard et al. (2002). On the relationship between fast Lyapunov indicator and periodic orbits for continuous flows. *Celestial Mechanics and Dynamical Astronomy* 83(1-4), 205-222. doi:10.1023/a:1020199201070 -- FLI for flows, periodic-orbit link, introduces OFLI. NOTE: not an FLI-vs-MEGNO comparison (seed description corrected).
- **Barrio2005** R. Barrio (2005). Sensitivity tools vs. Poincaré sections. *Chaos, Solitons & Fractals* 25(3), 711-726. doi:10.1016/j.chaos.2004.11.092 -- The OFLI2/OFLI sensitivity-tool paper (the 'Barrio 2005 OFLI2' of the seed).
- **Barrio2006** R. Barrio (2006). Painting chaos: a gallery of sensitivity plots of classical problems. *International Journal of Bifurcation and Chaos* 16(10), 2777-2798. doi:10.1142/s021812740601646x -- Gallery of OFLI2 maps for classical systems.
- **CincottaSimo2000** P. M. Cincotta & C. Simó (2000). Simple tools to study global dynamics in non-axisymmetric galactic potentials - I. *Astronomy and Astrophysics Supplement Series* 147(2), 205-228. doi:10.1051/aas:2000108 -- Introduces MEGNO.
- **Cincotta2003** P. M. Cincotta, C. M. Giordano & C. Simó (2003). Phase space structure of multi-dimensional systems by means of the mean exponential growth factor of nearby orbits. *Physica D* 182(3-4), 151-178. doi:10.1016/s0167-2789(03)00103-9 -- MEGNO theory, tests on 3D quartic oscillator and canonical maps.
- **Mestre2011** M. F. Mestre, P. M. Cincotta & C. M. Giordano (2011). Analytical relation between two chaos indicators: FLI and MEGNO. *Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society: Letters* 414(1), L100-L103. doi:10.1111/j.1745-3933.2011.01065.x -- Analytical FLI-MEGNO link (the genuine 'FLI vs MEGNO' paper).
- **Maffione2011a** N. P. Maffione et al. (2011). A comparison of different indicators of chaos based on the deviation vectors: application to symplectic mappings. *Celestial Mechanics and Dynamical Astronomy* 111(3), 285-307. doi:10.1007/s10569-011-9373-z -- Core comparison on maps (LI, MEGNO, SALI, FLI, SSN/D, RLI); recommends FLI+RLI globally, MEGNO+SALI per orbit. = arXiv:1108.2196.
- **Maffione2011b** N. P. Maffione, C. M. Giordano & P. M. Cincotta (2011). Testing a fast dynamical indicator: the MEGNO. *International Journal of Non-Linear Mechanics* 46(1), 23-34. doi:10.1016/j.ijnonlinmec.2010.06.008 -- MEGNO-specific reliability tests. DISTINCT from Maffione2011a and Darriba2012.
- **Darriba2012** L. A. Darriba et al. (2012). Comparative study of variational chaos indicators and ODEs' numerical integrators. *International Journal of Bifurcation and Chaos* 22(10), 1230033. doi:10.1142/s0218127412300339 -- Flow-system (Hénon-Heiles) comparison of LI, MEGNO, SALI/GALI, FLI/OFLI vs integrator choice. First author Darriba, year 2012 (not 'Maffione 2011').
- **Maffione2013** N. P. Maffione et al. (2013). Chaos detection tools: application to a self-consistent triaxial model. *Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society* 429(3), 2700-2717. doi:10.1093/mnras/sts539 -- Same indicator set applied to a self-consistent N-body model.
- **Hinse2010** T. C. Hinse et al. (2010). Application of the MEGNO technique to the dynamics of Jovian irregular satellites. *Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society* 404(2), 837-857. doi:10.1111/j.1365-2966.2010.16307.x -- Contains a MEGNO theory review with asymptotic laws (quoted in benchmarks).
- **LNP915** Ch. Skokos, G. A. Gottwald & J. Laskar (2016). Chaos Detection and Predictability. *Lecture Notes in Physics, vol. 915 (Springer)*. doi:10.1007/978-3-662-48410-4 -- Edited volume: reference set for FLI, OFLI, MEGNO, SALI/GALI, RLI, 0-1 test, frequency analysis.
- **LegaGuzzoFroeschle2016** E. Lega, M. Guzzo & C. Froeschlé (2016). Theory and applications of the Fast Lyapunov Indicator (FLI) method. *Chaos Detection and Predictability, LNP 915* 915, 35-54. doi:10.1007/978-3-662-48410-4_2 -- LNP915 chapter on FLI.
- **Barrio2016** R. Barrio (2016). Theory and applications of the Orthogonal Fast Lyapunov Indicator (OFLI and OFLI2) methods. *Chaos Detection and Predictability, LNP 915* 915, 55-92. doi:10.1007/978-3-662-48410-4_3 -- LNP915 chapter on OFLI/OFLI2.
- **CincottaGiordano2016** P. M. Cincotta & C. M. Giordano (2016). Theory and applications of the Mean Exponential Growth factor of Nearby Orbits (MEGNO) method. *Chaos Detection and Predictability, LNP 915* 915, 93-128. doi:10.1007/978-3-662-48410-4_4 -- LNP915 chapter on MEGNO.
- **SkokosManos2016** Ch. Skokos & T. Manos (2016). The Smaller (SALI) and the Generalized (GALI) Alignment Indices: efficient methods of chaos detection. *Chaos Detection and Predictability, LNP 915* 915, 129-181. doi:10.1007/978-3-662-48410-4_5 -- LNP915 chapter on SALI/GALI (arXiv:1412.7401 verified as the preprint of this chapter).
- **SandorMaffione2016** Zs. Sándor & N. P. Maffione (2016). The Relative Lyapunov Indicators: theory and application to dynamical astronomy. *Chaos Detection and Predictability, LNP 915* 915, 183-220. doi:10.1007/978-3-662-48410-4_6 -- LNP915 RLI chapter (Sándor-Maffione follow-up; symplectic maps and flows; arXiv:1501.07264).
- **Sandor2004** Zs. Sándor et al. (2004). The Relative Lyapunov Indicator: an efficient method of chaos detection. *Celestial Mechanics and Dynamical Astronomy* 90(1-2), 127-138. doi:10.1007/s10569-004-8129-4 -- Introduces RLI.
- **Laskar1990** J. Laskar (1990). The chaotic motion of the solar system: a numerical estimate of the size of the chaotic zones. *Icarus* 88(2), 266-291. doi:10.1016/0019-1035(90)90084-m -- Frequency analysis (NAFF) applied to the Solar System; chaotic-zone map.
- **LaskarFroeschleCelletti1992** J. Laskar, C. Froeschlé & A. Celletti (1992). The measure of chaos by the numerical analysis of the fundamental frequencies. Application to the standard mapping. *Physica D* 56(2-3), 253-269. doi:10.1016/0167-2789(92)90028-l -- Frequency-map analysis on the standard map: direct precedent for Fourier-based comparison.
- **Laskar1993** J. Laskar (1993). Frequency analysis for multi-dimensional systems. Global dynamics and diffusion. *Physica D* 67(1-3), 257-281. doi:10.1016/0167-2789(93)90210-r -- Frequency Map Analysis; diffusion-based chaos measure.
- **Laskar2003** J. Laskar (2003). Frequency map analysis and quasi periodic decompositions. *arXiv:math/0305364 (preprint; later appeared as a book chapter, venue not verified)*. https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0305364 -- Describes refined-Fourier/quasi-periodic approximation basis of FMA.
- **Chandre2003** C. Chandre, S. Wiggins & T. Uzer (2003). Time-frequency analysis of chaotic systems. *Physica D* 181(3-4), 171-196. doi:10.1016/s0167-2789(03)00117-9 -- Wavelet time-frequency chaos analysis. (ScienceDirect pii S0167278903001179 resolves to exactly this article.)
- **Gomez2010a** G. Gómez, J.-M. Mondelo & C. Simó (2010). A collocation method for the numerical Fourier analysis of quasi-periodic functions. I: Numerical tests and examples. *Discrete and Continuous Dynamical Systems - Series B* 14(1), 41-74. doi:10.3934/dcdsb.2010.14.41 -- Refined Fourier analysis by collocation (part I).
- **Gomez2010b** G. Gómez, J.-M. Mondelo & C. Simó (2010). A collocation method for the numerical Fourier analysis of quasi-periodic functions. II: Analytical error estimates. *Discrete and Continuous Dynamical Systems - Series B* 14(1), 75-109. doi:10.3934/dcdsb.2010.14.75 -- Error estimates for collocation Fourier analysis (part II).
- **Bartolini1996** R. Bartolini et al. (1996). Tune evaluation in simulations and experiments. *Particle Accelerators* 52, 147-177. https://inspirehep.net/literature?q=Tune%20evaluation%20in%20simulations%20and%20experiments [verified-secondary] -- Precision of frequency (tune) estimation from finite orbits (interpolated FFT, windowing). NO DOI: confirmed via INSPIRE-HEP record (Part. Accel. 52, 147-177); companion INSPIRE entry: 'Precise measurement of the betatron tune', Part. Accel. 55, 1-10 (1996).
- **GottwaldMelbourne2004** G. A. Gottwald & I. Melbourne (2004). A new test for chaos in deterministic systems. *Proceedings of the Royal Society A* 460(2042), 603-611. doi:10.1098/rspa.2003.1183 -- 0-1 test for chaos (time series, no tangent dynamics).
- **Das2017** S. Das et al. (2017). Quantitative quasiperiodicity. *Nonlinearity* 30(11), 4111-4140. doi:10.1088/1361-6544/aa84c2 -- Weighted Birkhoff averages: fast-converging quasiperiodicity/rotation-number detector for maps.
- **Panichi2017** F. Panichi, K. Goździewski & G. Turchetti (2017). The reversibility error method (REM): a new, dynamical fast indicator for planetary dynamics. *Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society* 468(1), 469-491. doi:10.1093/mnras/stx374 -- REM from time-reversal integration error. CORRECTION: MNRAS 468, not CNSNS.
- **BountisSkokos2012** T. Bountis & H. Skokos (2012). Complex Hamiltonian Dynamics. *Springer Series in Synergetics*. doi:10.1007/978-3-642-27305-6 -- Textbook coverage of chaos indicators, FPU, GALI/SALI.
- **Voglis1994** N. Voglis & G. Contopoulos (1994). Invariant spectra of orbits in dynamical systems. *Journal of Physics A: Mathematical and General* 27(14), 4899-4909. doi:10.1088/0305-4470/27/14/017 -- Spectra of stretching numbers as a chaos indicator.
- **Voglis1999** N. Voglis, G. Contopoulos & C. Efthymiopoulos (1999). Detection of ordered and chaotic motion using the dynamical spectra. *Celestial Mechanics and Dynamical Astronomy* 73(1-4), 211-220. doi:10.1023/a:1008307332442 -- Dynamical spectra of stretching numbers (SSN); used with the spectral distance D in Maffione2011a.
- **GiordanoCincotta2018** C. M. Giordano & P. M. Cincotta (2018). The Shannon entropy as a measure of diffusion in multidimensional dynamical systems. *Celestial Mechanics and Dynamical Astronomy* 130(5), 35. doi:10.1007/s10569-018-9832-x -- Entropy-based diffusion indicator.
- **Celletti2022** A. Celletti et al. (2022). Classification of regular and chaotic motions in Hamiltonian systems with deep learning. *Scientific Reports* 12, 1890. doi:10.1038/s41598-022-05696-9 -- Deep-learning classifier of regular vs chaotic orbits in Hamiltonian systems.
- **LeeFlach2020** W. S. Lee & S. Flach (2020). Deep learning of chaos classification. *Machine Learning: Science and Technology* 1(4), 045019. doi:10.1088/2632-2153/abb6d3 -- ML classifier on the standard map; compared with LCE computation (finite-time accuracy trade-off).
- **Zimper2023** S. Zimper et al. (2023). Performance of chaos diagnostics based on Lagrangian descriptors. Application to the 4D standard map. *Physica D* 453, 133833. doi:10.1016/j.physd.2023.133833 -- Recent 4D-standard-map indicator benchmark.
- **Bazzani2023** A. Bazzani et al. (2023). Performance analysis of indicators of chaos for nonlinear dynamical systems. *Physical Review E* 107(6), 064209. doi:10.1103/physreve.107.064209 -- Predictive-power comparison of chaos indicators on orbits of limited length, on a symplectic Hénon-like cubic map. = arXiv:2304.08340 (the user's PRE link). Core to finite-time comparison.

On the behavior of Linear Dependence, Smaller, and Generalized Alignment Indices in discrete and continuous chaotic systems. *Chaos, Solitons & Fractals* 205, 117884. doi:10.1016/j.chaos.2026.117884 -- LDI/SALI/GALI behaviour in maps and flows.

Application to the Hénon-Heiles model and to the restricted three-body problem. *arXiv preprint 1005.5611*. https://arxiv.org/abs/1005.5611 -- Symplectic integration of tangent/deviation vectors for SALI/MEGNO-type indicators on Hénon-Heiles. - **Kovacs2026** T. Kovács (2026). Advanced techniques in stability analysis of trans-Neptunian objects. *Universe* 12(7), 203. doi:10.3390/universe12070203 -- Chaos indicators applied to TNO stability (= arXiv:2607.13629).
- **Benettin1976** G. Benettin, L. Galgani & J.-M. Strelcyn (1976). Kolmogorov entropy and numerical experiments. *Physical Review A* 14(6), 2338-2345. doi:10.1103/physreva.14.2338 -- Early numerical mLCE / Kolmogorov-entropy estimation on Hamiltonian systems (precursor to the 1980 algorithm).
- **Marwan2007** N. Marwan et al. (2007). Recurrence plots for the analysis of complex systems. *Physics Reports* 438(5-6), 237-329. doi:10.1016/j.physrep.2006.11.001 -- Recurrence-plot / RQA review: an alternative orbit-based regularity measure.
- **Mancho2013** A. M. Mancho et al. (2013). Lagrangian descriptors: a method for revealing phase space structures of general time dependent dynamical systems. *Communications in Nonlinear Science and Numerical Simulation* 18(12), 3530-3557. doi:10.1016/j.cnsns.2013.05.002 -- Defines Lagrangian descriptors (arc-length-based phase-space diagnostics; basis of Zimper2023).
