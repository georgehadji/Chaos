# Bibliography: Symplectic integrators and geometric numerical integration

Layer 3, canonical. Annotated entries; records in `references.bib`.

- **Ruth1983** R. D. Ruth (1983). A canonical integration technique. *IEEE Transactions on Nuclear Science* 30(4), 2669-2671. doi:10.1109/tns.1983.4332919 -- First explicit symplectic 3rd-order scheme. (Crossref OCR title is garbled as 'Can0nical Integrati0n'.)
- **ForestRuth1990** E. Forest & R. D. Ruth (1990). Fourth-order symplectic integration. *Physica D* 43(1), 105-117. doi:10.1016/0167-2789(90)90019-l -- 4th-order symmetric composition (FR4).
- **CandyRozmus1991** J. Candy & W. Rozmus (1991). A symplectic integration algorithm for separable Hamiltonian functions. *Journal of Computational Physics* 92(1), 230-256. doi:10.1016/0021-9991(91)90299-z -- Independent derivation of 4th-order symplectic scheme.
- **Yoshida1990** H. Yoshida (1990). Construction of higher order symplectic integrators. *Physics Letters A* 150(5-7), 262-268. doi:10.1016/0375-9601(90)90092-3 -- Triple-jump composition to arbitrary even order.
- **Suzuki1990** M. Suzuki (1990). Fractal decomposition of exponential operators with applications to many-body theories and Monte Carlo simulations. *Physics Letters A* 146(6), 319-323. doi:10.1016/0375-9601(90)90962-n -- Fractal decomposition giving higher-order splitting schemes.
- **McLachlan1995** R. I. McLachlan (1995). On the numerical integration of ordinary differential equations by symmetric composition methods. *SIAM Journal on Scientific Computing* 16(1), 151-168. doi:10.1137/0916010 -- Optimised symmetric composition methods.
- **McLachlanQuispel2002** R. I. McLachlan & G. R. W. Quispel (2002). Splitting methods. *Acta Numerica* 11, 341-434. doi:10.1017/s0962492902000053 -- Comprehensive splitting-method review.
- **BlanesMoan2002** S. Blanes & P. C. Moan (2002). Practical symplectic partitioned Runge-Kutta and Runge-Kutta-Nyström methods. *Journal of Computational and Applied Mathematics* 142(2), 313-330. doi:10.1016/s0377-0427(01)00492-7 -- Efficient high-order SPRK/RKN coefficients for Hamiltonian flows.
- **Omelyan2002** I. P. Omelyan, I. M. Mryglod & R. Folk (2002). Optimized Forest-Ruth- and Suzuki-like algorithms for integration of motion in many-body systems. *Computer Physics Communications* 146(2), 188-202. doi:10.1016/s0010-4655(02)00451-4 -- PEFRL: 4th-order scheme with small error constant.
- **KahanLi1997** W. Kahan & R.-C. Li (1997). Composition constants for raising the orders of unconventional schemes for ordinary differential equations. *Mathematics of Computation* 66(219), 1089-1099. doi:10.1090/s0025-5718-97-00873-9 -- Composition constants for raising order of symmetric schemes.
- **SofroniouSpaletta2005** M. Sofroniou & G. Spaletta (2005). Derivation of symmetric composition constants for symmetric integrators. *Optimization Methods and Software* 20(4-5), 597-613. doi:10.1080/10556780500140664 -- Systematic derivation of composition constants.
- **LaskarRobutel2001** J. Laskar & P. Robutel (2001). High order symplectic integrators for perturbed Hamiltonian systems. *Celestial Mechanics and Dynamical Astronomy* 80(1), 39-62. doi:10.1023/a:1012098603882 -- SABA/SBAB schemes for near-integrable H = A + eB.
- **Blanes2013** S. Blanes et al. (2013). New families of symplectic splitting methods for numerical integration in dynamical astronomy. *Applied Numerical Mathematics* 68, 58-72. doi:10.1016/j.apnum.2013.01.003 -- Optimised splitting families for perturbed (planetary) problems.
- **Farres2013** A. Farrés et al. (2013). High precision symplectic integrators for the Solar System. *Celestial Mechanics and Dynamical Astronomy* 116(2), 141-174. doi:10.1007/s10569-013-9479-6 -- Companion paper: precision of splitting schemes on the Solar System.
- **WisdomHolman1991** J. Wisdom & M. Holman (1991). Symplectic maps for the n-body problem. *The Astronomical Journal* 102, 1528. doi:10.1086/115978 -- Wisdom-Holman map (Kepler drift + interaction kick). (Crossref gives start page only.)
- **WisdomHolmanTouma1996** J. Wisdom, M. Holman & J. Touma (1996). Symplectic correctors. *Integration Algorithms and Classical Mechanics, Fields Institute Communications 10* 10, 217-244. doi:10.1090/fic/010/14 -- Correctors improving WH accuracy.
- **ReinLiu2012** H. Rein & S.-F. Liu (2012). REBOUND: an open-source multi-purpose N-body code for collisional dynamics. *Astronomy & Astrophysics* 537, A128. doi:10.1051/0004-6361/201118085 -- REBOUND code paper.
- **ReinTamayo2015** H. Rein & D. Tamayo (2015). WHFast: a fast and unbiased implementation of a symplectic Wisdom-Holman integrator for long-term gravitational simulations. *Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society* 452(1), 376-388. doi:10.1093/mnras/stv1257 -- WHFast.
- **ReinSpiegel2015** H. Rein & D. S. Spiegel (2015). IAS15: a fast, adaptive, high-order integrator for gravitational dynamics, accurate to machine precision over a billion orbits. *Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society* 446(2), 1424-1437. doi:10.1093/mnras/stu2164 -- IAS15 non-symplectic reference integrator (Crossref online date Nov 2014; vol 446 = Jan 2015).
- **Rein2019** H. Rein, D. Tamayo & G. Brown (2019). High-order symplectic integrators for planetary dynamics and their implementation in REBOUND. *Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society* 489(4), 4632-4640. doi:10.1093/mnras/stz2503 -- SABA / higher-order WH integrators in REBOUND.
- **Rein2019b** H. Rein, G. Brown & D. Tamayo (2019). On the accuracy of symplectic integrators for secularly evolving planetary systems. *Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society* 490(4), 5122-5133. doi:10.1093/mnras/stz2942 -- Secular accuracy of symplectic schemes (shadow-Hamiltonian-type errors).
- **Rein2019hybrid** H. Rein et al. (2019). Hybrid symplectic integrators for planetary dynamics. *Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society* 485(4), 5490-5497. doi:10.1093/mnras/stz769 -- MERCURIUS hybrid integrator for close encounters (author list truncated after Lau, per Crossref listing).

- **HaddenTamayo2022** S. Hadden & D. Tamayo (2022). celmech: a Python package for celestial mechanics. *The Astronomical Journal* 164(5), 179. doi:10.3847/1538-3881/ac8d01 -- Perturbation-theory / secular-resonance dynamics package.
