# Bibliography: Lyapunov-exponent algorithms

Layer 3, canonical. Annotated entries; records in `references.bib`.

- **Oseledec1968** V. I. Oseledec (1968). A multiplicative ergodic theorem. Lyapunov characteristic numbers for dynamical systems. *Transactions of the Moscow Mathematical Society* 19, 197-231. https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/mmo214 [verified-secondary] -- Foundation: existence of LCEs. NO DOI: confirmed via Math-Net.Ru (Russian original Trudy Mosk. Mat. Obsc. 19, 179-210; English transl. pp. 197-231).
- **ShimadaNagashima1979** I. Shimada & T. Nagashima (1979). A numerical approach to ergodic problem of dissipative dynamical systems. *Progress of Theoretical Physics* 61(6), 1605-1616. doi:10.1143/ptp.61.1605 -- Gram-Schmidt computation of the LCE spectrum.
- **EckmannRuelle1985** J.-P. Eckmann & D. Ruelle (1985). Ergodic theory of chaos and strange attractors. *Reviews of Modern Physics* 57(3), 617-656. doi:10.1103/revmodphys.57.617 -- Review of ergodic theory / LCEs.
- **Eckmann1986** J.-P. Eckmann et al. (1986). Liapunov exponents from time series. *Physical Review A* 34(6), 4971-4979. doi:10.1103/physreva.34.4971 -- LCE estimation from time series.
- **GreeneKim1987** J. M. Greene & J.-S. Kim (1987). The calculation of Lyapunov spectra. *Physica D* 24(1-3), 213-225. doi:10.1016/0167-2789(87)90076-5 -- SVD/QR spectrum computation.
- **GreeneKim1989** J. M. Greene & J.-S. Kim (1989). Introduction of a metric tensor into linearized evolution equations. *Physica D* 36(1-2), 83-91. doi:10.1016/0167-2789(89)90249-2 -- Physica D 36, 83 is this paper (metric tensor in linearised evolution), not obviously a 'continuous SVD' paper; treat the SVD description as unconfirmed.
- **Goldhirsch1987** I. Goldhirsch, P.-L. Sulem & S. A. Orszag (1987). Stability and Lyapunov stability of dynamical systems: a differential approach and a numerical method. *Physica D* 27(3), 311-337. doi:10.1016/0167-2789(87)90034-0 -- Differential (continuous) approach to LCE computation.
- **HabibRyne1995** S. Habib & R. D. Ryne (1995). Symplectic calculation of Lyapunov exponents. *Physical Review Letters* 74(1), 70-73. doi:10.1103/physrevlett.74.70 -- LCE computation preserving symplectic structure.
- **Geist1990** K. Geist, U. Parlitz & W. Lauterborn (1990). Comparison of different methods for computing Lyapunov exponents. *Progress of Theoretical Physics* 83(5), 875-893. doi:10.1143/ptp.83.875 -- Comparison of Gram-Schmidt / Householder / continuous-QR methods. Bibliographic data confirmed; its CPU-time table could NOT be accessed (see UNVERIFIED).
- **DanielGragg1976** J. W. Daniel et al. (1976). Reorthogonalization and stable algorithms for updating the Gram-Schmidt QR factorization. *Mathematics of Computation* 30(136), 772-795. doi:10.1090/s0025-5718-1976-0431641-8 -- Stable Gram-Schmidt reorthogonalization (basis for RGS in LCE codes).
- **Householder1958** A. S. Householder (1958). Unitary triangularization of a nonsymmetric matrix. *Journal of the ACM* 5(4), 339-342. doi:10.1145/320941.320947 -- Householder QR (H1/H2-type orthogonalisation in LCE algorithms).
- **DieciVanVleck1995** L. Dieci & E. S. Van Vleck (1995). Computation of a few Lyapunov exponents for continuous and discrete dynamical systems. *Applied Numerical Mathematics* 17(3), 275-291. doi:10.1016/0168-9274(95)00033-q -- QR methods for a few LCEs.
- **Dieci1997** L. Dieci, R. D. Russell & E. S. Van Vleck (1997). On the computation of Lyapunov exponents for continuous dynamical systems. *SIAM Journal on Numerical Analysis* 34(1), 402-423. doi:10.1137/s0036142993247311 -- Continuous/discrete QR and SVD methods with error analysis.
- **DieciVanVleck2002** L. Dieci & E. S. Van Vleck (2002). Lyapunov spectral intervals: theory and computation. *SIAM Journal on Numerical Analysis* 40(2), 516-542. doi:10.1137/s0036142901392304 -- Spectral intervals (QR-based numerical theory).
- **DieciVanVleck2005** L. Dieci & E. S. Van Vleck (2005). On the error in computing Lyapunov exponents by QR methods. *Numerische Mathematik* 101, 619-642. doi:10.1007/s00211-005-0644-z -- Error analysis of QR methods.
- **DieciVanVleck2007** L. Dieci & E. S. Van Vleck (2007). Lyapunov and Sacker-Sell spectral intervals. *Journal of Dynamics and Differential Equations* 19(2), 265-293. doi:10.1007/s10884-006-9030-5 -- Lyapunov vs Sacker-Sell spectra (online 2006; issue 2007).
- **DieciJollyVanVleck2010** L. Dieci, M. S. Jolly & E. S. Van Vleck (2010). Numerical techniques for approximating Lyapunov exponents and their implementation. *Journal of Computational and Nonlinear Dynamics* 6(1), 011003. doi:10.1115/1.4002088 -- Algorithms and codes (LESLIS/LESLIL family); the 'DJVfinal.pdf' preprint is presumably this paper (link not opened).
- **McDonaldHigham2001** E. J. McDonald & D. J. Higham (2001). Error analysis of QR algorithms for computing Lyapunov exponents. *Electronic Transactions on Numerical Analysis* 12, 234-251. https://strathprints.strath.ac.uk/173/ [verified-secondary] -- Error analysis of QR LCE algorithms (Strathprints record 173 confirmed: McDonald & Higham, ETNA 12, 234-251). DOI not retrieved.
- **Rangarajan1998** G. Rangarajan, S. Habib & R. D. Ryne (1998). Lyapunov exponents without rescaling and reorthogonalization. *Physical Review Letters* 80(17), 3747-3750. doi:10.1103/physrevlett.80.3747 -- LCEs without renormalisation.
- **ChristiansenRugh1997** F. Christiansen & H. H. Rugh (1997). Computing Lyapunov spectra with continuous Gram-Schmidt orthonormalization. *Nonlinearity* 10(5), 1063-1072. doi:10.1088/0951-7715/10/5/004 -- Continuous Gram-Schmidt formulation.
- **RamasubramanianSriram2000** K. Ramasubramanian & M. S. Sriram (2000). A comparative study of computation of Lyapunov spectra with different algorithms. *Physica D* 139(1-2), 72-86. doi:10.1016/s0167-2789(99)00234-1 -- Comparison of QR/SVD-type algorithms.
- **vonBremen1997** H. F. von Bremen, F. E. Udwadia & W. Proskurowski (1997). An efficient QR based method for the computation of Lyapunov exponents. *Physica D* 101, 1-16. doi:10.1016/s0167-2789(96)00216-3 -- Efficient QR variant (ScienceDirect pii S0167278996002163 = this article).
- **ChenDjidjeliPrice2006** Z.-M. Chen, K. Djidjeli & W. G. Price (2006). Computing Lyapunov exponents based on the solution expression of the variational system. *Applied Mathematics and Computation* 174, 982-996. doi:10.1016/j.amc.2005.05.016 -- pii S0096300305005254 resolves to this article (variational-system solution expression); I did not confirm the 'simple discrete QR' characterisation.
- **Fiedler2024** R. Fiedler, H. Hetzler & S. Bäuerle (2024). Efficient numerical calculation of Lyapunov-exponents and stability assessment for quasi-periodic motions in nonlinear systems. *Nonlinear Dynamics* 112, 8299-8327. doi:10.1007/s11071-024-09497-9 -- LE computation for quasi-periodic motions.
- **Ginelli2007** F. Ginelli et al. (2007). Characterizing dynamics with covariant Lyapunov vectors. *Physical Review Letters* 99(13), 130601. doi:10.1103/physrevlett.99.130601 -- Covariant Lyapunov vector algorithm.
- **Ginelli2013** F. Ginelli et al. (2013). Covariant Lyapunov vectors. *Journal of Physics A: Mathematical and Theoretical* 46(25), 254005. doi:10.1088/1751-8113/46/25/254005 -- CLV review.
- **WolfeSamelson2007** C. L. Wolfe & R. M. Samelson (2007). An efficient method for recovering Lyapunov vectors from singular vectors. *Tellus A* 59(3), 355. doi:10.1111/j.1600-0870.2007.00234.x -- CLVs from singular vectors (start page only confirmed).
- **KuptsovParlitz2012** P. V. Kuptsov & U. Parlitz (2012). Theory and computation of covariant Lyapunov vectors. *Journal of Nonlinear Science* 22(5), 727-762. doi:10.1007/s00332-012-9126-5 -- CLV theory/computation review.
- **Froyland2013** G. Froyland et al. (2013). Computing covariant Lyapunov vectors, Oseledets vectors, and dichotomy projectors: a comparative numerical study. *Physica D* 247(1), 18-39. doi:10.1016/j.physd.2012.12.005 -- Comparison of CLV / Oseledets-vector algorithms (pii S0167278912003090 confirmed identical).
- **Rosenstein1993** M. T. Rosenstein, J. J. Collins & C. J. De Luca (1993). A practical method for calculating largest Lyapunov exponents from small data sets. *Physica D* 65(1-2), 117-134. doi:10.1016/0167-2789(93)90009-p -- Time-series mLCE from short data.
- **Kantz1994** H. Kantz (1994). A robust method to estimate the maximal Lyapunov exponent of a time series. *Physics Letters A* 185(1), 77-87. doi:10.1016/0375-9601(94)90991-1 -- Robust time-series mLCE.
- **PikovskyPoliti2016** A. Pikovsky & A. Politi (2016). Lyapunov Exponents: A Tool to Explore Complex Dynamics. *Cambridge University Press*. doi:10.1017/cbo9781139343473 -- Monograph (Crossref e-book date Dec 2015; print 2016).
- **Haller2001** G. Haller (2001). Distinguished material surfaces and coherent structures in three-dimensional fluid flows. *Physica D* 149(4), 248-277. doi:10.1016/s0167-2789(00)00199-8 -- FTLE ridges / Lagrangian coherent structures.
- **Shadden2005** S. C. Shadden, F. Lekien & J. E. Marsden (2005). Definition and properties of Lagrangian coherent structures from finite-time Lyapunov exponents in two-dimensional aperiodic flows. *Physica D* 212(3-4), 271-304. doi:10.1016/j.physd.2005.10.007 -- FTLE definition/properties.
- **Lega2000** E. Lega et al. (2000). On the computation of Lyapunov exponents for discrete time series: applications to two-dimensional symplectic and dissipative mappings. *International Journal of Bifurcation and Chaos* 10(12), 2791-2805. doi:10.1142/s0218127400001857 -- LCEs from finite map time series on symplectic maps. CORRECTION: authors are Lega, Penna, Froeschlé, Celletti (not Lega-Guzzo-Froeschlé).
