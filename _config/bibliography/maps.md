# Bibliography: Maps and model systems

Layer 3, canonical. Annotated entries; records in `references.bib`.

- **Chirikov1979** B. V. Chirikov (1979). A universal instability of many-dimensional oscillator systems. *Physics Reports* 52(5), 263-379. doi:10.1016/0370-1573(79)90023-1 -- Resonance-overlap criterion; origin of the standard map and of the large-K logarithmic law for its Lyapunov exponent.
- **Greene1979** J. M. Greene (1979). A method for determining a stochastic transition. *Journal of Mathematical Physics* 20(6), 1183-1201. doi:10.1063/1.524170 -- Residue criterion; golden-mean torus breakup in the standard map defines the benchmark K_c.
- **MacKay1983** R. S. MacKay (1983). A renormalisation approach to invariant circles in area-preserving maps. *Physica D* 7(1-3), 283-300. doi:10.1016/0167-2789(83)90131-8 -- Renormalisation explanation of Greene's critical K; universality of the critical golden curve.
- **Henon1969** M. Hénon (1969). Numerical study of quadratic area-preserving mappings. *Quarterly of Applied Mathematics* 27(3), 291-312. doi:10.1090/qam/253513 -- The quadratic area-preserving (Hénon) map: second 2D symplectic test map besides the standard map.
- **HenonHeiles1964** M. Hénon & C. Heiles (1964). The applicability of the third integral of motion: some numerical experiments. *The Astronomical Journal* 69, 73. doi:10.1086/109234 -- Original Hénon-Heiles Hamiltonian and Poincaré-section study; the 2-DOF flow benchmark. (Crossref gives start page only.)
- **Froeschle1972** C. Froeschlé (1972). Numerical study of a four-dimensional mapping. *Astronomy and Astrophysics* 16, 172-189. https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/1972A&A....16..172F [verified-secondary] -- Original 4D coupled-map (Froeschlé map) study; testbed for GALI/SALI in 4D symplectic maps. NO DOI: confirmed only via several independent reference lists (A&A 16, 172-189).
- **Meiss1992** J. D. Meiss (1992). Symplectic maps, variational principles, and transport. *Reviews of Modern Physics* 64(3), 795-848. doi:10.1103/revmodphys.64.795 -- Standard review on symplectic maps, cantori, and transport/stickiness.
- **MacKayMeissPercival1984** R. S. MacKay, J. D. Meiss & I. C. Percival (1984). Transport in Hamiltonian systems. *Physica D* 13(1-2), 55-81. doi:10.1016/0167-2789(84)90270-7 -- Cantori as partial barriers; theory behind stickiness that biases finite-time indicators.
- **Karney1983** C. F. F. Karney (1983). Long-time correlations in the stochastic regime. *Physica D* 8(3), 360-380. doi:10.1016/0167-2789(83)90232-4 -- Numerical demonstration of stickiness/long-time correlations near islands in area-preserving maps.
- **LichtenbergLieberman1992** A. J. Lichtenberg & M. A. Lieberman (1992). Regular and Chaotic Dynamics, 2nd edition. *Springer*. doi:10.1007/978-1-4757-2184-3 -- Textbook reference for standard-map phenomenology, resonance overlap and Lyapunov exponents.
- **FrahmShepelyansky2010** K. M. Frahm & D. L. Shepelyansky (2010). Ulam method for the Chirikov standard map. *European Physical Journal B* 76, 57-68. doi:10.1140/epjb/e2010-00190-6 -- Verifiable source (read in full text) for the quoted critical value K_g = 0.971635406.
- **CarlettiLaskar2000** T. Carletti & J. Laskar (2000). Scaling law in the Standard Map critical function. Interpolating Hamiltonian and frequency map analysis. *Nonlinearity* 13(6), 2033-2061. doi:10.1088/0951-7715/13/6/308 -- Second source (arXiv nlin/0003040 text) for K_crit = 0.971635... and Mather's bound K_c < 4/3.
- **Shevchenko2004** I. I. Shevchenko (2004). On the maximum Lyapunov exponent of the motion in a chaotic layer. *JETP Letters* 79(11), 523-528. doi:10.1134/1.1787098 -- Describes Chirikov's ln(K/2) law for the standard-map Lyapunov exponent and numerical deviations (stickiness spikes). Read via arXiv:1605.08623.
- **Zotos2017** E. E. Zotos (2017). An overview of the escape dynamics in the Hénon-Heiles Hamiltonian system. *Meccanica* 52, 2615-2630. doi:10.1007/s11012-017-0647-8 -- States the Hénon-Heiles escape energy h_esc = 1/6 and the three saddle points (read via arXiv:1709.04360).
- **ZhaoDu2007** H. J. Zhao & M. L. Du (2007). Escape rates of the Hénon-Heiles system. *arXiv preprint nlin/0701028*. https://arxiv.org/abs/nlin/0701028 -- Secondary statement: motion regular for E < 1/12, chaotic fraction grows until E = 1/6 (preprint, not peer-reviewed).
- **BarrioBlesaSerrano2008** R. Barrio, F. Blesa & S. Serrano (2008). Fractal structures in the Hénon-Heiles Hamiltonian. *EPL (Europhysics Letters)* 82(1), 10003. doi:10.1209/0295-5075/82/10003 -- Hénon-Heiles escape/basin structure; cross-check for indicator maps on this system.
- **Simo2014** C. Simó (2014). Measuring the total amount of chaos in some Hamiltonian systems. *Manuscript submitted to AIMS Journals (preprint PDF, grey literature)*. http://www.maia.ub.es/dsg/2014/1405simo.pdf [verified-secondary] -- Hénon-Heiles variants in 2 and 3 DOF; fraction of positive-Lyapunov initial conditions vs energy. Preprint hosted at maia.ub.es/dsg/2014/1405simo.pdf; final journal version NOT verified.
- **Contopoulos2002** G. Contopoulos (2002). Order and Chaos in Dynamical Astronomy. *Springer*. doi:10.1007/978-3-662-04917-4 -- Monograph on order/chaos in Hamiltonian astronomy (maps, Hénon-Heiles, stretching numbers).
- **Balescu1997** R. Balescu (1997). Continuous time random walk model for standard map dynamics. *Physical Review E* 55(3), 2465-2474. doi:10.1103/physreve.55.2465 -- Gives a continuous-time random-walk description of standard-map chaotic orbits bounded by KAM barriers, relevant to trapping-time statistics (M-SQ1) and also to accelerator-mode transport (M-SQ2).
- **ChirikovShepelyansky1999** B. V. Chirikov & D. L. Shepelyansky (1999). Asymptotic Statistics of Poincaré Recurrences in Hamiltonian Systems with Divided Phase Space. *Physical Review Letters* 82(3), 528-531. doi:10.1103/physrevlett.82.528 -- Argues for a power-law recurrence tail with density exponent 3 at the golden critical curve of the standard map, reached only after about 1e6 iterations, and gives exit-time scales for orbits started at unstable fixed points, so it supplies trapping-time targets and sticky-orbit labels (M-SQ1, M-SQ5).
- **Zaslavsky2004** G. M. Zaslavsky (2004). Poincare´ Recurrences. *Hamiltonian Chaos and Fractional Dynamics*, 173-186. doi:10.1093/oso/9780198526049.003.0011 -- Book chapter on Poincaré recurrences in Hamiltonian chaos, background for trapping-time statistics (M-SQ1).
- **ZaslavskyEdelman2005** G. M. Zaslavsky & M. Edelman (2005). Polynomial dispersion of trajectories in sticky dynamics. *Physical Review E* 72(3). doi:10.1103/physreve.72.036204 -- Treats polynomial dispersion of trajectories and Poincaré recurrences in sticky Hamiltonian dynamics, background for trapping-time statistics (M-SQ1).
- **AltmannMotterKantz2006** E. G. Altmann, A. E. Motter & H. Kantz (2006). Stickiness in Hamiltonian systems: From sharply divided to hierarchical phase space. *Physical Review E* 73(2). doi:10.1103/physreve.73.026207 -- Shows that a sharply divided phase space (piecewise-linear maps, mushroom billiards) gives a cumulative recurrence tail exponent of 2 while a hierarchical border gives slower, oscillating decay (M-SQ1), but it runs no sine standard map, so its values do not transfer.
- **AltmannKantz2007** E. G. Altmann & H. Kantz (2007). Hypothesis of strong chaos and anomalous diffusion in coupled symplectic maps. *Europhysics Letters (EPL)* 78(1), 10008. doi:10.1209/0295-5075/78/10008 -- Identifies three recurrence-time regimes in the standard map with weak noise and in coupled standard maps (M-SQ1), but almost all its numbers belong to noisy or coupled systems and give no deterministic benchmark.
- **AltmannTel2008** E. G. Altmann & T. Tél (2008). Poincaré Recurrences from the Perspective of Transient Chaos. *Physical Review Letters* 100(17). doi:10.1103/physrevlett.100.174101 -- Shows that recurrence and escape times from a leak share one exponential rate followed by a power-law tail from sticking near the island, using a standard-map example, which indicates how long finite-time windows must be before sticky behaviour dominates (M-SQ1).
- **CristadoroKetzmerick2008** G. Cristadoro & R. Ketzmerick (2008). Universality of Algebraic Decays in Hamiltonian Systems. *Physical Review Letters* 100(18). doi:10.1103/physrevlett.100.184101 -- Conjectures a universal tail exponent near 1.57 from a Markov-tree model and cubic-potential area-preserving maps, not the standard map, and serves mainly as a warning that single-system finite-time exponents scatter (M-SQ1).
- **Venegeroles2009** R. Venegeroles (2009). Universality of Algebraic Laws in Hamiltonian Systems. *Physical Review Letters* 102(6). doi:10.1103/physrevlett.102.064101 -- Links the recurrence-tail exponent and the superdiffusion exponent by gamma + beta = 3 and conjectures 3/2 for trapping at accelerator-mode islands, pointing to K just above 2 pi l and K near golden-torus breakup as sticky regimes (M-SQ1, M-SQ2).
- **ContopoulosHarsoula2008** G. Contopoulos & M. Harsoula (2008). STICKINESS IN CHAOS. *International Journal of Bifurcation and Chaos* 18(10), 2929-2949. doi:10.1142/s0218127408022172 -- Describes standard-map stickiness at K = 5 through the manifolds of the unstable 4/9 orbit around an island, with escape-time maps and a fast-escape versus slow-escape labelling for orbits (M-SQ1, M-SQ4, M-SQ5).
- **ArtusoCavallascaCristadoro2008** R. Artuso, L. Cavallasca & G. Cristadoro (2008). Dynamical and transport properties in a family of intermittent area-preserving maps. *Physical Review E* 77(4). doi:10.1103/physreve.77.046206 -- Studies recurrence-time distributions and transport of a lift in a family of intermittent area-preserving maps, not the standard map (M-SQ1).
- **CederAgam2013** R. Ceder & O. Agam (2013). Fluctuations in the relaxation dynamics of mixed chaotic systems. *Physical Review E* 87(1). doi:10.1103/physreve.87.012918 -- Shows in a tree model that the approach to the power-law recurrence tail is very slow and the local slope fluctuates between samples, a caution against reading exponents from single curves (M-SQ1); its standard-map test is a randomised map, not the deterministic one.
- **AbuddeCarvalho2013** C. V. Abud & R. E. de Carvalho (2013). Multifractality, stickiness, and recurrence-time statistics. *Physical Review E* 88(4). doi:10.1103/physreve.88.042922 -- Relates recurrence-time statistics to stickiness near resonance islands (M-SQ1).
- **AlusFishmanMeiss2014** O. Alus, S. Fishman & J. D. Meiss (2014). Statistics of the island-around-island hierarchy in Hamiltonian phase space. *Physical Review E* 90(6). doi:10.1103/physreve.90.062923 -- Gives continued-fraction and flux-ratio statistics of the island-around-island hierarchy, computed for the Hénon quadratic map rather than the standard map, with no tail exponent (M-SQ1).
- **DatserisDiakonosSchmelcher2015** G. Datseris, F. K. Diakonos & P. Schmelcher (2015). Effective intermittency and cross correlations in the standard map. *Physical Review E* 92(1). doi:10.1103/physreve.92.012914 -- Finds three dynamically separated chaotic zones in the standard map for 0.85 < k <= 1.2 with very long zone-to-zone passage times, giving time-scale labels for sticky orbits but no Lyapunov values (M-SQ1).
- **Meiss2015** J. D. Meiss (2015). Thirty years of turnstiles and transport. *Chaos: An Interdisciplinary Journal of Nonlinear Science* 25(9). doi:10.1063/1.4915831 -- Survey of turnstiles, cantori and transport that defines exit, return and transit times and the exponent relations, plus one standard-map orbit at k = 0.975 that looks confined for 1e6 steps and escapes after 2.3e9 (M-SQ1).
- **daSilvaMancheinBeims2018** R. M. da Silva, C. Manchein & M. W. Beims (2018). Exploring conservative islands using correlated and uncorrelated noise. *Physical Review E* 97(2). doi:10.1103/physreve.97.022219 -- Gives noiseless standard-map cumulative recurrence-tail exponents of 1.55 (K = 3.28) and 1.58 (K = 4.23) and shows stickiness is stronger when the central fixed point is elliptic, which guides the choice of panel K values (M-SQ1, M-SQ5); its noisy results belong to a different system.
- **MartinsdeOliveiraCiroCaldas2020** V. Martins de Oliveira, D. Ciro & I. L. Caldas (2020). Dynamical trapping in the area-preserving Hénon map. *The European Physical Journal Special Topics* 229(8), 1507-1516. doi:10.1140/epjst/e2020-900155-8 -- Explains trapping in the open area-preserving Hénon map, a different map from the standard map, through invariant-manifold crossings of three saddles, and offers only the qualitative lesson that sticky orbits sit next to a detached resonant island chain (M-SQ1).
- **MiguelSimoVieiro2022** N. Miguel, C. Simó & A. Vieiro (2022). Escape Times Across the Golden Cantorus of the Standard Map. *Regular and Chaotic Dynamics* 27(3), 281-306. doi:10.1134/s1560354722030029 -- Treats escape times across the golden cantorus of the standard map, bearing on trapping-time statistics (M-SQ1) and on cantori (M-SQ4).
- **HomanMeiss2026** J. R. Homan & J. D. Meiss (2026). Noise-enhanced stickiness in the Harper map. *Chaos: An Interdisciplinary Journal of Nonlinear Science* 36(4). doi:10.1063/5.0303868 -- Studies recurrence and stickiness in the noisy Harper map, a different map from the standard map, and offers a recipe combining recurrence time with trapping time inside island boxes for labelling an orbit as sticky (M-SQ1, M-SQ5).
- **DanaFishman1985** I. Dana & S. Fishman (1985). Diffusion in the standard map. *Physica D: Nonlinear Phenomena* 17(1), 63-74. doi:10.1016/0167-2789(85)90134-4 -- Listed as a source on diffusion in the standard map for the accelerator-mode and anomalous-transport question (M-SQ2) (screened on title; check when read).
- **IchikawaKamimuraHatori1987** Y. H. Ichikawa, T. Kamimura & T. Hatori (1987). Stochastic diffusion in the standard map. *Physica D: Nonlinear Phenomena* 29(1-2), 247-255. doi:10.1016/0167-2789(87)90060-1 -- Listed as a source on stochastic diffusion in the standard map for the accelerator-mode and anomalous-transport question (M-SQ2) (screened on title; check when read).
- **Ishizaki1990** R. Ishizaki et al. (1990). Long-Time Correlations and Anomalous Diffusion Due to Accelerator Modes in the Standard Maps. *Progress of Theoretical Physics* 84(2), 179-184. doi:10.1143/ptp.84.179 -- Reports long-time correlations and anomalous diffusion caused by accelerator modes in the standard map (M-SQ2).
- **Ishizaki1991** R. Ishizaki et al. (1991). Anomalous Diffusion Due to Accelerator Modes in the Standard Map. *Progress of Theoretical Physics* 85(5), 1013-1022. doi:10.1143/ptp.85.1013 -- Reports anomalous diffusion caused by accelerator modes in the standard map (M-SQ2).
- **IshizakiMori1997** R. Ishizaki & H. Mori (1997). Anomalous Diffusion Induced by External Force in the Standard Map. *Progress of Theoretical Physics* 97(2), 201-211. doi:10.1143/ptp.97.201 -- Studies anomalous diffusion arising from accelerator-mode islands when the standard map is driven by an external force (M-SQ2).
- **IshizakiMori1998** R. Ishizaki & H. Mori (1998). Anomalous Diffusion Induced by External Force in the Standard Map. II. *Progress of Theoretical Physics* 100(6), 1131-1144. doi:10.1143/ptp.100.1131 -- Continues the external-force study with the nonlinear response of diffusion in the presence of accelerator-mode islands (M-SQ2).
- **Murakami1999** C. Murakami et al. (1999). Higher Order Symmetry and Bifurcation of the Period-2 Step-1 Accelerator Mode in the Standard Map. *Progress of Theoretical Physics* 102(4), 777-783. doi:10.1143/ptp.102.777 -- Analyses the bifurcation of the period-2 step-1 accelerator mode of the standard map (M-SQ2).
- **Hirose1999** K. Hirose et al. (1999). Bifurcation Scenario for the Period-3 Accelerator Modes in the Standard Map. *Journal of Plasma and Fusion Research* 75(1), 65-74. doi:10.1585/jspf.75.65 -- Covers the bifurcation scenario of the period-3 accelerator modes of the standard map (M-SQ2).
- **ManosRobnik2014** T. Manos & M. Robnik (2014). Survey on the role of accelerator modes for anomalous diffusion: The case of the standard map. *Physical Review E* 89(2). doi:10.1103/physreve.89.022905 -- Survey of the role of accelerator modes in anomalous diffusion in the standard map (M-SQ2).
- **HarsoulaContopoulos2018** M. Harsoula & G. Contopoulos (2018). Global and local diffusion in the standard map. *Physical Review E* 97(2). doi:10.1103/physreve.97.022215 -- Examines global and local diffusion exponents in the standard map, separating normal from anomalous diffusion (M-SQ2).
- **Percival1980** I. C. Percival (1980). Variational principles for invariant tori and cantori. *AIP Conference Proceedings* 57, 302-310. doi:10.1063/1.32113 -- Gives a variational principle for invariant tori and cantori, applied to the standard mapping (M-SQ3).
- **Efthymiopoulos1997** C. Efthymiopoulos et al. (1997). Stickiness and cantori. *Journal of Physics A: Mathematical and General* 30(23), 8167-8186. doi:10.1088/0305-4470/30/23/016 -- Studies stickiness in the standard map in terms of cantori with noble rotation numbers (M-SQ3, M-SQ4).
- **EfthymiopoulosContopoulosVoglis1999** C. Efthymiopoulos, G. Contopoulos & N. Voglis (1999). Cantori, Islands and Asymptotic Curves in The Stickiness Region. *International Astronomical Union Colloquium* 172, 221-230. doi:10.1017/s0252921100072572 -- Proceedings paper on cantori, islands and asymptotic curves in the stickiness region (M-SQ3) (screened on title; check when read).
- **Livorati2012** A. L. P. Livorati et al. (2012). Stickiness in a bouncer model: A slowing mechanism for Fermi acceleration. *Physical Review E* 86(3). doi:10.1103/physreve.86.036203 -- Uses finite-time Lyapunov exponents to visualise trapping and measures survival probability in a 2D measure-preserving bouncer map, not the standard map (M-SQ3).
- **MancheinBeimsRost2012** C. Manchein, M. W. Beims & J. M. Rost (2012). Characterizing the dynamics of higher dimensional nonintegrable conservative systems. *Chaos: An Interdisciplinary Journal of Nonlinear Science* 22(3). doi:10.1063/1.4748855 -- Examines the effect of sticky motion on finite-time Lyapunov statistics in higher-dimensional conservative systems rather than the 2D standard map (M-SQ3).
- **AlusFishmanMeiss2017** O. Alus, S. Fishman & J. D. Meiss (2017). Universal exponent for transport in mixed Hamiltonian dynamics. *Physical Review E* 96(3). doi:10.1103/physreve.96.032204 -- Gives a survival-probability power law near the island-around-island hierarchy in area-preserving maps, linking stickiness to hierarchy statistics (M-SQ3, M-SQ1).
- **Santos2019** M. S. Santos et al. (2019). Using rotation number to detect sticky orbits in Hamiltonian systems. *Chaos: An Interdisciplinary Journal of Nonlinear Science* 29(4). doi:10.1063/1.5078533 -- Proposes the rotation number as a detector of sticky orbits in Hamiltonian systems (M-SQ3).
- **Sales2023** M. R. Sales et al. (2023). Stickiness and recurrence plots: An entropy-based approach. *Chaos: An Interdisciplinary Journal of Nonlinear Science* 33(3). doi:10.1063/5.0140613 -- Uses recurrence-time entropy as a stickiness indicator that correlates with the largest Lyapunov exponent, with entropy modes matching island-hierarchy levels (M-SQ3).
- **Graciano2024** F. H. Graciano et al. (2024). Investigating the Stickiness in a Potential Well. *International Journal of Bifurcation and Chaos* 34(13). doi:10.1142/s021812742430026x -- Applies finite-time Lyapunov exponent statistics to stickiness in a potential-well map (M-SQ3).
- **BerrettiChierchia1990** A. Berretti & L. Chierchia (1990). On the complex analytic structure of the golden invariant curve for the standard map. *Nonlinearity* 3(1), 39-44. doi:10.1088/0951-7715/3/1/003 -- Examines the analytic structure of the golden invariant curve of the standard map (M-SQ4).
- **MarmiStark1992** S. Marmi & J. Stark (1992). On the standard map critical function. *Nonlinearity* 5(3), 743-761. doi:10.1088/0951-7715/5/3/007 -- Studies the standard-map critical function, which bears on the breakdown threshold of KAM circles (M-SQ4).
- **YamaguchiTanikawa1999** Y. Yamaguchi & K. Tanikawa (1999). A Remark on the Smoothness of Critical KAM Curves in the Standard Mapping. *Progress of Theoretical Physics* 101(1), 1-24. doi:10.1143/ptp.101.1 -- Addresses the smoothness of critical KAM curves in the standard mapping (M-SQ4).
- **SearaVillanueva2000** T. M. Seara & J. Villanueva (2000). Asymptotic behaviour of the domain of analyticity of invariant curves of the standard map. *Nonlinearity* 13(5), 1699-1744. doi:10.1088/0951-7715/13/5/315 -- Treats the analyticity domain of the golden invariant curve of the standard map (M-SQ4).
- **Calleja2017** R. C. Calleja et al. (2017). Global transport in a nonautonomous periodic standard map. *Communications in Nonlinear Science and Numerical Simulation* 51, 198-215. doi:10.1016/j.cnsns.2017.04.004 -- Studies a nonautonomous variant of the standard map, covering destruction of invariant circles and the boundary of global transport (M-SQ4).
- **NietoCapeansSanjuan2024** A. R. Nieto, R. Capeáns & M. A. F. Sanjuán (2024). Systematic search for islets of stability in the standard map for large parameter values. *Nonlinear Dynamics* 112(12), 10147-10155. doi:10.1007/s11071-024-09566-z -- Reports a systematic search for islets of stability in the standard map at large K (M-SQ4).
- **Eckhardt1993** B. Eckhardt (1993). Periodic orbits and diffusion in standard maps. *Physics Letters A* 172(6), 411-415. doi:10.1016/0375-9601(93)90231-n -- Listed as a source on periodic orbits and diffusion in standard maps for orbit selection (M-SQ5) (screened on title; check when read).
- **ShigangYouqin1993** C. Shi-gang & W. You-qin (1993). Measure of chaotic orbits for standard map. *Acta Physica Sinica (Overseas Edition)* 2(7), 481-489. doi:10.1088/1004-423x/2/7/001 -- Measures the share of chaotic orbits in the standard map as a function of K, useful for orbit selection (M-SQ5).

Disentangling regular and chaotic motion in the standard map using complex network analysis of recurrences in phase space. *Chaos: An Interdisciplinary Journal of Nonlinear Science* 26(2). doi:10.1063/1.4942584 -- Applies complex-network analysis of recurrences to separate regular from chaotic orbits in the standard map (M-SQ5).
- **Zhou2012** L.-Y. Zhou et al. (2012). Hyperbolic Structure and Stickiness Effect: A case of a 2D Area-Preserving Twist Mapping. *arXiv preprint 1212.3673*. arXiv:1212.3673 -- Studies the stickiness effect and hyperbolic structure in a 2D area-preserving twist map, which is not necessarily the standard map (M-SQ3).
