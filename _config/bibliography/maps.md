# Bibliography: Maps and model systems

Layer 3, canonical. Annotated entries; records in `references.bib`.

- **Chirikov1979** B. V. Chirikov (1979). A universal instability of many-dimensional oscillator systems. *Physics Reports* 52(5), 263-379. doi:10.1016/0370-1573(79)90023-1 -- Resonance-overlap criterion; origin of the standard map and of the large-K logarithmic law for its Lyapunov exponent.
- **Greene1979** J. M. Greene (1979). A method for determining a stochastic transition. *Journal of Mathematical Physics* 20(6), 1183-1201. doi:10.1063/1.524170 -- Residue criterion; golden-mean torus breakup in the standard map defines the benchmark K_c.
- **MacKay1983** R. S. MacKay (1983). A renormalisation approach to invariant circles in area-preserving maps. *Physica D* 7(1-3), 283-300. doi:10.1016/0167-2789(83)90131-8 -- Renormalisation explanation of Greene's critical K; universality of the critical golden curve.
- **Henon1969** M. Hénon (1969). Numerical study of quadratic area-preserving mappings. *Quarterly of Applied Mathematics* 27(3), 291-312. doi:10.1090/qam/253513 -- The quadratic area-preserving (Hénon) map: second 2D symplectic test map besides the standard map.
- **HenonHeiles1964** M. Hénon & C. Heiles (1964). The applicability of the third integral of motion: some numerical experiments. *The Astronomical Journal* 69, 73. doi:10.1086/109234 -- Original Hénon-Heiles Hamiltonian and Poincaré-section study; the 2-DOF flow benchmark. (Crossref gives start page only.)
- **Froeschle1972** C. Froeschlé (1972). Numerical study of a four-dimensional mapping. *Astronomy and Astrophysics* 16, 172-189. https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/1972A&A....16..172F [verified-secondary] -- Original 4D coupled-map (Froeschlé map) study; testbed for GALI/SALI in 4D symplectic maps. NO DOI: confirmed only via several independent reference lists (A&A 16, 172-189).
- **Meiss1992** J. D. Meiss (1992). Symplectic maps, variational principles, and transport. *Reviews of Modern Physics* 64(3), 795-848. doi:10.1103/revmodphys.64.795 -- Standard review on symplectic maps, cantori, and transport/stickiness.
- **MacKayMeissPercival1984** R. S. MacKay, J. D. Meiss & I. C. Percival (1984). Transport in Hamiltonian systems. *Physica D* 13(1-2), 55-81. doi:10.1016/0167-2789(84)90270-7 -- Cantori as partial barriers; theory behind stickiness that biases finite-time indicators.
- **Karney1983** C. F. F. Karney (1983). Long-time correlations in the stochastic regime. *Physica D* 8(3), 360-380. doi:10.1016/0167-2789(83)90232-4 -- Numerical demonstration of stickiness/long-time correlations near islands in area-preserving maps.
- **LichtenbergLieberman1992** A. J. Lichtenberg & M. A. Lieberman (1992). Regular and Chaotic Dynamics, 2nd edition. *Springer*. doi:10.1007/978-1-4757-2184-3 -- Textbook reference for standard-map phenomenology, resonance overlap and Lyapunov exponents.
- **FrahmShepelyansky2010** K. M. Frahm & D. L. Shepelyansky (2010). Ulam method for the Chirikov standard map. *European Physical Journal B* 76, 57-68. doi:10.1140/epjb/e2010-00190-6 -- Verifiable source (read in full text) for the quoted critical value K_g = 0.971635406.
- **CarlettiLaskar2000** T. Carletti & J. Laskar (2000). Scaling law in the Standard Map critical function. Interpolating Hamiltonian and frequency map analysis. *Nonlinearity* 13(6), 2033-2061. doi:10.1088/0951-7715/13/6/308 -- Second source (arXiv nlin/0003040 text) for K_crit = 0.971635... and Mather's bound K_c < 4/3.
- **Shevchenko2004** I. I. Shevchenko (2004). On the maximum Lyapunov exponent of the motion in a chaotic layer. *JETP Letters* 79(11), 523-528. doi:10.1134/1.1787098 -- Describes Chirikov's ln(K/2) law for the standard-map Lyapunov exponent and numerical deviations (stickiness spikes). Read via arXiv:1605.08623.
- **Zotos2017** E. E. Zotos (2017). An overview of the escape dynamics in the Hénon-Heiles Hamiltonian system. *Meccanica* 52, 2615-2630. doi:10.1007/s11012-017-0647-8 -- States the Hénon-Heiles escape energy h_esc = 1/6 and the three saddle points (read via arXiv:1709.04360).
- **ZhaoDu2007** H. J. Zhao & M. L. Du (2007). Escape rates of the Hénon-Heiles system. *arXiv preprint nlin/0701028*. https://arxiv.org/abs/nlin/0701028 -- Secondary statement: motion regular for E < 1/12, chaotic fraction grows until E = 1/6 (preprint, not peer-reviewed).
- **BarrioBlesaSerrano2008** R. Barrio, F. Blesa & S. Serrano (2008). Fractal structures in the Hénon-Heiles Hamiltonian. *EPL (Europhysics Letters)* 82(1), 10003. doi:10.1209/0295-5075/82/10003 -- Hénon-Heiles escape/basin structure; cross-check for indicator maps on this system.
- **Simo2014** C. Simó (2014). Measuring the total amount of chaos in some Hamiltonian systems. *Manuscript submitted to AIMS Journals (preprint PDF, grey literature)*. http://www.maia.ub.es/dsg/2014/1405simo.pdf [verified-secondary] -- Hénon-Heiles variants in 2 and 3 DOF; fraction of positive-Lyapunov initial conditions vs energy. Preprint hosted at maia.ub.es/dsg/2014/1405simo.pdf; final journal version NOT verified.
- **Contopoulos2002** G. Contopoulos (2002). Order and Chaos in Dynamical Astronomy. *Springer*. doi:10.1007/978-3-662-04917-4 -- Monograph on order/chaos in Hamiltonian astronomy (maps, Hénon-Heiles, stretching numbers).
