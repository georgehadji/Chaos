# Bibliography: Solar-system dynamics

Layer 3, canonical. Annotated entries; records in `references.bib`.

- **SussmanWisdom1988** G. J. Sussman & J. Wisdom (1988). Numerical evidence that the motion of Pluto is chaotic. *Science* 241(4864), 433-437. doi:10.1126/science.241.4864.433 -- First demonstration of chaos in the outer Solar System (Pluto).
- **SussmanWisdom1992** G. J. Sussman & J. Wisdom (1992). Chaotic evolution of the solar system. *Science* 257(5066), 56-62. doi:10.1126/science.257.5066.56 -- ~100 Myr Digital Orrery integration; Solar System chaotic, exponential divergence time ~4 Myr.
- **Laskar1989** J. Laskar (1989). A numerical experiment on the chaotic behaviour of the Solar System. *Nature* 338(6212), 237-238. doi:10.1038/338237a0 -- Secular-equation integration showing chaotic inner planets.
- **Laskar1994** J. Laskar (1994). Large-scale chaos in the solar system. *Astronomy and Astrophysics* 287, L9-L12. https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/1994A&A...287L...9L [verified-secondary] -- Extended chaos / Mercury-instability result. NO DOI: confirmed via two independent citing lists only (A&A 287, L9-L12).
- **Laskar1996** J. Laskar (1996). Large scale chaos and marginal stability in the solar system. *Celestial Mechanics and Dynamical Astronomy* 64(1-2), 115-162. doi:10.1007/bf00051610 -- Long follow-up of the 1994 result.
- **LaskarGastineau2009** J. Laskar & M. Gastineau (2009). Existence of collisional trajectories of Mercury, Mars and Venus with the Earth. *Nature* 459(7248), 817-819. doi:10.1038/nature08096 -- 2501 solutions over 5 Gyr; ~1% with large Mercury eccentricity growth.
- **BatyginLaughlin2008** K. Batygin & G. Laughlin (2008). On the dynamical stability of the Solar System. *The Astrophysical Journal* 683(2), 1207-1216. doi:10.1086/589232 -- Independent instability analysis (Mercury destabilised via secular resonance with Jupiter; outer planets stable over 24 Gyr). Note: its abstract does not give a ~1% figure.
- **MurrayHolman1999** N. Murray & M. Holman (1999). The origin of chaos in the outer Solar System. *Science* 283(5409), 1877-1881. doi:10.1126/science.283.5409.1877 -- Analytic origin of Jovian chaos; Lyapunov time ~10 Myr.
- **Zeebe2015** R. E. Zeebe (2015). Highly stable evolution of Earth's future orbit despite chaotic behavior of the Solar System. *The Astrophysical Journal* 811(1), 9. doi:10.1088/0004-637x/811/1/9 -- 1600 integrations x 5 Gyr; stresses integrator accuracy effects on statistics.
- **MogaveroLaskar2021** F. Mogavero & J. Laskar (2021). Long-term dynamics of the inner planets in the Solar System. *Astronomy & Astrophysics* 655, A1. doi:10.1051/0004-6361/202141007 -- This is the Mogavero & Laskar paper the seed dates '2022'; it is 2021 (A&A 655, A1).
- **MogaveroLaskar2022** F. Mogavero & J. Laskar (2022). The origin of chaos in the Solar System through computer algebra. *Astronomy & Astrophysics* 662, L3. doi:10.1051/0004-6361/202243327 -- Identifies the resonances responsible for inner-planet chaos.
- **Hoang2022** N. H. Hoang, F. Mogavero & J. Laskar (2022). Long-term instability of the inner Solar System: numerical experiments. *Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society* 514(1), 1342-1350. doi:10.1093/mnras/stac1299 -- Truncated secular models; instability probability and Mercury half-life context.
- **MogaveroHoangLaskar2023** F. Mogavero, N. H. Hoang & J. Laskar (2023). Timescales of chaos in the inner Solar System: Lyapunov spectrum and quasi-integrals of motion. *Physical Review X* 13(2), 021018. doi:10.1103/physrevx.13.021018 -- Full Lyapunov spectrum of the inner planets; abstract states Lyapunov time of 5 Myr.
- **Park2021** R. S. Park et al. (2021). The JPL planetary and lunar ephemerides DE440 and DE441. *The Astronomical Journal* 161(3), 105. doi:10.3847/1538-3881/abd414 -- Reference ephemerides (initial conditions for Solar System runs).
- **MurrayDermott1999** C. D. Murray & S. F. Dermott (1999). Solar System Dynamics. *Cambridge University Press*. doi:10.1017/cbo9781139174817 -- Textbook (Crossref DOI date Feb 2000; ISBNs 9780521572958 hb, 9780521575973 pb).
- **Morbidelli2002** A. Morbidelli (2002). Modern Celestial Mechanics: Aspects of Solar System Dynamics. *Taylor & Francis*. https://www-n.oca.eu/morby/celmech.pdf [verified-secondary] -- Textbook, Advances in Astronomy and Astrophysics vol. 5; ISBNs seen in catalogues: 0415279399 and 0415279380 (sources disagree on which is hb/pb). No DOI. Author posts a PDF at https://www-n.oca.eu/morby/celmech.pdf.
