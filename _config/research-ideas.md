## Prior art to verify before claiming any gap

User-supplied review, 2026-10-14 ("Numerical indicators of chaos in Hamiltonian systems: a structured literature review"; its claims are UNVERIFIED until literature-review/03-verify): symplecticity is not a certificate that a computed label belongs to the target flow; documented timestep-dependent labels and discretization-induced chaos in the integrable Toda chain; energy error is not a proxy for secular-frequency accuracy; bit-wise reversibility is not accuracy.
