# Indicators

Chaos indicators as reducers: the Smaller Alignment Index SALI, the Generalized Alignment Index `GALI` (GALI_k for every order 2..K of K columns, the log form for K = d, `gali_values`, `gali_value`, `gali_floor`), the Fast Lyapunov Indicator FLI, the MEGNO, the Relative Lyapunov Indicator RLI with its pair start, and the stretching-number spectra `StretchingNumbers` (cumulative histograms of the per-handoff log growth of two deviation vectors, kept in a per-orbit workspace, with the spectral distance D, `stretching_range` and the run check `check_run`), their pure functions and the frame checks. Frequency map analysis of an orbit series (catalog rows I12 and I12b): `window_frequency` and `frequency_map` (the windowed Fourier frequency of a window and of a pair of windows with the cosine window family of order 0 to 5, the stationary point of the squared transform, the mirror and tie flags), `circle_distance`, `frequency_distance`, `frequency_index` (the index `D = log10 max(delta, 32 eps)` with its floor flag), `check_frequency_map`, and the weighted mean phase advance: the pure functions `birkhoff_weight`, `birkhoff_weights`, `phase_increments`, `weighted_phase_frequency` and the O(1) reducer `WeightedPhaseAdvance` with `PlanarObservable` and `LiftedAngleObservable` and its run check `check_run`. Weighted Birkhoff averages (catalog row I13): the pure function `weighted_birkhoff` on a stored series, the pair index `birkhoff_index` (signed difference, absolute and relative digits, floor flag) and the O(1) reader reducer `WeightedBirkhoff` for K observables of the orbit point (`CosObservable`, `SinObservable`, `LiftedObservable` or any callable) over H horizons of one pass. The Orthogonal Fast Lyapunov Indicator (catalog row I6): the reducer `OFLI(flow)` (the FLI of the deviation component orthogonal to the flow) with `ofli_initial` and a `check_frame` method.
