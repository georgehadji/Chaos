## Scope

- Catalog row L12 (`_config/catalogs/lyapunov.md`, section B): covariant Lyapunov vectors (CLVs) by the Ginelli method: forward discrete QR along the tangent dynamics storing R (and the frame at the sampled times), then backward iteration of an upper-triangular coefficient matrix by back-substitution with column normalization; outputs: the CLVs, the local (finite-time covariant) exponents, and the tangencies (angles between CLVs and between the unstable and the stable CLV subspaces); memory for stored R factors and checkpointing (the row's wording).
- Prerequisites: the delivered discrete QR (Householder and Gram-Schmidt orthonormalizers, handoff interval tau) and the tangent stream supply the forward phase; the orbit and the tangent are the same as for every other estimator (L1 S2). For a flow the dynamics is the one-step map of the integrator (the tangent is the exact derivative of that map, integrator-catalog Equations item 17, as in CQ F4): the CLVs computed are those of the numerical map (V1, A2).
- In scope: V1 to V23; the first k CLVs for k <= d; local exponents; pairwise and principal angles; the memory formula and the checkpointed variant (bitwise equal results); closed-form fixtures; a static cross-check variant in the verification environment (P12).
- Out of scope, held: L12b (Oseledets vectors by wedge-product SVD and dichotomy projectors): the only source that gives it is Froyland2013 and it is not on disk (F1), so no equation of it is written; the last m CLVs by the time-reversed dynamics (Ginelli2013 p.14, "Scalability", P1); adjoint covariant vectors and their angles with the CLVs (KuptsovParlitz2012 Eqs. (53), (54) p.11; they need a second orthonormalization pass, P11); two different handoff intervals for transients and for sampling (Ginelli2013 pp.13 to 14, P6); non-invertible maps (Ginelli2013 Sect. 4.3 p.16, V21); adaptive tau; banded or sparse tangents; GPU kernels (Compute, P9); writing CLVs to the run store (the experiments layer, P4); noise injection against false tangencies (KuptsovParlitz2012 p.16, P14).
- No catalog row is added. The constant-Jacobian maps and linear flows of the tests are suite fixtures, not systems, as in CQ.

### Findings for the owner

- F1. Sources on disk. Read: Ginelli2007 (4 pages), Ginelli2013 (review; Sect. 3.2 pp.9 to 10, Sect. 4 pp.10 to 16, Sect. 5 pp.16 to 18, Sect. 6 pp.21 to 22, Sect. 7 pp.23 to 26), KuptsovParlitz2012 (Sects. III, IV pp.9 to 17 and V pp.17 to 18, appendix pp.19 to 20). Not on disk, nothing cited: WolfeSamelson2007 (row L12 names it; its algorithm is known here only as Ginelli2013 Sect. 6.1 p.21 and KuptsovParlitz2012 Sect. IV C pp.14 to 15 report it) and Froyland2013 (L12b). The benchmarks file (`_config/bibliography/benchmarks.md`) has no CLV row: no published number is a target; the only printed exponents in a read source are those of KuptsovParlitz2012 p.18 (R6).
- F2. The algorithm is four steps (Ginelli2013 p.13, Sect. 4.2 (i) to (iv); Ginelli2007 p.2): forward transient, forward recording of the frame and R, backward transient of an arbitrary upper-triangular matrix, backward recording. Its computational weight is the forward QR; the backward step is a triangular solve (Ginelli2013 p.15). The method is exact in the sense of the numerical map (V1) and converges exponentially in both transients at a rate set by the gaps between consecutive exponents (Ginelli2013 pp.16, 17 to 18): the transient lengths are therefore system-dependent and are an owner choice (P3).
- F3. The first CLV is the first Gram-Schmidt vector (Ginelli2013 p.11, after Eq. (27); derived from the triangular C, V5); the last CLVs are the worst conditioned (R has the small diagonal entries, KuptsovParlitz2012 p.16: "ill-conditioned R"), so errors do not spread over the whole spectrum but sit in the small-exponent end. The criteria bound the angle error per vector, not only the largest.
- F4. Degenerate exponents (Hamiltonian flows have a pair of zero exponents on every orbit, Skokos2010 Eqs. (47), (48) p.28 via CQ F7): individual CLVs inside a degenerate block have no meaning, the algorithm returns an arbitrary basis of the block that depends on the starting matrix (Ginelli2013 p.14; KuptsovParlitz2012 p.10). The criteria on F3 therefore test the span of the zero block, the vectors outside it, and the structural identities (V14, V15), never the individual zero-block vectors.
- F5. Two exact identities need no variational reference: the telescoping identity of the diagonal (V10) ties the local exponents to the forward log-diagonal of R and to the angle of each CLV with its own Gram-Schmidt vector; and the covariance by construction gives, for symplectic maps, a pair identity (V14) valid for any starting matrix. Both are derived here and used as criteria 2 and 5.
- F6. No external CLV oracle exists. ChaosTools 3.5.4 in `verify/Manifest.toml` has no CLV routine (searched by the names covariant, clv, ginelli in its installed source, 2026-11-19); TaylorIntegration offers orbits and variational equations only. The oracles are closed forms (V16), identities (V10, V14, V15, V23), the module's own algorithm in BigFloat(256) at the realized orbit points, and one independent algorithm, the static null-space method of KuptsovParlitz2012 Sect. IV B (V19, criterion 13).
- F7. Memory is the design problem (Ginelli2013 p.14). For d = k = 4 and n_s = n_b = 1000 (Float64) the stored R and frames are about 288 kB per orbit (V17); an ensemble of 4096 orbits needs about 1.2 GB. The checkpointed variant costs one repeated forward pass (Ginelli2013 p.15) and, for h_b = 100, about 24.5 kB per orbit. The reducer protocol of the tangent stream carries a fixed-size state per orbit, which cannot hold O(n) data: the module has its own per-orbit loop (P10, Inconsistencies (d)).
- F8. Sign and normalization conventions (P7): CLVs are defined up to sign and length (Ginelli2013 p.11). The unit Euclidean norm and the sign rule c_ii > 0 make the covariance Lambda_i > 0 hold without sign flips (V9). Local exponents depend on the norm used for the normalization (KuptsovParlitz2012 p.11, text after Eq. (52)); their averages do not.

## Equations

Notation. Orbit points z_n at multiples of tau h from the start of the recorded window; M_n the tangent of the map over the interval n (V1); handoff index n runs over the forward transient and then over 0 .. N-1 for the recorded window; "c_i(n)" the i-th column of C_n; v_i(n) = G_n c_i(n) the i-th CLV at sample n (V5); u^T denotes transpose; J the canonical matrix (J^T = -J) and omega(u, w) = u^T J w.

V1. Tangent product over an interval, one-step map. M_{k,n} = product of the Jacobians J(x_i), i = n .. n+k-1, with the cocycle properties M_{k+l,n} = M_{k,n+l} M_{l,n}, M_{0,n} = I, M_{-k,n} = (M_{k,n-k})^-1. Ginelli2013 Eqs. (14), (15) p.7 (the paper's k is the number of map steps, tau here). For a flow the map is the integrator's one-step map and its tangent is its exact derivative (integrator-catalog Equations item 17): the vectors computed are the CLVs of that map. Derived, not cited: for a symplectic splitting method the one-step map is symplectic (integrator-catalog), so M_n^T J M_n = J up to rounding (V14).

V2. Forward QR (Gram-Schmidt dynamics). M_{k,n} G_n = G_{n+k} R_{k,n}, G_{n+k} orthonormal, R_{k,n} upper triangular, [R_{k,n}]_{ii} the local growth rate of the i-th Gram-Schmidt vector; the exponents are lambda_i = lim (1/T) sum ln [R]_{ii} (Ginelli2013 Eqs. (23), (24) p.9; same procedure Ginelli2007 p.1 right column to p.2). With the convention of the delivered discrete QR, R_ii > 0 (the Householder and Gram-Schmidt spectra, GS G6), so R is unique (Skokos2010 Theorem 4, cited in CQ C2). For k < d the first k columns only: G_n is d x k, R is k x k (Ginelli2013 p.14, "Scalability").

V3. The forward Gram-Schmidt vectors converge, for n large, to vectors that depend only on the phase-space point (eigenvectors of the backward Oseledets matrix): Ginelli2013 Eq. (25) p.10 (reported, Ershov and Potapov); Ginelli2007 p.2 (the "m-th Gram-Schmidt vectors" depend solely on x_m). They are norm-dependent except the first (Ginelli2013 p.10), and are not covariant (Ginelli2013 p.10, KuptsovParlitz2012 p.2).

V4. CLVs and covariance. For non-degenerate exponents the CLV v_n^(i) is the unit vector spanning the Oseledets subspace of lambda_i: covariant, M_{k,n} v_n^(i) is parallel to v_{n+k}^(i), and invariant under time reversal (Ginelli2013 pp.10 to 11, Sect. 4 intro; Ginelli2007 p.2). In matrix form M_{k,n} V_n = V_{n+k} D_{k,n}, D diagonal with entries Lambda_{k,n}^(i) = ||M_{k,n} v_n^(i)||; the finite-time exponents are ln Lambda (Ginelli2013 Eqs. (28), (29) p.11) and their time average is the exponent (Ginelli2013 p.12; Ginelli2007 p.2, "the ith exponent is the average of the growth rate of the ith vector"). The asymptotic statement is F(t1, t1 +- t) v = exp(+- lambda t) (KuptsovParlitz2012 Eq. (39) p.10, reported).

V5. Expansion in the Gram-Schmidt basis. v_n^(i) = sum_{j <= i} c_n^(j,i) g_n^(j) (Ginelli2013 Eq. (27) p.11), V_n = G_n C_n with C_n upper triangular (Ginelli2013 p.12, after Eq. (29)), columns normalized, sum_j (c_n^(j,i))^2 = 1 (Eq. (30) p.12). Consequences (derived): the first CLV is g_n^(1) (C has one nonzero entry in column 1, which Ginelli2013 p.11 states); V_n has unit columns because G_n is orthonormal; v^(i) lies in the span of the first i Gram-Schmidt vectors (Ginelli2007 p.2, "S_m^j"; Ginelli2013 p.11).

V6. Backward recursion. G_{n+k} C_{n+k} D = M_{k,n} G_n C_n = G_{n+k} R_{k,n} C_n, hence C_n = R_{k,n}^-1 C_{n+k} D_{k,n} (Ginelli2013 Eqs. (31), (32) pp.11 to 12; Ginelli2007 p.2: c_{m-1}^{ij} = sum_k [R_m]^-1_{ik} c_m^{kj}, C_m = R_m C_{m-1}). R^-1 is upper triangular, so a triangular C stays triangular and each column i uses only the top-left i x i block of R (derived; KuptsovParlitz2012 Eq. (66) p.16 writes the same implicit solve).

V7. Convergence of the backward iteration. Any non-singular upper-triangular C_{n+k}, iterated backward by V6 with column normalization, converges to the true CLV coefficients up to the column scale (Ginelli2013 Eqs. (33) to (40) pp.12 to 13); the convergence is exponential, controlled by the ratio of the growth factors of consecutive subspaces, i.e. by the difference of consecutive exponents (Ginelli2013 Sect. 5 pp.16 to 18, Fig. 2 p.18, and "Transient length" p.16). The same holds for the forward transient (V3). The CLVs are independent of where the backward iteration is started along the orbit once the transient is long enough (Ginelli2007 p.2). Derived rate on constant data (V22).

V8. Practical backward step. Back-substitution, no explicit inverse (Ginelli2013 p.15, "Computational time"; about m^3/3 operations); the normalization of each column by its norm; the implicit column-by-column form R(1:i,1:i) c(1:i, i) = c_{n+1}(1:i, i) (KuptsovParlitz2012 Eq. (66) p.16). The backward phase has two parts: a transient of n_b steps with nothing stored, then n_s steps whose normalized C_n is stored or delivered (Ginelli2013 p.13, (iii) and (iv)).

V9. Growth factors and local exponents, derived from V4 and V6. The unit normalization of V6 gives Lambda_i(n) = 1 / || R_n^-1 c_i(n+1) || = || R_n c_i(n) ||, and the forward form c_i(n+1) = R_n c_i(n) / Lambda_i(n) (G orthonormal: M_n v_i(n) = G_{n+1} R_n c_i(n)). Lambda_i(n) > 0 when the diagonal of C and of R is positive. Local (finite-time covariant) exponent per unit time: lambda_i(n) = ln Lambda_i(n) / (tau h) (per map step for a map, h = 1); the paper's FTLE is the same without the division (Ginelli2013 Eq. (29) p.11; KuptsovParlitz2012 Eq. (52) p.11 divides by the interval).

V10. Telescoping identity, derived. The diagonal of a product of upper-triangular matrices is the product of the diagonals, so V6 gives c_ii(n) = c_ii(n+1) Lambda_i(n) / R_ii(n), i.e. ln Lambda_i(n) = ln R_ii(n) + ln c_ii(n) - ln c_ii(n+1), with 0 < c_ii <= 1 and c_ii = g_n^(i) . v_n^(i) the cosine of the angle between the CLV and its own Gram-Schmidt vector. Summed over n = a .. b-1: sum ln Lambda_i = sum ln R_ii + ln c_ii(a) - ln c_ii(b). Hence the window mean of the local exponent differs from the forward estimate by at most (|ln c_ii(a)| + |ln c_ii(b)|) / ((b-a) tau h): the CLV exponents and the QR exponents agree up to a boundary term of the angle.

V11. Phase-space CLVs and covariance chain, derived. V_n = G_n C_n (V5); for m intervals the product of the growth factors equals the norm of the propagated vector: || M_{m,n} v_i(n) || = prod_{l=0}^{m-1} Lambda_i(n+l), with M_{m,n} from V1. Because C is upper triangular and G orthonormal, V_n^T V_n = C_n^T C_n.

V12. Angles and tangencies. (a) Pairwise: cos theta_ij(n) = |v_i . v_j| = |c_i(n)^T c_j(n)|, computable from C alone (derived from V11; Ginelli2013 p.14: "the relative angles can be computed directly with reference to the GS vectors basis"; KuptsovParlitz2012 p.14 same). (b) Between subspaces: for two CLV sets spanning subspaces of dimensions m1 <= m2 with columns U1 (d x m1), U2, factor U1 = Q1 R1, U2 = Q2 R2; the cosines of the m1 principal angles are the singular values of Q1^T Q2 (Ginelli2013 Eqs. (71), (72) p.24, with the expansion-coefficient columns in the Gram-Schmidt basis; for the first m1 CLVs, U1 is upper triangular and Q1 is the identity block, p.24); the tangency measure is the smallest principal angle between the unstable set (lambda > 0) and the stable set (lambda < 0) (Ginelli2013 p.24, mu_n = min theta; the zero block excluded, p.24, Sect. 7.2 "m1 = m2 = N - 2" for the FPU chain; hyperbolicity: the stable and unstable subspaces nowhere tangent, Eq. (70) p.23). Angles are not invariant under a nonlinear change of coordinates but zero and non-zero angles are (Ginelli2013 p.23); they are computed here in the Euclidean metric of the state coordinates (F8). Two CLVs of non-degenerate exponents never become exactly parallel along an orbit (Ginelli2013 p.23), trajectories only pass arbitrarily close.

V13. Held, not built: adjoint CLVs and the cosines D = diag(theta_i . v_i) of the angles between each CLV and its adjoint, a tangency indicator (KuptsovParlitz2012 Eqs. (53), (54) p.11), and the null-vector construction of the CLVs from the matrix P of scalar products of forward and backward Gram-Schmidt vectors (Eqs. (45) to (47) p.10, (55), (56) p.13; V19).

V14. Symplectic structure, derived (the exact relations need only M^T J M = J up to rounding and V4, V6). (a) For any starting C (converged or not), omega(v_i(n+1), v_j(n+1)) Lambda_i Lambda_j = omega(v_i(n), v_j(n)), so ln Lambda_i(n) + ln Lambda_j(n) = ln|omega_n(i,j)| - ln|omega_{n+1}(i,j)| whenever omega_n(i,j) is not zero. (b) For converged vectors and non-degenerate exponents omega(v_i, v_j) tends to zero unless lambda_i + lambda_j = 0: omega_{n+m} = omega_n exp(-(lambda_i + lambda_j) m Dt) would otherwise be unbounded in one time direction; so for d = 4 with exponents (a, b, -b, -a) only the pairs (1,4), (2,3) have omega not zero, and with the zero block degenerate (b = 0) the identity of (b) holds for the pairs (1, z), (4, z) with z any vector of the zero block, which is the form tested. The zero block itself carries omega(f, u) = grad H . u with f the field (derived from f = J grad H), so pair (2,3) of a Hamiltonian flow has omega not zero. (c) For k = d, ln|det M_n| = 0, hence sum_i ln Lambda_i(n) = ln|det C_n| - ln|det C_{n+1}| (derived from V6: the determinant of the triangular C is the product of its diagonal; the sum of the local exponents telescopes and has mean zero up to the boundary term).

V15. Flow direction and energy covector, derived (ofli O4 (i) Y(t) f(z_0) = f(z_t), (ii) grad H(z_t)^T Y(t) = grad H(z_0)^T, cited through CQ C17, C18). For the exact flow, f(z)/||f(z)|| belongs to the zero-exponent covariant subspace, and for every CLV with lambda_i not zero grad H(z) . v_i = 0: with s(t) = grad H(z_t) . v_i(t), (ii) gives s(0) = s(t) ||Y(t) v_i(0)||, so s(0) is not zero would make s unbounded in one time direction while |s| <= ||grad H|| is bounded on a bounded orbit. For the numerical map both hold up to the defect of the map against the flow (ofli O10, order h^p) and the transient errors (V7). These are the closed-form checks that need no variational reference for the flows.

V16. Constant-Jacobian fixtures, derived (V4: for constant M the CLVs are its eigenvectors, M v = mu v, lambda = ln|mu| / Dt, the exponents ordered decreasing; for a constant Jacobian J of a flow likewise with lambda the real parts of the eigenvalues, KuptsovParlitz2012 Sect. V A p.17). (a) KuptsovParlitz2012 Eq. (69) p.17, J = [[1,-2,0],[0,-1,0],[0,2,-3]] with exponents (1, -1, -3) and CLV matrix Gamma with columns (1,0,0), (1,1,1)/sqrt 3, (0,0,1) (Eq. (70) p.17, reported; checked by hand: J gamma_1 = gamma_1, J gamma_2 = -gamma_2, J gamma_3 = -3 gamma_3). The map fixture is M = exp(J) (exponents (1,-1,-3) per step, same CLVs); it is a d = 3 linear map, non-symplectic, a test double. (b) Non-normal symplectic map, d = 4 in the ordering (q1, q2, p1, p2): A = S diag(a1, a2, 1/a1, 1/a2) S^-1 with S = [[I, B],[0, I]], B symmetric (then S^T J S = J), 1 < a2 < a1; the exponents (ln a1, ln a2, -ln a2, -ln a1); the CLVs are v_1 = S e_1, v_2 = S e_2, v_3 = S e_4, v_4 = S e_3, normalized. The unstable plane is the q-plane; the stable plane is spanned by the columns of [B; I], and the cosines of the principal angles between them are the singular values of B (I + B^2)^(-1/2), i.e. beta / sqrt(1 + beta^2) for the eigenvalues beta of B; for B = [[1,2],[2,1]] (beta = 3, -1) the angles are arctan(1/3) = 0.3217505544 and arctan(1) = pi/4 (derived, checked by hand). (c) Arnold cat map A = [[2,1],[1,1]] (catalog Y5; symmetric, so the CLVs are the orthogonal eigenvectors (1, mu - 2) and (1, 1/mu - 2), mu = (3 + sqrt 5)/2, exponents +-0.9624236501, angle pi/2; CQ-era Householder spec E13 has the same eigenvector). (d) Separable linear flow, d = 4, H = |p|^2/2 - q^T K q/2 with K = Rot diag(k1, k2) Rot^T (Rot a rotation by 0.6): A = [[0, I],[K, 0]], eigenvectors (Rot e_i; s sqrt(k_i) Rot e_i) / sqrt(1 + k_i) with eigenvalue s sqrt(k_i), s = +-1; for (k1, k2) = (0.64, 0.16) the exponents (0.8, 0.4, -0.4, -0.8) and v_1 = (Rot e_1; 0.8 Rot e_1), v_2 = (Rot e_2; 0.4 Rot e_2), v_3 = (Rot e_2; -0.4 Rot e_2), v_4 = (Rot e_1; -0.8 Rot e_1), each divided by sqrt(1 + k_i). The numerical one-step map of a splitting method is a constant symplectic matrix exp(h A_mod), A_mod = A + O(h^p) (modified generator, derived), so its CLVs differ from those of (d) by O(h^p) (estimate, tested in criterion 8). (e) Planted tangency, d = 2: M = Gamma diag(2, 1/2) Gamma^-1 with Gamma = [[1,1],[0,delta]], that is [[2, -1.5/delta],[0, 0.5]] (det 1, area preserving); CLVs (1,0) and (1,delta)/sqrt(1+delta^2), exponents +-ln 2, angle arctan(delta). The standard statement that CLVs are eigenvectors is not a cited oracle for the maps of the catalog; it is used on these linear fixtures only.

V17. Memory and checkpointing. Reported (Ginelli2013 Eqs. (41), (42) p.14, N the system dimension, m the number of CLVs, t sampling points, t0 backward-transient points): M_tot = t m (N + (m+1)/2) + t0 m (m+1)/2 floating-point numbers, and M'_tot = (t + t0) m (m+1)/2 when only angles are wanted (frames not stored). This spec's count, derived (n_s + 1 frames, N upper triangles): bytes_store = s_T [ N k(k+1)/2 + (n_s + 1) d k ] + bytes_work, bytes_work = s_T (d + 2 d k + k(k+1)/2 + 2 k) + 16 (state, current frame, carried C, scratch, counters), s_T = sizeof of the element type; angles-only drops the frame term (+ one frame still needed for the forward step). Example (Float64, d = k = 4, n_s = n_b = 1000): 8 (2000 x 10 + 1001 x 16) + 448 = 288576 bytes. Checkpointed variant (Ginelli2013 pp.14 to 15): store (z, G) at the start of each of n_c = ceil(N / h_b) blocks of h_b handoffs during a first forward pass that does not run the last block; then, block by block from the last, rerun the block forward storing R and G, run the backward step, and start the next block backward from the C carried out of the previous one; bytes_ckpt = s_T [ n_c (d + d k) + h_b (k(k+1)/2 + d k) + k(k+1)/2 ] + bytes_work + the delivered output of one block. Example (h_b = 100, N = 2000): 8 (20 x 20 + 100 x 26 + 10) + 448 = 24528 bytes. Forward handoffs: n_f + N (store-all); n_f + (N - h_last) + N (checkpointed, h_last the size of the last block), i.e. about twice the forward work (reported: "doubling the algorithm computational time", Ginelli2013 p.15). Derived: with the same orbit points bitwise (L1 S2) and the same seeded starting C, the checkpointed run reproduces the store-all values bit for bit, because every R is recomputed from the same inputs by the same arithmetic and the carried C is the one the continuous run would have.

V18. Cost, reported (Ginelli2013 Eq. (43) p.15): T_tot ~ t m^2 (m/3 + N/2 + 2N) for easy dynamics, forward QR ~ 2 m^2 N (stabilized Gram-Schmidt), back-substitution ~ m^3/3, expansion in phase-space coordinates ~ m^2 N / 2; the backward evolution is about 2.4 times faster than the forward one for m = N, or 6 times without the phase-space expansion (the arithmetic checks: 2/(1/3 + 1/2) = 2.4, 2/(1/3) = 6). The per-step cost of the tangent dynamics is system dependent (Ginelli2013 p.15). Measured ratios are recorded (R1), not scored.

V19. Static alternative for the cross-check (reported). The j-th CLV expansion coefficients on the forward Gram-Schmidt basis are the null vector of P(1:j-1, 1:j), P = (G^-)^T G^+ the matrix of scalar products of the backward and forward Gram-Schmidt vectors at the same point: P(1:j-1, 1:j) a(1:j, j) = 0 (KuptsovParlitz2012 Eq. (55) p.13; Ginelli2013 Eqs. (66) to (69) p.22), with unit columns; it needs the forward frame and the backward frame (the second from the transposed tangent propagation run backward in time, KuptsovParlitz2012 Sect. II, not read in full here); the cost is a second orthonormalization pass and a singular value decomposition per column (about 6 m^3 against m^3/3, Ginelli2013 p.22). Wolfe and Samelson's system (Ginelli2013 Eqs. (62) to (65) p.21; KuptsovParlitz2012 Eqs. (63), (64) p.15) is the same solution set (KuptsovParlitz2012 p.15); not used.

V20. Degenerate exponents (reported): for a covariant subspace of dimension g > 1 the vectors inside it have no individual meaning and the algorithm returns some basis of it that depends on the starting C (Ginelli2013 p.14; KuptsovParlitz2012 p.10: ambiguity if and only if the exponents are degenerate). The subspace is well defined: the subspace-valued checks of this spec use it (F4).

V21. Non-invertible dynamics (reported, Ginelli2013 Sect. 4.3 p.16): past images are taken as those visited by the forward orbit; the CLVs are then path dependent. Not in scope: the module raises a health error when the tangent has no inverse (a zero pivot of the forward QR, GS Algorithm item 5, V6 needs R^-1).

V22. Backward convergence on constant data, derived (no source states it for constant M). With M constant, non-degenerate and exponents lambda_1 > ... > lambda_k, the forward frame converges to the Q factor of the eigenvector matrix Gamma (positive-diagonal QR), the asymptotic C is its R factor with unit columns, and R = C L C^-1 in the limit. The error of column j of the backward iterate after m backward steps from a generic start is of order exp(-(lambda_{j-1} - lambda_j) Dt m) (the nearest gap above; column 1 has no error), and the error of the forward frame after n_f steps is of order exp(-g_min Dt n_f), g_min the smallest gap (estimates, checked by criterion 3(a); consistent with Ginelli2013 p.16 "the convergence to the i-th CLV depends on lambda_i - lambda_{i-1}").

V23. Time reversal, derived and reported. The CLVs are invariant under time reversal (Ginelli2007 p.2, "we have verified"; Ginelli2013 p.10). For an invertible map with inverse map T^-1 the tangent is M^-1, the exponents change sign and reverse order (Ginelli2013 Eq. (26) p.10), and the CLV of index i of T at z is the CLV of index d + 1 - i of T^-1 at z (derived from V4: M v_i = Lambda v_i' gives M^-1 v_i' = Lambda^-1 v_i; same direction, reversed index).

## Algorithm

Per orbit (ensemble member, orbit id j, run seed s), a map or a flow with its one-step map, handoff interval tau (default the run's renorm interval), k (default d), element type, orthonormalizer of the forward phase (P8), transients n_f, n_b and window n_s given by the caller (P3):
1. Initialisation. A seeded orthonormal d x k frame G_0 (the frame and seed conventions of the tangent stream, orthonormal to 8 k eps), compensated sums for ln R_ii (for the forward exponents, V2), the start z_0 from the caller, health check of z_0 and the frame. A second seeded stream, labelled differently and keyed by the orbit id, yields the starting backward matrix C_N: upper triangular, positive diagonal, unit columns, column j independent of k (P13).
2. Forward transient: n_f handoffs of the delivered discrete QR (V2) along the tangent dynamics of the orbit, nothing stored; the forward exponents are accumulated as the delivered spectrum does (the same orbit and the same G_n bitwise as the delivered spectrum with the same orthonormalizer, L1 S2).
3. Forward recording: for n = 0 .. N-1 apply the interval map to G_n, factor into G_{n+1} R_n (positive diagonal), store R_n for all N intervals and G_n for n = 0 .. n_s (or checkpoint, V17). A factorization breakdown (zero pivot, non-finite, passes cap, window conditioning rule of the orthonormalizer) is a numerical health error with orbit id and handoff, nothing stored (the delivered conventions, GS Algorithm item 5).
4. Backward transient: from C_N, for n = N-1 down to n_s + 1 apply V6 and V8 (back-substitution with R_n, column normalization with an overflow-safe norm); nothing stored. A column with zero or non-finite norm after the solve is a health error of kind "backward breakdown" with orbit id and handoff (P14).
5. Backward recording: for n = n_s down to 0 apply the same step and deliver C_n, the growth factors Lambda_i(n) from the normalization (V9, one division per column), and the quantities of the requested output set (P5): V_n = G_n C_n; local exponents lambda_i(n); the pairwise cosines (V12a); the smallest principal angle between the sets of positive and negative exponents (V12b, the split chosen by the caller from the forward spectrum, P11). Output order is decreasing n (block by block when checkpointed); the caller may reverse.
6. At the end: the forward exponents of the recorded window with their compensation terms, the final C_0, the run record (below).
7. The orbit never depends on the estimator: the orbit points are bitwise equal to those of the integrator alone and of the delivered estimators on the same system, step and start (L1 S2). The result of one orbit depends only on (system, parameters, start, seed, j, tau, h, orthonormalizer, k, n_f, n_s, n_b, element type, storage mode), not on threads, chunk, or position in the ensemble.
Parameters recorded per run: system or flow identifier, h, the integrator table and its order (flows), tau, k, n_f, n_s, n_b, the orthonormalizer, the storage mode and block length h_b, element type, the seeds of the frame and of C, backend and the fallback reason, the output set.

## Invariants

- I1. Structure: G_n has orthonormal columns to 8 k eps; R_n upper triangular with positive diagonal; C_n upper triangular with unit columns and positive diagonal; V_n has unit columns; v_1(n) = g_1(n) exactly (column 1 of C is e_1).
- I2. Covariance by construction (V6, V9): R_n c_i(n) = Lambda_i(n) c_i(n+1) up to rounding, Lambda_i(n) > 0, for every starting C, converged or not.
- I3. Telescoping (V10) and, for k = d and symplectic M, the pair and sum identities (V14 (a), (c)), valid for every starting C.
- I4. Convergence: after the transients, the CLVs do not depend on the starting frame, on the starting C, on tau (at common sample times), or on the orthonormalizer, up to exp(-gap x transient) plus rounding (V7).
- I5. Column independence for k < d: the first k CLVs of a k-column run equal those of a larger run (V6: column i uses the top-left i x i block of R; G columns independent of k).
- I6. Orbit independence and execution invariance: bitwise equal results for 1 against N threads, chunk sizes and ensemble position (CPU); across storage modes (store-all against checkpointed) bitwise (V17); the per-handoff work allocates nothing after the storage is allocated.
- I7. Index order is column order, never re-sorted (GS G6); for a degenerate block the individual vectors are not invariants, the block span is (V20).
- I8. Time reversal (V23) and symplectic pairing (V14 (b)) at the converged vectors, to the transient error.

## Compute

Policy: `_config/architecture.md`, "Compute" (D17, D25, D29, D54). Per orbit the work is data parallel (independent orbits). Forward phase: the same per-orbit tangent step and small QR as the delivered discrete QR (kernel-path orthonormalizers run on the GPU in Float64 as reducers); but every handoff stores R (k(k+1)/2 numbers) and, for output, the frame (d k), so the per-orbit storage grows with N (V17: about 288 kB for d = k = 4, n_s = n_b = 1000 in Float64; 4096 orbits about 1.2 GB), against a fixed-size state for a reducer (F7). Backward phase: a sequential recurrence over N handoffs per orbit with an upper-triangular solve of size k and a column normalization; independent over orbits, no library factorization; a device kernel is possible in principle for fixed small k, but not built: it needs R and the frames of all orbits resident on the device. Honest statement for this release: the module is CPU only (threads over orbits; BLAS threads set to 1 under Julia threads, policy). A request for the GPU or `auto` runs on the CPU and records the fallback reason of the policy list for a CPU-only reducer ("a reducer in the chain that is CPU-only"); no device criterion. Double64 and BigFloat are CPU only anyway (risk K20; D24). The GPU forward phase with host-side backward phase is a held option (P9), not measured. Chunk size from free host memory (never a fixed number), counting bytes_store or bytes_ckpt (V17) per orbit; the block length h_b chosen so that a chunk fits (P4). Random streams are keyed by orbit id, not by thread (policy, Reproducibility). The CPU run is the verified reference; there is no CPU-versus-GPU tolerance to state (criterion 14 records the fallback).

## Assumptions

- A1. The CLVs of the numerical map (V1) converge to those of the flow as h -> 0 at the order p of the integrator for the fixtures with constant Jacobian (derived through the modified generator, V16 (d); tested in criterion 4) and, for a chaotic nonlinear flow, over the horizons used (ofli O10 and the henon-heiles pointwise limit are not needed because the criteria compare a numerical map with its own identities, not with the flow, except criterion 7 whose tolerance carries h^p).
- A2. The backward iteration of V8 is stable in floating point for the first columns and loses accuracy only toward small exponents (reported, KuptsovParlitz2012 p.16; Ginelli2013 p.12, "stable dynamical rule"); a rounding bound for the CLVs is not given by any source on disk: B_v of criterion 11 is a proposal (Inconsistencies (f)).
- A3. The orbit stays on a bounded region where the tangent is bounded (F3: E < 1/6; the standard map K = 5 is bounded in the lifted momentum only up to the sampled horizon); an orbit that escapes is outside the scope of the criteria.

## Inconsistencies

(a) Row L12 lists WolfeSamelson2007 beside Ginelli2007 and KuptsovParlitz2012 as the sources of "Ginelli forward QR + backward upper-triangular iteration". Wolfe and Samelson's algorithm is a static method (Ginelli2013 Sect. 6.1 p.21; KuptsovParlitz2012 Sect. IV C p.14), not the backward iteration; the bibliography records its start page only and the text is not on disk. The Ginelli method rests on Ginelli2007, Ginelli2013 Sect. 4 and KuptsovParlitz2012 Sect. IV D.
(b) Row L12b describes "Oseledets vectors via wedge-product SVD / dichotomy projectors" with source Froyland2013 and "one variant as cross-check of L12". The static algorithms in the sources on disk are those of Wolfe and Samelson and of Kuptsov and Parlitz (null-space or LU of the matrix P), neither a wedge-product SVD nor a dichotomy projector. Froyland2013 is not on disk, so L12b is held; the cross-check in criterion 13 uses the KuptsovParlitz2012 variant and is offered as the "one variant" only if the owner accepts that reading (P12).
(c) Ginelli2007 has no numbered equation for the algorithm: the bibliography entry and the row cite it as the algorithm source, but it can be cited by page and column only. The algorithm is first written with numbered equations in Ginelli2013 (Eqs. (23), (27) to (32) pp.9 to 12).
(d) `_config/architecture.md`, "Module map" row Lyapunov, says "estimators are reducers" and, in the same row, "Ginelli CLVs with stored R factors and checkpoints". A reducer of the tangent stream keeps an isbits state of fixed size per orbit (`src/Tangent/CONTEXT.md`: reducer protocol, per-orbit `UInt32` workspace), which cannot hold O(N) stored R factors and frames. The module therefore runs its own per-orbit loop (as continuous-qr and two-trajectory do) and reuses the delivered orthonormalizers and the orbit (P10).
(e) Notation: Ginelli2013 uses k for the number of map steps between orthonormalizations and N for the dimension, m for the number of CLVs, t and t0 for sampling points and backward-transient points; this repository uses k for the number of columns, d for the dimension, tau for the interval and h for the integrator step. Eq. (41) (Ginelli2013 p.14) counts t frames, this spec n_s + 1 (both end frames stored). Local exponents: Ginelli2013 Eq. (29) is ln Lambda per interval, KuptsovParlitz2012 Eq. (52) divides by the interval length; this spec divides (V9, P5), as continuous-qr P14.
(f) No source on disk bounds the rounding error of the computed CLVs (Ginelli2013 Sect. 5 analyses the convergence of the dynamics, not floating point; KuptsovParlitz2012 p.16 reports the ill-conditioning qualitatively). The earlier modules had a derived rounding bound (L1 S3, Amendment D35); here only the proposal B_v exists.
(g) The catalog row L8 note and the continuous-qr spec (P12) anticipated that clv-ginelli might need the triangular factor R of the continuous method; the Ginelli method uses the discrete QR (and, for a flow, the discrete QR of the integrator's one-step map), so that open point is moot and no amendment of continuous-qr is needed.
(h) The delivered discrete QR and the tangent stream count map steps; the CLV local exponents of a flow need per unit time. As in CQ (f), the conversion is the division by tau h, applied by the module, not by the caller.

## Open points

- P1. Which CLVs. Recommended: the first k (1 <= k <= d), k = d primary, as Ginelli2013 p.14; the cost of the forward phase and the memory fall as k^2. Alternative: also the last m CLVs by the time-reversed dynamics (invertible maps only, needs the inverse tangent): held, not built.
- P2. Maps and flows. Recommended: maps and flows together; a flow is its integrator's one-step map and the CLVs are those of that map (V1, A1, F4), with the order-h^p difference to the flow reported (criterion 4, 7). Alternative: maps only first; rejected, the flows (F3) are the physically interesting use and cost nothing extra here.
- P3. Transient lengths. Recommended: n_f and n_b are required parameters with no hidden default, recorded in the run; guidance n* = ceil(25 / (g_min Dt)) from the forward spectrum of the same run, and the diagnostic of criterion 3 (b) (two starting matrices at the first recorded sample) available on request; keys `clv_forward_transient`, `clv_backward_transient` in `numerics-defaults.toml` proposed as study defaults only (values left to the owner at stage 02, for example 1000 and 1000 handoffs of one time unit). Alternative: a convergence-driven stop (continue until two seeded C agree); rejected for now, it doubles the backward work and breaks the fixed-size layout.
- P4. Storage. Recommended: store-all when bytes_store fits the chunk budget, otherwise the checkpointed variant with h_b chosen from free memory (V17, policy "chunk from free memory"); both modes bitwise equal (criterion 8). Disk spill (Ginelli2013 p.14: slow, limited) held; the output sink streams one block at a time.
- P5. Outputs. Recommended: always the coefficients C_n, the local exponents lambda_i(n) and the pairwise cosines (V12 a); the phase-space CLVs V_n = G_n C_n and the smallest principal angle between the unstable and stable sets on request; sampling every s_out handoffs (default 1). Angles-only runs need no stored frame (Ginelli2013 Eq. (42) p.14). Alternative: always V_n; rejected, it is the dominant memory term.
- P6. Interval. Recommended: one interval tau for transients and sampling (default the run's renorm interval, 1). Alternative: two intervals as Ginelli2013 pp.13 to 14 suggest (a coarser one for the transients): held.
- P7. Normalization, norm and sign. Recommended: Euclidean unit columns of C (hence of V), c_ii > 0 (hence Lambda_i > 0 and no sign flips along the orbit), local exponents per unit time (maps per step). Alternative: sign fixed by the first nonzero component of V; rejected, it can flip along an orbit and lose covariance of the sign.
- P8. Forward orthonormalizer. Recommended: Householder (the L5 reference) by default, the Gram-Schmidt variants selectable (repeated Gram-Schmidt with its conditioning rule), the library path (LAPACK) allowed and CPU only; the CLVs depend on the choice only through rounding (I4, criteria 2, 11). Alternative: Gram-Schmidt only; rejected, its conditioning rule can raise on strongly contracting tangents where Householder continues.
- P9. Compute. Recommended: CPU only in this release (Compute); the run records the fallback reason when the GPU is requested; the GPU forward phase with host backward phase or a device kernel for fixed small k is a held option to be decided only after a measured per-method record shows a win (D54). Alternative: build the hybrid now; rejected, unmeasured and the storage is per orbit.
- P10. Module home and layout. Recommended: the Lyapunov module, own per-orbit loop on the orbit and tangent of the stream (not a reducer, Inconsistencies (d)), importing the layers it already imports; the interface is not specified here (stage 03). Alternative: extend the reducer protocol with a host-side store; rejected, it changes the stream contract for all reducers.
- P11. Tangency outputs and held items. Recommended: pairwise cosines and the smallest principal angle between the sets {lambda > lambda_0} and {lambda < -lambda_0}, lambda_0 taken by the caller from the forward spectrum (zero block excluded as in Ginelli2013 Sect. 7.2 p.24). Adjoint CLVs and their angles (KuptsovParlitz2012 p.11) held: a second pass and the backward frame are needed.
- P12. Cross-check variant and L12b. Recommended: the KuptsovParlitz2012 static null-space method in the verification environment (V19, criterion 13) as the independent algorithm; L12b stays held (Froyland2013 not on disk) and the row is annotated at stage 05. Alternative: read Froyland2013 first and implement the wedge-product variant as the cross-check; offered if the owner supplies the paper.
- P13. Starting data and seeds. Recommended: the forward frame as the tangent stream (seeded, column j independent of k); the starting C from a separate stream label keyed by the orbit id, column by column (upper triangular, positive diagonal, unit columns), recorded; the same seed reproduces results bitwise, a different seed changes them only through the unconverged transient (criterion 3 (b)).
- P14. Underflow and false tangency. Recommended: no noise injection; a column with zero or non-finite norm after the back-substitution is a health error of kind "backward breakdown" with orbit id and handoff (criterion 14 (c)). KuptsovParlitz2012 p.16 cures false tangencies by adding a small amount of noise to the diagonal; noise changes the result and its dependence on the seed, so it is held. Alternative: the noise, keyed by orbit id; offered if the owner needs very long runs on strongly contracting systems.
- P15. Systems of the criteria. Recommended: the linear fixtures (exact), the standard map K = 5, coupled maps with d = 4, Henon-Heiles F3 orbit C (chaotic), and the regular orbit R reported only; the systems of the papers (tent-map chain, Henon chain, FPU) are not built. Alternative: add the FPU chain F5 (optional in the catalog) for a published hyperbolicity comparison; offered after M5.
- P16. Tolerances and catalog wording. All numbers marked proposal (B_fix, B_cov, c_8, B_flow, B_v, c_v, the 1e-8, 1e-9, 1e-10, 1e-12 gates, n* = 25 / gamma, the slope tolerance, the 95 percent rule) are to be calibrated by a drafting run at stage 02; the closed forms (criteria 1, 6 (a), 6 (c), the algebra of 2, 5), the bitwise criteria (8, 9, 10) and the error criteria (14) are not negotiable. Wording of row L12 at stage 05: sources "Ginelli2007, Ginelli2013, KuptsovParlitz2012; WolfeSamelson2007 not on disk", the CPU-only status, the memory formula and the checkpoint reference; row L12b unchanged (held).
