# Spec: gali

Library layer: Indicators (`_config/architecture.md`, "Module map" row Indicators). Milestone M5 (`_config/build-order.md`, row "M5 P3": gali, decision D70). Status: approved at the stage 01 checkpoint, 2026-11-05 (D106), with open points P1 to P9 settled as proposed (rewrite of the M4 draft of 2026-10-22, which D70 deferred and which this file replaces). The 4D system it waited for exists: coupled-maps (Y3 and Y4, decisions D103 to D105, verified and integrated). Shared definitions are cited, not repeated: the sali spec (`sali-spec.md`) as "sali A4", "sali criterion 16", "sali Amendments D67, D68"; the Benettin spec as "L1 S3" (same-orbit bound, Psi_n, Amendment D35); the coupled-maps spec as "cm item 10", "cm criterion 13"; the fli-ofli spec for the one-replacer rule (D71). Spec-local equation labels are B1 to B13, findings F1 to F9, open points P1 to P9. Symbols not defined here (S_i(n), L(n), W_0, B_L, Psi_n^(i), D_orb, "identical orbit", "resolved", "regular class") are those of sali. Element types: Float64, Double64 (effective epsilon 2^-100), BigFloat(256) for oracle runs; Float32 is out (D24). Page numbers are pages of the arXiv PDFs as fetched (`data/literature/gali/`, `data/literature/sali/`); for SkokosManos2016 they are the page numbers of the chapter, which the arXiv PDF repeats.

## Scope

- Catalog row I3 (`_config/catalogs/indicators.md`, section A): GALI_k, the norm of the wedge product of k unit deviation vectors, for every order 2 <= k <= d on the 4D map Y3 (d = 4) and on the chain Y4 (d = 2N, N up to 100). The 2D maps keep the D70 outcome (F1): there GALI_2 is the log form of the sali reducer and no new indicator exists.
- Catalog row I4 (LGALI, "GALI for local dynamics"): not built. No source on disk or in the bibliography defines an indicator called LGALI (F6). What the sources define under "local dynamics" is the behaviour of GALI_k near periodic orbits (SkokosManos2016 Sect. 3.4, Eqs. (36) to (38), p.27 to 31); those are laws of I3, and criterion 12 tests them on the fixed points of Y3 and Y4 (periodic orbits of period 1) with exact exponents. Recommendation: row I4 stays `planned` and its text is corrected at stage 05, or the owner closes it as merged into I3 (P7).
- Prerequisites (catalog statuses read 2026-11-05): sali (I2), fli-ofli (I5), megno (I8) and the Lyapunov rows `verified`; tangent-core, precision; coupled-maps (Y3, Y4) `verified` by stage 05, with its criterion 13 (exact tangent products), the fixed-size chain state (D105) and the fixed orbits C and D (cm item 3, item 10). The tangent stream, its seeded frame (the first k columns of one Haar frame, so the first two columns do not depend on k), health errors, scaled norms, compensated sums and the D66 and D71 chain rules are used as they are.
- The module is a reducer that never changes the orbit. It owns K deviation columns of the stream (3 <= K <= d is the new case; K = 2 is allowed and equals SALI information), normalises each column at every handoff, and reports GALI_k for every order 2 <= k <= K, nested on the leading columns (B12), plus the floor-free log form where it exists (K = d, B5).
- Out of scope: classification thresholds and the stopping rule (a study concern, thresholds pre-registered per study; SkokosManos2016 p.17 advises thresholds a few orders below the smallest regular value, which is a study rule), early termination of an orbit, LGALI, wedge products on flows, regular motion on low-dimensional tori as a detection strategy (SkokosManos2016 Sect. 3.3.1; the law B7 is tested, the strategy is not), time-dependent re-initialisation (Sect. 4.3).
- Keys: no `numerics-defaults.toml` key is proposed. tau stays `renorm_interval_tau`, default 1.

### Contract question for the owner (P1): the design the M4 draft left open

Facts that decide it. (i) Every existing column reducer (SALI, FLI, MEGNO, RLI, stretching numbers) accepts k = 2 only, so on a stream with K > 2 columns the new reducer is the only column reducer (F2). (ii) For k < d the volume of the propagated frame is not invariant, so a column-normalised chain has no floor-free log form; for k = d it has (B5). (iii) A QR-factor route gives a floor-free log form for every k (F3) but orthonormalises the columns, so it is a mixing reducer that D66 keeps in a separate pass. (iv) The evaluation for all orders 2..K comes from one factorisation of the unit matrix (B2 iv, B12).

- G1. Recommended. One column-normalising reducer over K columns, placed last in its chain as the one replacer (D71). At every handoff it does what sali does per column (scaled norm, compensated log sum S_i, replacement by the unit column) and then evaluates GALI_k for k = 2..K from the unit columns by the nested route. Direct form for every k, with the floor of B9 stated and carried in the output. The log form of B5 is retained when K = d. For K = d = 2 the log form equals the sali log form bitwise (B5, criterion 8c). SALI on a 4D map needs no second reducer: it follows from GALI_2 by B4 (F1). Cost: floor B9 for k < d.
- G2. Alternative. G1 plus a second reducer on the QR-factor route (floor-free ln GALI_k for every k, K x K state, mixing class), run in a separate pass on the bitwise identical orbit. Evidence that it works: the drafting checks (e) agree with a 120-digit reference to 8 digits at depth 1e-25 where the direct form is already unresolved. Cost: a second pass, a thin QR written inside the layer (Indicators may not import the QR family, a peer layer), about 12 more criteria. Not needed for the p3 thresholds (SkokosManos2016 p.17 uses 1e-8 to 1e-16). Revisit if a study needs depth below B9.
- G3. Alternative. Generalise the verified sali reducer to K columns and report SALI, GALI_k and L from one owner of the lengths. Cleanest, but amends the verified sali spec and code and re-opens its criteria 1 to 18. Not recommended.
- G4. Rejected. One stream per order k (K = k). It repeats the orbit and the tangent work k - 1 times; B12 makes it unnecessary.
- Under G1 the other column reducers on 4D maps run as separate k = 2 passes over the bitwise identical orbit (L1 S2), or are widened to read the first two columns of a wider frame (amends four verified specs). The choice belongs to the p3 study spec, where the cost comparison is made (P4).

### Findings for the owner

- F1. Overlap with SALI. For d = k = 2, GALI_2 is exp(L) of the sali reducer (B5 equals sali A7) and a monotone function of SALI (B4); on 2D maps the two are one indicator (D70, still true). For d >= 3 it is new information only for k >= 3, but GALI_2 of a K-column run gives the SALI of the first two columns by B4 at no cost, so a 4D study needs no second reducer for SALI. Thresholds map by B4: tau_g = tau_S sqrt(4 - tau_S^2)/2, and with the same number below 0.1 they differ by at most 0.13 percent.
- F2. Chain and one pass. Architecture says all indicators run in ONE pass, D66 and D71 give the chain rule (one replacer, last), but every existing column reducer is fixed at k = 2 (sali, fli, megno, rli and stretching numbers each throw for another k). The chain rule is therefore moot for K > 2; the one-pass comparison on 4D maps needs either separate passes per k or widened reducers (P4). The pass-through readers (weighted Birkhoff, frequency reducers, any k >= 1) can precede the new reducer.
- F3. Log form and the QR-factor route. (i) B5 holds for k = d only. (ii) The QR-factor route (sali P1(c)): Y_n W_0 = Q_n P_n with P_n the upper triangular product of the R factors; the true unit columns are Q_n P_n D^-1, D the column norms of P_n, so GALI_k = prod_{i<=k} P_ii / prod_{i<=k} norm(P_:,i), a sum of logs of a K x K matrix, floor-free for every k (derived). Drafting check (e): at n = 300 and 600 it matches a 120-digit evaluation of the same definition to 8 digits at ln GALI_4 = -25.2 and -44.0, where the direct Float64 form is at 1e-14 and has lost accuracy. (iii) Skokos2008 p.4 (after Eq. (11)) says the singular values permit the "accurate determination of large negative values of log(GALI_k)"; that holds for the stored unit columns, not for the true propagated ones: this spec reads it as "does not underflow" (P5). (iv) The Gram-determinant evaluation squares the quantity and has a floor near sqrt(eps); it is the negative control, not an option.
- F4. Sourcing upgrade. For maps the laws are now printed: SkokosManos2016 p.19 states that the indices follow laws (22) and (23) for symplectic maps with n for t, and shows a chaotic and a regular orbit of a 4D map (Fig. 8 p.20, caption values lambda_1 = 0.07, lambda_2 = 0.008 for K = 0.5, beta = 0.05) and of a 6D map (Fig. 9 p.21); Skokos2007 p.38 states it, and prints GALI_2 about 1/t^2 for regular orbits of 2D maps (also SkokosManos2016 Eq. (33) p.20). The sali and coupled-maps specs cite that statement as p.38 and "Sec. 6 p.37"; the page marker puts it at the top of p.38 (conflict of page numbers, no effect on content).
- F5. The floor B9 is a guarantee, not a prediction. Drafting check (a), Float64, Y3 orbit D, direct form against the 256-bit-checked log form: n = 400: 2.20595523e-14 vs 2.20595518e-14; n = 800: 5.52109e-22 vs 5.52172e-22 (relative 1.1e-4); n = 1500: 2.59e-27 vs 2.46e-27; n = 3000: 4.4e-34 vs 2.8e-37. The absolute error stays far below B9 = 1.8e-15 because the first-order error of GALI_k is 2 eps times the sum of the (k-1)-volumes of the other columns (B9 sharper form), which are small for a nearly dependent set. Consequence for criteria: gate the absolute bound (B9, B10); report the realized depth. The ChaosTools value for sali was blind below 1e-15 (sali stage 04), so that behaviour is a property of its arithmetic, not a rule.
- F6. LGALI. Search done: `data/literature/gali/*.txt`, `data/literature/sali/*.txt`, all other texts on disk contain no "LGALI"; the literature review (`chaos-indicators-candidates.md`, notes, plan sub-question I-SQ4) found no source defining it; risk K6 records it. `Skokos2008?` in row I4 is the GALI-in-FPU-lattices paper that supplies the SVD evaluation (SkokosManos2016 ref. [85]); it contains no LGALI. The only in-bib text on "the local dynamics of periodic orbits" is ManosSkokosAntonopoulos2012 (SkokosManos2016 ref. [67]); its full text is not on disk and is not used. Skokos2007's title ("Geometrical properties of local dynamics ...") is the probable origin of the phrase "GALI for local dynamics".
- F7. Oracle conflict. The brief and cm P12 say ChaosTools `gali` is reachable for k = 2 only. The installed source (ChaosTools 3.5.4) takes the order k as an argument, builds a tangent system with k vectors and multiplies the singular values after normalising every step, so it should also serve for k = 3, 4 on a hand-written Y3. Not run; stage 04 confirms first (criterion 19(b)). It is the same algorithm class as the module, so it is a code cross-check, not a second derivation.
- F8. Regular-orbit transients. Drafting check (c): the local slopes of ln GALI_3 and ln GALI_4 of orbit C over doubling windows below n = 3000 range from -0.7 to -2.4 and from -0.65 to -5.7; over n in [1e3, 2e4] the fitted slopes are -1.98 and -3.96, and for five seeded small-amplitude starts -1.91 to -1.97 and -3.87 to -3.98. The law B7 needs windows that start at n >= 1e3 and span a decade (criterion 14). One Y4 start (N = 3, K = -1, eps = 0.3) behaves like a 2-torus (k = 3 slope -0.65, k = 4 slope -2.24), as Eq. (34) of SkokosManos2016 predicts for s = 2; starts are classified, not forced.
- F9. Chaotic offsets. The law B6 holds up to a constant (Skokos2007 Eq. (43) is a proportionality). Drafting check (b): ln GALI_k(n) minus (-n sum of exponent gaps from the log-singular values of the 256-bit-quality product) is constant in n to within 0.3 over n = 100 to 1600 on orbit D (k = 2, 3, 4: 2.56 to 2.30, 3.17, 7.52 to 7.38) and on a second start (1.77 to 1.84, 2.85, 3.91). The Float64 singular values of the product are unusable beyond n about 1000 for k = 4 (sigma_1/sigma_4 above 1e16), which is why the 256-bit product of cm criterion 13 is the reference.

Hat = unit vector. l_1 >= l_2 >= ... >= l_d the Lyapunov exponents, E_k the exponent of the chaotic law.

## Algorithm

Where it sits: one reducer on the tangent stream with K columns in the Indicators layer; pure evaluations beside it (no import of a peer layer; the scaled norm comes from Foundation, D69). Per orbit (ensemble member, orbit id j, run seed s), contract G1:

1. Initialisation. The K columns are the first K columns of the seeded orthonormal frame of the stream (s, j), so G_k(0) = 1 and abs(det W_0) = 1 for K = d. A supplied frame is accepted iff G_K(0) >= 10 B_meas(T, K); because the volumes are nested (B3) this implies G_k(0) >= 10 B_meas(T, k) for every k <= K. Otherwise the run is rejected before any work with a numerical health error of kind initial frame (as sali Algorithm item 1, Amendment D67 item 1).
2. At each handoff (step n = j tau), for i = 1..K: alpha_i = scaled Euclidean norm of column i (no overflow or underflow). A zero or non-finite alpha_i raises a numerical health error naming the deviation matrix, orbit id, column and step (as sali). Add ln alpha_i to the compensated sum S_i. Replace column i by the unit vector (a diagonal S, tangent-core E7). The reducer is the one replacer of the chain and sits last (D71).
3. Direct forms. From the K unit columns, evaluate G_k for k = 2..K by the nested route (B2 iv, B12) or the singular values (B2 iii); the evaluation is scaled, uses no library call that the GPU kernel cannot compile (Compute), and reaches the bound B_meas(T, k) (criterion 1). The Gram-determinant evaluation is not allowed (F3 iv).
4. Log form (K = d only). L(n) = ln abs(det W_0) - ((S_1 + S_2) + ... ) formed as the negative of the left-to-right compensated total of S_1..S_d, so that for d = 2 it is the sali L bitwise. A caller with a non-orthonormal frame adds ln abs(det W_0) (sali Amendment D67 item 3). For K < d no log form is reported (F3).
5. Emit G_2..G_K and, for K = d, L at every retained handoff (schedule chosen by the caller, as L1 S8); at the end the final S_i with their compensation terms, G_k(0), and the floor B_meas(T, k) for each k.

Representation. G_k is a number in [0, 1] stored as computed; it is meaningful above B_meas(T, k) (B9). A value below the floor is reported as computed (including an exact 0 or a rounding excess above 1), never clipped, never replaced by a placeholder; the floor is part of the output so that the consumer can mark it. A display of log10 G_k uses max(G_k, B_meas) and says so. The log form is finite to any depth and is the reported quantity beyond the floor for K = d. A value computed in Float64 does not underflow (the floor is far above the smallest normal number).

Parameters recorded per run: tau, element type, state mode (wrapped with counts, or lifted), seed, K, the chain (reducers and which one replaces), frame source (seeded or supplied), floors. Defaults: tau = 1, seeded frame.

Chain order: this reducer last; a pass-through reader may precede it. A column reducer of another k cannot share the stream (F2).

## Invariants

- I1 Range and algebra (B3): 0 <= G_k <= 1 within B_meas; sign, permutation and orthogonal-map invariance; G_{k+1} <= G_k within 2 B_meas(T, k+1).
- I2 k = 2 equivalence (B4): g = SALI sqrt(4 - SALI^2)/2 and SALI/sqrt 2 <= g <= SALI, each within 2 B_meas(T, 2).
- I3 Determinant identity (B5): G_d(n) = abs(det W_0) exp(-sum S_i(n)) for symplectic tangent products; it fails for k < d.
- I4 Transparency: G_k(n) does not depend on tau in exact arithmetic (B1).
- I5 Nesting (B12): G_k of a K-column run equals G_k of a k-column run on the identical orbit within 2 B_meas (bitwise if the column product is column-independent, reported).
- I6 Floor (B9, B10): no value below B_meas is reported as resolved; the log form is resolved to any depth within B_L.
- I7 The orbit is unchanged and the reducer adds only its own state: orbit independence and execution invariance are those of sali (I3, I7).
- I8 Symmetries of the system carry over: the tangent maps of Y3 and Y4 are symplectic, so the exponents pair (cm criterion 13) and E_d = d l_1 (B6).

## Compute

Policy `_config/architecture.md`, "Compute" (D17, D25). Data-parallel: independent orbits, fused into the stream kernel. Per handoff: K scaled norms, K logs, one nested factorisation of a d x K matrix, O(d K^2) operations; for the p3 panel K <= 6. Recommended evaluation: a factorisation written for small fixed sizes (a Householder QR of the unit matrix gives all orders at once, B2 iv), so the kernel compiles for the GPU and the chain is not forced to the CPU under the reason "a reducer in the chain that is CPU-only". If the owner prefers a library singular-value routine, the chain resolves to the CPU with that reason (P2).

Memory per orbit added by the reducer (Float64): K compensated sums and K compensation terms, K - 1 current values, the step index, the handoff count and a health code: sizeof(T) (3K - 1) + 24 bytes. K = 4: 112 bytes (Float64), 200 (Double64); with the tangent-core formula M(d, K, T) = sizeof(T)(d + dK + 2K) + 16 (d = 4, K = 4: 240 bytes) the total is 352 bytes (Float64). Y4 N = 100 (d = 200), K = 4: M = 8 080 bytes plus 112, and a transient d x K work matrix (800 entries) per worker; GPU only if the tangent-core benchmark shows a win for the element type on this machine (risk K15, D25). K = d on a large chain is a dense d x d frame (N = 100: 40 000 entries, 320 kB per orbit) and a CPU case; the criteria cover K = d up to d = 20. Chunk size from free memory at run start, never a constant. BigFloat is CPU-only. CPU-GPU agreement: B11 (the D68 form), Float64 only; Double64 falls back with the recorded reason "kernel does not compile for the element type" if it does not compile, and that is the pass. CI has no GPU. Fixed-size Y4 states (D105) are required for the chain on the stream.
