## Equations

Notation. State z = (q1, q2, p1, p2), positions first (as `_config/research-question.md`). r^2 = q1^2 + q2^2, theta the polar angle of q. eps_T the machine epsilon of the element type.

1. Hamiltonian. H = (p1^2 + p2^2)/2 + V(q), V = (q1^2 + q2^2)/2 + q1^2 q2 - q2^3/3. Sources: AntonopoulosManosSkokos2010 Sect. 3.1, Eq. (2) (read in `data/literature/sali/arxiv_1008.2538.txt`; printed with x, y, px, py for q1, q2, p1, p2); Hairer2008 Sect. 2, "Numerical experiment" (as recorded in integrator-catalog-spec Equations 18; not re-read in this draft). Origin of the system: HenonHeiles1964 (not read, no equation is sourced to it).
2. Equations of motion (derived). dq/dt = p, dp1/dt = -q1 (1 + 2 q2), dp2/dt = -(q2 + q1^2 - q2^2). The force g = grad V = (q1 + 2 q1 q2, q2 + q1^2 - q2^2).
3. Hessian (derived). Hess V = [[1 + 2 q2, 2 q1], [2 q1, 1 - 2 q2]]; third derivatives V_112 = 2, V_222 = -2, others 0. The tangent (variational) flow is d(dz)/dt = [[0, I], [-Hess V(q), 0]] dz.
4. Critical points of V (derived). g = 0: q1 (1 + 2 q2) = 0 and q2 + q1^2 - q2^2 = 0. Case q1 = 0: q2 (1 - q2) = 0, q2 in {0, 1}. Case q2 = -1/2: q1^2 = q2^2 - q2 = 3/4, q1 = +-sqrt(3)/2. Four points: O = (0, 0), V = 0, Hess = I (minimum); S0 = (0, 1), S1,2 = (+-sqrt(3)/2, -1/2); V = 1/6 at the three (S0: 1/2 - 1/3; S1,2: 1/2 - 3/8 + 1/24 = 1/6); Hess at S0 = diag(3, -1); at S1,2 = [[0, +-sqrt(3)], [+-sqrt(3), 2]] with eigenvalues 3 and -1 (lambda^2 - 2 lambda - 3 = 0). All three are saddles. Agreement check: Zotos2017 states the escape energy h_esc = 1/6 and three saddle points on V = 1/6 (benchmarks.md, "Henon-Heiles escape energy": read in arXiv:1709.04360; no equation or section number is recorded, so Zotos2017 is an agreement check here, not the source of an equation). The saddle ordinates printed in benchmarks.md (+1/2) are not critical points of this V (Inconsistencies a).
5. Polar form and D3 symmetry (derived). With w = q1 + i q2, Im(w^3) = 3 q1^2 q2 - q2^3, so V = r^2/2 + (r^3/3) sin(3 theta). The rotation by 2 pi/3 (theta -> theta + 2 pi/3, sin(3 theta + 2 pi) = sin 3 theta) and the reflection q1 -> -q1 (theta -> pi - theta, sin(3 pi - 3 theta) = sin 3 theta) leave V invariant; they generate the dihedral group D3 of order 6. The kinetic term is invariant under the same orthogonal map applied to p, so each element acts as a canonical (point) transformation (q, p) -> (G q, G p) that commutes with the flow and preserves H. The saddles are at theta = pi/2, 7 pi/6, 11 pi/6 (the rotation orbit of S0). Time reversal (q, p) -> (q, -p) maps a solution to a solution traversed backwards, since H is even in p; together with D3 the symmetry group of the flow has order 12.
6. Energy-allowed region and the bound below 1/6 (derived). On each ray V = v(r) + (r^3/3)(s + 1) with v(r) = r^2/2 - r^3/3 and s = sin 3 theta >= -1, so V >= v(r). v increases on [0, 1] from 0 to 1/6 (v' = r (1 - r)). For E < 1/6 let r_b(E) in (0, 1) be the root of v(r) = E. Then V > E on the annulus r_b < r <= 1, so the component of {V <= E} that contains O lies in r <= r_b(E) < 1; an orbit that starts in it stays in it. On that component V >= v(r) >= 0, so |p|^2 <= 2 E. At E = 1/6, r_b = 1 (the three saddles touch the circle r = 1). For E > 1/6, v(1) = 1/6 < E: the barrier is open along the rays sin 3 theta = -1 (theta = pi/2, 7 pi/6, 11 pi/6), the three exits.
7. Section line potential (derived). V(0, q2) = q2^2/2 - q2^3/3 =: u(q2); u(1) = 1/6, u(-1/2) = 1/8 + 1/24 = 1/6, u is increasing on [0, 1], decreasing on [-infinity, 0] and on [1, infinity). For E < 1/6 the roots a(E) < 0 < b(E) <= 1 of u = E bound the origin component of the section domain; at E = 1/6, a = -1/2 and b = 1.
8. Energy-surface start (derived). Given E, q1 = 0, q2, p2 with p2^2/2 + u(q2) <= E, the momentum p1 = +sqrt(2 (E - p2^2/2 - u(q2))) >= 0 (q1 = 0 makes the q1-dependent part of V vanish). The section domain D(E) = {(q2, p2): p2^2/2 + u(q2) <= E}; its origin component has area A(E) = integral over [a, b] of 2 sqrt(2 (E - u(q2))) dq2, and A(E) / (2 pi E) -> 1 as E -> 0 (the harmonic limit). The initial values of the Brouwer experiment of Hairer2008 Sect. 2 (q1 = 0, q2 = 0.3, p2 = 0.2, E = 1/8) give p1^2 = 2 (1/8 - 0.02 - 0.036) = 0.138, p1 = 0.3715 > 0, inside D (drafting arithmetic).
9. Published orbits at E = 1/8 (AntonopoulosManosSkokos2010 Sect. 3.1, in (x, y, px, py) = (q1, q2, p1, p2) with q1 = 0, p2 = 0): R = (0, 0.1, 0.490578, 0), S = (0, -0.0135, 0.499816, 0), C = (0, -0.015, 0.499773, 0); deviation vectors (1, 0, 0, 0) and (0, 0, 1, 0). Printed behaviour: SALI of R fluctuates around 1.25 (ordered); SALI of C reaches the 1e-16 floor after about 1000 time steps (chaotic); S is sticky and turns chaotic after about 5000 time steps. The printed p1 reproduces H = 1/8 only to about 2e-7 (R: 0.1250002, C: 0.12500015, drafting arithmetic), so starts are rebuilt with p1 from Equation 8. The step size of the paper is not stated.
10. Surface of section (definition). Plane Sigma = {q1 = 0}, crossing direction dq1/dt = p1 > 0, section coordinates (q2, p2). Each crossing of an orbit with H = E < 1/6 in the origin component is a point of D(E) (Equation 8); p1 is recovered from E. The choice of plane and direction is open point P3; the initial values of Equation 8 (Hairer2008 Sect. 2) and the printed section of AntonopoulosManosSkokos2010 Sect. 3.1 ("PSS x = 0", coordinates (y, py)) both use it.
11. Crossing location (derived). A fixed-step integrator gives the orbit at times n tau. A crossing is bracketed by two consecutive steps with q1(n tau) < 0 <= q1((n + 1) tau) (and p1 > 0 at the second). Let Phi_s be the one-step map of the same integrator with step s in (0, tau]. The crossing is the root s* of q1(Phi_s(z_n)) = 0, found to |q1| <= 16 eps_T. Because the root is solved with the same method and the step s*, the crossing point carries the integrator's local error O(s*^(p+1)), not an interpolation error; it is not exactly symplectic with the full-step map (stated, tested in criterion 9).
12. Section Jacobian (derived). Let f = (p, -g) be the vector field, Phi the tangent matrix of the (numerical) flow from the start to the crossing at time t*, and grad(q1) = (1, 0, 0, 0). Differentiating q1(phi_t(z)) = 0 at t = t*(z): dt*/dz = -grad(q1)^T Phi / (grad(q1)^T f). Hence the Jacobian of the return to the plane (4 x 4) is DP = (I - f grad(q1)^T / (grad(q1)^T f)) Phi, with grad(q1)^T f = p1 at the crossing. Restriction to the section: a displacement (0, dq2, dp1, dp2) on the energy surface has dp1 = -(g_2 dq2 + p2 dp2) / p1 (from dH = 0 at q1 = 0, g_1 dq1 = 0). The 2 x 2 matrix J = d(q2', p2') / d(q2, p2) follows from DP and this dp1. Expected: det J = 1 (the return map is area preserving, Poincare; to be confirmed by criterion 8, not assumed).
13. Return time (derived). The time between consecutive section points is the sum of the integrator steps plus the sub-step of Equation 11; for E < 1/6 it is bounded below by the shortest period of the harmonic limit region only as a reported value, not a criterion.
14. Escape (derived structure). For E > 1/6 an orbit escapes through one of three exits. Exit sector k in {0, 1, 2} = the center angle theta_k in {pi/2, 7 pi/6, 11 pi/6} nearest to the polar angle of q when r first exceeds R_esc. Escape is declared at the first time r > R_esc (the radius is open point P5); at E <= 1/6 no orbit of the origin component can reach it (Equation 6, R_esc > 1). The D3 rotation by 2 pi/3 maps exit k to k + 1 (mod 3) in the order above; the reflection q1 -> -q1 fixes exit 0 and swaps exits 1 and 2. Above r = 1 along an exit ray V = -r^3/3 + r^2/2, so r'' grows like r^2 and the orbit blows up in finite time, which is why a threshold is reached before overflow (criterion 12).
15. Spectrum structure of the flow (derived). The tangent flow is symplectic, so the spectrum pairs, lambda_i = -lambda_(5-i), i = 1, 2 (diagnostics-spec E4: Skokos2010 Eq. (48) p.28). The flow direction f(z) is mapped to f(phi_t z), which is bounded and (away from equilibria) bounded away from 0 on a bounded energy surface, so one exponent is 0; its pair is the second 0. The spectrum of a 2-DOF orbit is therefore (lambda_1, 0, 0, -lambda_1) in exact arithmetic with lambda_1 >= 0; the two zeros are approached at rates ln(growth)/T and ln(T)/T (the shear growth with the energy, linear in t for regular orbits; stated as proposal for chaotic orbits). The sum of all exponents is 0.
16. Closed forms at fixed points (derived). At S0 (an equilibrium; f vanishes exactly in arithmetic: q1 (1 + 2 q2) = 0 and q2 + q1^2 - q2^2 = 1 - 1 = 0) the tangent flow has constant matrix with Hess = diag(3, -1): eigenvalues +-i sqrt(3) (q1 block) and +-1 (q2 block). The tangent matrix at time t is the q2 block [[cosh t, sinh t], [sinh t, cosh t]] and the q1 block [[cos(sqrt(3) t), sin(sqrt(3) t) / sqrt(3)], [-sqrt(3) sin(sqrt(3) t), cos(sqrt(3) t)]]; the largest singular value is e^t, the spectrum is (1, 0, 0, -1). At O the tangent flow is a rotation with frequencies (1, 1), the case of the harmonic oscillator with unit frequencies (integrator-catalog exact flow); all four exponents are 0.
17. Tangent through the integrator and the GALI laws. The tangent step is the exact derivative of the one-step map and is symplectic: the product of elementary Jacobians, drift [[I, c tau I], [0, I]] and kick [[I, 0], [-d tau Hess V, I]] (SkokosGerlach2010 Eqs. (46), (47), as recorded in integrator-catalog-spec Equations 17; the deviation vector is advanced by the same splitting, SkokosGerlach2010 Eq. (51)). GALI_k decay laws as set in gali-spec B6, B7 (SkokosManos2016 Eqs. (22), (23) p.14; Skokos2007 Eqs. (43), (44), (79), (80), (85)): chaotic, G_k of order exp(-E_k lambda_1 t) with E_k = sum_(j=2..k) (lambda_1 - lambda_j)/lambda_1; with the spectrum of Equation 15, E_2 = 1, E_3 = 2, E_4 = 4 (derived, as in gali-spec B6); regular on a 2-torus: G_2 constant, G_3 of order t^-2, G_4 of order t^-4 (gali-spec B7).

## Algorithm

1. Flow and tangent: the one-step map and its tangent are those of the chosen splitting integrator applied to Equations 2 and 3; nothing new. The default element type is binary64; the other element types are exercised by the precision ladder of Compute.
2. Start on an energy surface: given E, a window for (q2, p2) and a seed, draw (q2, p2) uniformly in the window, keep it if inside D(E) of Equation 8 (rejection), set q1 = 0 and p1 from Equation 8. A request with no admissible point returns a status, never a NaN state. For E > 1/6 the window is given by the user (D(E) is unbounded along q2 > 1) and the start is accepted only inside r <= 1.
3. Section map: integrate with the chosen method, detect bracketed crossings (Equation 11), solve each to |q1| <= 16 eps_T, record (t*, q2, p2) and optionally J of Equation 12. Grazing: a bracket with p1 <= 0 at either end is not a crossing; if |p1| < 8 eps_T at the root the crossing is flagged grazing and not recorded. The first section point of a start on the section is the start itself at t = 0.
4. Portrait at energy E: a uniform grid of n x n starts over the bounding box of D(E), kept where inside D(E); each start integrated to a final time T_f with the one-pass indicator chain of the existing modules (SALI, GALI_k) and the finite-time exponent; a start is called chaotic when its SALI at T_f is below a preregistered threshold (the definition of AntonopoulosManosSkokos2010 Sect. 3.1: 1e-8 at T_f = 2000, 500 x 500 grid; thresholds are preregistered per study, `_config/numerics-policy.md`, so this module fixes the values only for its own criteria). The chaotic fraction is the number of chaotic grid points over the number of points in D(E) (uniform area measure, a derived choice, open point P6).
5. Escape detection: after each step test r > R_esc; the crossing of the circle r = R_esc is located as in Equation 11; the record is (escape time, exit sector of Equation 14, status). A non-finite state also stops the orbit with a status.
6. Spectrum and GALI of a flow orbit: by the existing estimators on the one-step map of the integrator, with the renormalization interval measured in steps. The two zero exponents are reported, not removed.

## Invariants

- I1: the energy H of Equation 1 changes only by the integrator's bounded error (modified-energy oscillation) plus the round-off random walk of integrator-catalog criterion 15; it has no secular drift for these orbits.
- I2: Equation 5 symmetries: V invariant under D3; the one-step map of a palindromic method commutes with the reflection q1 -> -q1 (the force is odd in q1, every operation is sign-symmetric, so bitwise) and with time reversal up to the round-off bound; the rotation commutes up to round-off amplified by the orbit's instability.
- I3: the tangent step has unit determinant and satisfies M^T Omega M = Omega to the diagnostics tolerances (diagnostics-spec Tolerances), the section Jacobian has determinant 1 (criterion 8).
- I4: for E < 1/6 and a start in the origin component, r <= r_b(E) < 1 and |p|^2 <= 2 E for all times (Equation 6); no escape is ever declared.
- I5: the spectrum pairs and its sum vanishes (Equation 15); two exponents are 0 in the limit.
- I6: every recorded section point has |q1| <= 16 eps_T, p1 > 0, and lies in D(E) up to the energy error.

## Compute

Policy: `_config/architecture.md`, "Compute".
- Data parallel, GPU candidates: grids of independent starts of a portrait (one orbit per work item, fixed-step explicit splitting, isbits state of 4 numbers plus the tangent frame of up to 4 x 4 numbers); escape and section crossings per orbit have a variable event count, so the per-orbit record buffer is bounded at launch (3 numbers per crossing, a maximum count per orbit) and a full buffer ends the orbit with a status. The step is the same kernel as the integrators; the module adds no kernel of its own.
- CPU: single long orbits for the full spectrum (QR factorization per renormalization); the crossing root-find has a per-orbit branch and is attempted on the device with fallback "kernel does not compile for the element type" as in integrator-catalog. BigFloat references: CPU.
- Memory per orbit: state 4; tangent frame 4 x k (k <= 4); crossing record 3 per crossing. Chunk size from free memory (policy).
- CPU-vs-GPU agreement: portraits are compared at the estimator level, never bitwise: chaotic fraction within 1 percentage point and pointwise class disagreement at most 2 percent of the grid (criterion 14). The reflection equivariance of criterion 3 is bitwise on the CPU.
- Reproducibility: seeds keyed by orbit id; results identical with 1 and N threads on the CPU.

## Acceptance criteria

Preamble. B(n) is the round-off bound of integrator-catalog-spec (Acceptance criteria preamble) in units of eps_T; tau denotes the step; "order-8 table" the table S12 of integrator-catalog and "order-4 table" the Forest-Ruth table S5; the default step 1/20 is a proposal. The gradient vanishes at O, S0, S1, S2 to 2^-200 in 256-bit arithmetic; V takes the exact values 0, 1/6, 1/6, 1/6 to 2^-200; the Hessian eigenvalues are (1, 1), (3, -1), (3, -1), (3, -1) to 2^-200; the points (+-sqrt(3)/2, +1/2) of benchmarks.md are not critical (gradient norm at least 1). Pass: all four checks.
2. Force and Hessian (Equations 2, 3, O2). Against the AD and complex-step values at 1000 seeded points in [-1.5, 1.5]^2: absolute error at most 8 eps_T max(1, |entry|) in binary64 and in double-double. Pass: all points.
3. D3 and time-reversal symmetry (Equation 5, I2). (a) |V(G q) - V(q)| <= 8 eps_T max(1, |V|) for the six elements at 1000 seeded points; (b) one step of the order-8 table from a seeded start commutes with the reflection q1 -> -q1 bitwise (1000 starts); (c) after n = 1000 steps from a start at E = 1/8 the rotation by 2 pi/3 (computed in 256-bit arithmetic and rounded) commutes within 16 B(n) amplified by exp(lambda_1 n tau) with lambda_1 of the orbit, 20 starts; (d) n steps, p -> -p, n steps return to the start within B(n) (n = 10^4, palindromic tables). Pass: all four.
4. Energy-surface starts (Equation 8, Algorithm 2). For E in {1/12, 1/8, 1/6}, 10^5 seeded starts: |H - E| <= 4 eps_T max(1, E); p1 > 0; the start lies in D(E); the empirical area fraction of the grid agrees with the quadrature A(E) of O1 to relative 1e-3 (a 1000 x 1000 grid). 5. Bound below the escape energy (Equation 6, I4). For E in {1/12, 1/8, 1/6}, 200 seeded starts each, T = 10^3, order-8 table, tau = 1/20: max over the orbit of r <= r_b(E) (1 + 10^-6) and |p|^2 <= 2 E (1 + 10^-6), and no escape is declared. Pass: all orbits.
6. Energy conservation (I1). Same orbits at E = 1/8, T = 10^3: max over n of |H(y_n) - H(y_0)| <= 10^-8 for the order-8 table at tau = 1/20 and <= 10^-4 for the order-4 table at tau = 1/20 (proposals; the scaling with tau^p is reported on tau, tau/2, tau/4). Pass: both bounds.
7. Crossings (Equations 10, 11, O3). 20 seeded starts at each E in {1/12, 1/8, 1/6}, T = 10^3: every recorded point has |q1| <= 16 eps_T and p1 > 0 (I6); the number of crossings equals that of the Taylor reference (no missed or extra crossing); each crossing point (q2, p2) and time agree with the reference to 10^-8 for the order-8 table at tau = 1/20 (proposal). A start that grazes (|p1| < 8 eps_T) is recorded as flagged, not as a crossing. Pass: all.
8. Section Jacobian (Equation 12). On the crossings of criterion 7: J agrees with the finite-difference Jacobian of the reference section map to 10^-6 (proposal; the reference differs from the tested map by the order-8 error); |det J - 1| <= det_residual_tol eps_T (1 + M_max) times the number of steps between crossings, the cumulative form of diagnostics-spec Tolerances, plus 4 eps_T for the energy-surface restriction. Pass: all crossings.
9. Section map symplecticity in the presence of the sub-step (Equation 11). The cumulative |det J - 1| of criterion 8 on 10^3 consecutive crossings of one chaotic orbit at E = 1/8 does not grow faster than linearly in the number of crossings, and its log-log slope against the crossing count is within 0.2 of 0.5 (random-walk) or 1; reported both ways, and the failure of exact symplecticity of the sub-step is quantified, not scored. Pass: slope at most 1.2.
10. Portrait reproduction (O5). E = 1/8, 200 x 200 grid (the paper's 500 x 500 is the preregistered full size), T_f = 2000, SALI threshold 1e-8, order-8 table: (a) the chaotic fraction is within 10 percentage points of 55 percent (proposal: the paper's measure is not stated); (b) orbit R: SALI(2000) in [0.5, 2] (printed about 1.25); (c) orbit C: SALI(2000) < 1e-8 and a SALI of at most 1e-12 reached before t = 10^4 (the printed 1e-16 floor lies near the 1e-14 resolution of binary64); (d) orbit S is reported only. Pass: (a), (b), (c). Not drafted-checked.
11. Fractions across the three energies. The chaotic fractions f(1/12) < f(1/8) < f(1/6), each gap at least 5 percentage points, from the same grid and thresholds (the monotone growth is the statement of ZhaoDu2007, low authority, and the paper's own energy scan in Fig. 4; it is not a source of a number). The fractions from the order-8 table and the order-4 table at tau = 1/20 differ by at most 3 percentage points at each E (integrator effect on the portrait, reported per row). Pass: all.
12. Escape (Equation 14, O3, O6). E in {0.2, 0.25}, 1000 seeded starts each inside r <= 1, order-8 table: (a) every declared escape has r >= R_esc at the flag and, continued to r = 2 R_esc or blow-up, never returns below R_esc / 2 (0 violations); (b) the escape time (first r > R_esc, located by Equation 11) agrees with the reference to 10^-6 for escapes before t = 100 (proposal), at the order-8 table and tau = 1/20; (c) the exit label is equivariant: the reflection changes label k to (-k) mod 3 bitwise (every start, labels 1 and 2 swapped, 0 fixed), and the rotation by 2 pi/3 shifts the label by 1 for the starts that escape before t = 50 (the rotation is rounded, the amplification is small); (d) a start with a non-finite state, and a step that overflows, end with a status, never an exception. ## Open points

- P1. Promotion. Proposed: promote the existing flow in place to F3 (rename the "test-only T2" wording, add invariants, symmetry, energy-surface starts); integrator-catalog criterion 15 stays where it is. Alternative: move criterion 15 here (integrator-catalog P16 alternative).
- P2. Placement. Proposed: the system and the energy-surface start in Systems; the section and escape tools in Integrators (it already owns the stepper and event stepping); the portrait grids and classification stay with Experiments/the study. Alternative: a new module above Tangent for section, escape and portraits.
- P3. Section plane and direction. Proposed: q1 = 0, p1 > 0, coordinates (q2, p2) (the plane of the Brouwer start of Hairer2008 and of the section of AntonopoulosManosSkokos2010). Alternative: also provide the q2 = 0 plane (needs the second symmetry-adapted domain).
- P4. Crossing location. Proposed: root solve with the integrator's own step s in (0, tau] (Equation 11). Alternative: Henon's variable change (no source in the bibliography, not exactly symplectic under a splitting) or Hermite interpolation of the bracket (error independent of the method).
- P5. Escape radius R_esc and rule. Proposed: R_esc = 10, first passage, with the 0-violation continuation test of criterion 12(a). Alternative: a derived no-return region (needs a proof not attempted here), or R_esc = 5 for earlier stopping.
- P6. Chaos measure and reference. Proposed: area measure on D(E), SALI < 1e-8 at 2000, 55 percent +- 10 points at E = 1/8 from the preprint. Alternative: report only, until HenonHeiles1964 is read (then add its percentages as a primary reference).
- P7. Portrait deliverable. Proposed: data only (grids, labels, fractions, indicator values), no figures. Alternative: include a figure script in the study.
- P8. Energy tolerance key. Proposed: no key (criterion 6 bounds only). Alternative: define `energy_error_tol` as a multiple of the integrator's local energy error constant.
- P9. Default integrator and step for this system: proposed order-8 table at tau = 1/20 and the order-4 table as the comparison; both to be calibrated by a drafting run. Alternative: the Hairer step 2/240 for the order-8 composition (integrator-catalog criterion 15).
- P10. Use of the symmetry: proposed as a test only (criterion 3), no folding of the portrait; alternative: fold by time reversal and reflection (the p2 symmetry used in the "fast PSS scanning" of AntonopoulosManosSkokos2010), which reduces the work to a quarter, with the folding error to be measured against criterion 10.
