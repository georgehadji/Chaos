# Spec: lyap-gram-schmidt

Status: approved at the Batch B 01-spec checkpoint, D30 (branch m2-p1-modules). Layer: Lyapunov (`_config/architecture.md`, "Module map"). Holds the SHARED definitions of the orthonormalisation-based spectrum (sections Equations G1 to G13 and Algorithm); `lyap-svd-stabilized` and `lyap-householder-qr` point here and do not repeat them. Conventions, the identical-orbit protocol, the comparison rule, the panel and starts, the single-vector items: `lyap-benettin` S1, S2, S5, S7 and E8 to E14 (pointed to, not repeated). Spec-local open points are P1 to P3; the owner's answers to the Batch B review are decision D30, cited as (D30). Notation: d = phase-space dimension, k = number of deviation vectors (1 <= k <= d), tau = handoff interval in map steps, j = window index, n = j tau, T = element type, eps = eps(T) (spacing at 1), u = eps/2. Files cited as "tangent-core item X" are `output/tangent-core-spec.md`; "diagnostics X" are `output/diagnostics-spec.md`. DanielGragg1976 pages are PDF pages (journal page = PDF page + 771).

## Scope

- Catalog rows (`_config/catalogs/lyapunov.md`, section B): L3 classical Gram-Schmidt (CGS), L4 modified Gram-Schmidt (MGS), L4b repeated Gram-Schmidt (RGS). k = 1 of every variant is the Benettin estimator (row L1, slug `lyap-benettin`, specified separately); Householder and Givens variants (L5, L5b, L6) are specified in `lyap-householder-qr`; their agreement with this module is its AC8(b).
- Prerequisites, all `planned` in the catalogs and at stage 04 on this branch (not yet `verified`): tangent-core (stream, tau, health, compensated sums), standard-map (Y1, Y5, wrapped system of decision D28), diagnostics (residual definitions), precision (element types). Decisions D20 (Double64), D24 (no Float32), D28 (wrapped state), D17 (GPU first, CPU fallback).
- Out of scope: exponents from singular values (`lyap-svd-stabilized`), the single-vector and two-trajectory estimators, adaptive tau (L7b), banded QR (L7c), continuous QR (L8, M5), covariant vectors (M5).
- Open points for the owner:
  - P1 (D30) Key `orthogonality_tol = 8` as defined in Algorithm, Tolerances (multiplier of eps x k x g(method); g = 1 for RGS, Householder and Givens); alternatives: a method-independent value (CGS and MGS would then trip at G_w near 8 k, i.e. at tau = 1 for K >= 3). Flagged consequence of the chosen structure: in the band eps^(-1/2) < G_w < 1/(2 eps) the CGS and MGS orthogonality check is vacuous (threshold of order 1) and only theta_sum, itself of order 1 there, speaks; the alternative cutoff G_w >= eps^(-1/2) (the tangent-core tau_max rule) closes the band at the price of rejecting runs MGS can still do.
  - P2 (D30) New health kind "orthonormalisation breakdown" (also used by `lyap-householder-qr` for an exact zero pivot) and the rule G_w eps >= 1/2 (CGS, MGS, RGS only): needs an entry in the diagnostics name list (placed last in the fixed order) and a one-line addition to the tangent-core health description.
  - P3 (D30) Provenance attributes (schema bump, as tangent-core proposed for tau, k, seed): `orthonormalizer` with one value list for the QR family (Algorithm item 3), plus the iteration path and `implementation` of `lyap-householder-qr`.
- Source gaps, stated plainly. (1) The printed Gram-Schmidt of Skokos2010 Eq. (76) is the classical form; modified Gram-Schmidt is NOT printed in the two permitted full texts, so G4 is derived. (2) Orthogonality-loss laws of CGS and MGS in terms of conditioning are not in a readable source; Algorithm "Tolerances" gives measured models (scratch runs by the spec author, to be reproduced in stage 04), not citations. (3) ShimadaNagashima1979 and Benettin1980b have no full text here: attribution only through Skokos2010 p.47. Geist1990 is read in `lyap-householder-qr`; no number from it is used here.

## Algorithm

The estimator is a reducer in the chain of the tangent stream (tangent-core "Contract"): it receives (orbit id, step n, point x_n, W_n) every tau steps, may replace W (E7), and later reducers see the replaced matrix; it reads x only through the point, so the wrapped state of D28 (state longer than the tangent dimension) needs no change (tangent-core Amendment A1, A5).

1. At each handoff j: factor W_j = Q_j R_j with the chosen variant (G2, G4 or G5). Replace W by Q_j. Offer (Q_j, the diagonal of R_j, lambda_i(n), G(j) of G10 and the resolved threshold_orth(j)) to the diagnostics reducer later in the chain (it cannot form g(method) itself). Column norms gamma_m are evaluated with the overflow-safe norm of `lyap-benettin` Algorithm step 1 (needed for AC10 to hold bitwise).
2. Accumulate ln R_{j,ii} in the compensated form of tangent-core "Accumulated logarithms" (S_i and a compensation term per i). lambda_i(n) = S_i / n for all i <= k at every handoff; the retained series cadence is the study's.
3. Variant is a run parameter recorded in provenance (`orthonormalizer`, the single value list of the QR family: CGS, MGS, RGS here; V-H, V-A, V-G of `lyap-householder-qr` (reflectors, augmented reflectors, rotations); plus, where they apply, the iteration path {diagonal, treppen} and `implementation` {library, kernel}; plus k, tau, precision, backend; schema addition proposed, `schema/run-store.md` root attributes (D30)). It is never switched during a run.
4. RGS: per column, the number of passes is recorded (maximum per orbit). Derived expectation: a first pass leaves a residual with relative component about eps ‖w‖ / ‖v'‖ along Q; once the window cancellation ratio ‖w‖/‖v'‖ is below 1/eps the second pass is reached and the test of G5 stops there, so exactly two passes (one for column 1) under the tangent-core rule tau <= tau_max (condition number at most eps^(-1/2)). Scratch: maximum 2 passes for G_w up to 1.5e13 (Y5 pair, tau 1 to 12); cost about twice CGS, as the catalog row states.
5. Breakdown (health). Raise the numerical health error of tangent-core, kind "orthonormalisation breakdown" (new name; the diagnostics name list det, symplecticity, orthogonality, sum, pairing has none, open point P2 (D30)), carrying orbit id, step index, column, when: a pivot gamma_m is zero or not finite; RGS needs a fifth pass; or G_w(j) x eps >= 1/2 (derived: cond(W) eps >= 1 means a rounding-size perturbation can make W singular, so the columns are not determined; 1/2 keeps one bit of margin). Why a separate rule: threshold_sum of diagnostics grows with G_max and cannot flag G_w > 1/eps. The G_w eps >= 1/2 rule applies to CGS, MGS and RGS only; Householder and Givens (`lyap-householder-qr`) raise this kind for an exact zero pivot only (D30).
6. k = 1: all three variants reduce to normalisation, u_1 = w_1 (Skokos2010 p.43, "u_1 coincides with w_1"); the result must equal `lyap-benettin` (AC10).

Tolerances. Proposed key `orthogonality_tol = 8` in `numerics-defaults.toml` (dimensionless multiplier of eps(T), as the diagnostics keys; toml comment: "multiplier of eps(T) x k x g(method); g = 1 for RGS, Householder and Givens, g = G_w for CGS and MGS; lyap-gram-schmidt spec, Tolerances"). Health threshold: threshold_orth(j) = orthogonality_tol x eps x k x g, g = 1 (RGS, Householder, Givens), g = G_w(j) (CGS, MGS); `lyap-householder-qr` applies this threshold with k, not d (D30). eps_orth is recorded every handoff whatever the threshold (diagnostics D4); a study may set the key to infinity to measure loss, a non-finite value still raises (diagnostics, Health error).
Justification (scratch runs by the spec author, Float64, to be reproduced in stage 04): (i) 8 k eps is the multiplier tangent-core AC7(a) already accepts for a seeded frame. (ii) RGS: max loss 2.2 eps over standard map K in {0.5, 2, 5, 10, 2 pi} x tau in {1, 2, 4} (2 x 10^4 steps), and 2.0 eps over real-hyperbolic Y5 sets with two and three pairs (tau 1 to 8, one frame) and over 20 seeded frames of the integer pair of G12c (tau 1 and 3); the threshold 8 k eps gives margin 7 (k = 2) to 16 (k = 4). DanielGragg1976 predicts the same order: attainable limiting precision about 3 m delta_0 / 2 without accumulated inner products (p.12) and ‖Q^T Q - I‖_F / (sqrt n delta_0) = 0.90 to 1.03 in Table 1, criterion 1 (p.22), with their m = d, n = k, delta_0 = u. (iii) CGS = MGS at k = 2 (one projection, same operations): measured loss <= 1.05 eps G_w on all 15 (K, tau) standard-map cases (G_w from 4.5 to 5e8), so 8 k G_w eps gives margin 15. (iv) MGS at k = 4: loss/(eps G_w) between 0.40 and 0.75 (tau 1 to 8, all sets above), margin 40 or more. (v) CGS at k = 4: loss between 0.006 and 0.06 times eps G_w^2 for G_w up to 10^9 (saturating near 1), so threshold_orth trips from G_w of order 10^3 (integer pair at tau = 3, G_w = 9.5e3: 20 of 20 frames): the documented failure of row L3, reported as a health error, not hidden. (vi) At tau = 1 (project default) every variant passes on the standard map up to K = 2 pi (G_w <= 95) and on the Y5 pair (G_w = 45).
Open assumption O3 of diagnostics (constant c <= 4 in B4): measured |sum_i lambda_i| / (eps (G_max/tau + M_max)) <= 0.015 for all three variants on the standard map (five K, tau 1 to 4, 2 x 10^4 steps, compensated sums) and <= 0.056 on the integer pair of G12c (tau 1 to 4, 5 frames, 500 windows, compensated sums), i.e. at least 70 times below c = 4; confirmed for these variants, to be re-measured in stage 04.

## Invariants

- I1 Variant independence in exact arithmetic: S_i(n), lambda_i(n) do not depend on the variant, on tau, or on the columns of Q_0 beyond the first i (G3, G7).
- I2 Q_j^T Q_j = I_k up to eps_orth <= threshold_orth(j) at every handoff; R_{j,ii} > 0.
- I3 sum_i S_i(n) = 0 at every n for det = 1 maps (G9), to diagnostics threshold_sum. Pairing equals the sum for d = 2 (diagnostics I3); for d >= 4 QR-diagonal exponents pair only asymptotically (diagnostics I4), so no finite-time pairing criterion is set for k = d >= 4.
- I4 Volume: sum_{i<=p} S_i(n) = ln vol_p(Y_n Q_{0,p}) (G7); sandwiched by singular values (G8).
- I5 Orbit independence, thread, chunk and backend rules of tangent-core I5; the estimator allocates nothing after warm-up; variant choice and tau are recorded, never inferred.
- I6 CGS and MGS are bitwise equal for k = 2 (identical operations); they differ for k >= 3.
- I7 Lifted coordinates are never wrapped inside the estimator; the wrapped system of D28 gives the same estimator input (the tangent depends on x only).

## Compute

Policy: `_config/architecture.md`, "Compute" (D17, D25). Owner decision, M2 kickoff: Double64 (DoubleFloats.jl) extended, BigFloat CPU-only (GPU request: reason "kernel does not compile for the element type").
- Data-parallel: independent orbits (grids, sweeps); within an orbit the windows are sequential. CGS, MGS and RGS use only dot products and axpy updates: no LAPACK, no CPU-only reason; fused into the same KernelAbstractions kernel as the stream (one source for CPU and CUDA). GPU candidate for d <= 8 (module map: StaticArrays dimension bound; per-orbit Q and R then fit thread-private storage, 8 x 8 Float64 = 512 B plus 36 x 8 = 288 B). For d > 8 a GPU win must be measured (risk K15, GTX 1050 cc 6.1, weak Float64) before `auto` picks the GPU. RGS has a data-dependent pass count (at most 4): mild divergence, no change of the rule.
- CPU-only: BigFloat oracle runs and single long orbits, as in tangent-core.
- Memory per orbit beyond tangent-core's M(s, d, k, T): zero for CGS and MGS (S_i and compensation are already counted, the triangular factor is per-worker scratch of sizeof(T) (k (k+1)/2 + 2 d) bytes); RGS adds an 8-byte pass maximum. Worked: d = k = 2, Float64: 96 + 0 (CGS, MGS), 104 (RGS); d = k = 4: 240 + 0, 248. Chunk size follows tangent-core's formula with these values.
- CPU vs GPU: never bitwise; compare S_i / n with the preregistered tolerances of tangent-core AC12, for every i <= k (AC12 below). Results on the GPU are computed locally; CI runs the CPU backend only.

## Oracle

Package suite (CI, CPU backend, small and self-contained): G12 analytic cases (integer matrices, K = 0), Gram-determinant and 256-bit chain recomputation on the identical realized orbit points (tangent-core AC4 style: evaluate the tangent at BigFloat precision at the SAME Float64 or Double64 orbit points; never a separate 256-bit trajectory, risk K18), cross-variant and cross-tau agreement, orthogonality and breakdown tests.
Verification environment (stage 04, `verify/`, owner decision D21/D27, not in `Pkg.test()`): the ChaosTools full-spectrum routine on the same system and parameters, lifted rule (facts and rule: `lyap-benettin` Oracle, where it accepts the identical initial deviation vectors (D30); AC13 takes lambda_1 pathwise from `lyap-benettin` AC13, since the first QR column equals the Benettin vector, S4(a), and stays at ensemble level for lambda_2), the CPU-vs-GPU comparison (tangent-core AC12), measured ratios loss/(eps G_w), pass histograms, scratch constants above. The comparisons ORACLE-CT1 and ORACLE-CT2 deferred in `tangent-core-verify-log.txt` belong to `lyap-benettin` stage 04; AC13 below is the spectrum version. Published lambda(K) values are loose sanity checks only (risk K18): the non-scored line in the 04 report compares lambda_1 at K = 10 with ln(K/2) (benchmarks.md "Standard map largest Lyapunov exponent, large K").
