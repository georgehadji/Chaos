# Spec: lyap-householder-qr

Status: approved at the Batch B 01-spec checkpoint, D30, branch m2-p1-modules. Float32 is out of M2 (decision D24); no Float32 case here. Layer: Lyapunov, a reducer on the tangent stream (`_config/architecture.md`, "Module map" row Lyapunov). Notation as in `tangent-core-spec.md`: d phase-space dimension, k deviation vectors (1 <= k <= d), tau handoff interval in map steps, T element type, W the d x k deviation matrix handed over, n a step count (multiple of tau). eps(T) is the spacing of T at 1 (Float64 2^-52); for Double64 the effective value 2^-100 (`precision-spec.md`, Amendments). Exponents are per map step. Shared items are pointed to, not repeated: conventions, identical-orbit protocol, comparison rule, panel and starts in `lyap-benettin` S1, S2, S5, S7; QR existence, the exponent formula, the volume identity, the tolerance unit and the health kind in `lyap-gram-schmidt` (GS) G3, G6, G7, G13, Algorithm item 5. Spec-local open points are P1 to P9; the owner's answers to the Batch B review are decision D30, cited as (D30).

## Scope

- Catalog rows (`_config/catalogs/lyapunov.md`, section B): L5 (Householder QR, "default reference"), L5b (Householder variants H1 and H2), L6 (Givens rotations QR). All three are reducers that turn the shared tangent stream into the QR-diagonal exponents; they differ only in the orthonormalization factorization (catalog intro of section A/B). Row texts that do not match the sources read are listed under Open points.
- Prerequisites (all `planned` in M2, none `verified`): tangent-core (the reducer contract and replacement algebra E7, the interval tau, the health error, compensated sums, Compute; cited as `tangent-core-spec.md` items), standard-map (Y1 and the Y5 linear symplectic maps), precision (element types, effective eps), diagnostics (consumes Q, the exponents and the window ratio G; `diagnostics-spec.md` Algorithm, "Inputs delivered by the tangent stream and the driving estimator").
- Out of scope: Gram-Schmidt family (lyap-gram-schmidt), single-vector Benettin (lyap-benettin), shadow orbit (lyap-two-trajectory), SVD (lyap-svd-stabilized); continuous QR (L8, M5), adaptive tau (L7b), banded or sparse QR (L7c), covariant vectors (L12). Flows: the reducer is system-independent, but only maps are verified in M2.
- numerics-defaults.toml: this spec first needs no key. `orthogonality_tol` belongs to lyap-gram-schmidt and is read here with g = 1 (threshold 8 k eps, GS Tolerances; (D30)); `pairing_residual_tol` enters through theta_sum (GS G13). Proposed values: none.
- Open points for the owner: P1 (D30) catalog L5b text is wrong against Geist1990 (below, Equations E7). P2 (D30) catalog L6 cites Geist1990, which contains no Givens method; Givens appears in Householder1958 p.2 and DanielGragg1976 Sect. 3 p.3-4. The L5 note "backward stable" and the L6 note "cheap for d = 2, 4 and banded" are not in any text read; they enter here as assumption A1 and derived counts. P3 (D30) Geist1990 Tables II and III are now readable (p.11, p.12); the `unverified.md` entry on the Toda CPU ratios can be resolved by a gated write (D26 style); the numbers stay a reproduction target, never an oracle (catalog D3). P4 Geist1990 (A-3) lets j run to k-1, correct only for k = d; the count used here is derived (Algorithm, A1); resolved by E9, not an owner decision. P5 (D30) H1 needs a stream with d columns; for the only M2 dimension d = 2 it costs the same as H2, so ship H1 only if the owner wants the Geist parity. P6 (D30) whether the hand-written small-dimension kernel (Compute) is built in M2 or left to the D29 time-boxed GPU step. P7 (D30) a rank-loss case raises the health kind "orthonormalisation breakdown" of GS (exact zero R_ii only); the alternative is the existing kind "deviation matrix". P8 (D30) the diagnostics constant 4 (diagnostics O3) is no longer the acceptance threshold: theta_sum (GS G13) is, and the measured c' is reported against 4 (AC7c). P9 (D30) resolution precedence of the new fallback reason (Compute).

## Algorithm

At every handoff (tangent-core Algorithm, "Contract"), per orbit, with W finite (the stream has already checked finiteness; a non-finite W never reaches this reducer):
1. Factor W = Q R by the run's orthonormalizer (the parameter `orthonormalizer`, recorded in provenance), using only W. Variant V-H (reflectors, E4, E5): for j = 1..r(d,k) form c^(j) with the overflow-safe norm of `lyap-benettin` Algorithm step 1 (entries near 1e200 or 1e-200 neither overflow nor underflow), use sign(0) = +1, skip the reflector if c^(j) = 0, apply H^(j) to columns j..k; Q = H^(1) ... H^(r) applied to the first k columns of I_d, reflectors in reverse order. Variant V-A (augmented, E6): same reflectors applied to [W, I_d]; Q read from S. Variant V-G (rotations, E8): for column j = 1..min(k, d-1), for row i = d down to j+1, rotate rows i-1 and i with rho from an overflow-safe norm (skip if rho = 0); Q = transposed rotations applied to the first k columns of I_d. In all three: rho_i = diagonal entries; if rho_i < 0 flip column i of Q and rho_i (E5); R_ii = rho_i >= 0. All variants give the unique factor of E1 up to rounding, so Q and R_ii are directly comparable across variants and across lyap-gram-schmidt.
2. If some R_ii = 0 (rank loss), raise the health error of kind "orthonormalisation breakdown" (GS Algorithm item 5) with orbit id, handoff step and column index (D30); never form ln 0. The rule G_w eps >= 1/2 of GS does not apply to Householder and Givens: at a nearly singular but nonzero W they continue (Q stays orthogonal to 8 k eps), so at the GS AC7 case (A^20 in Float64) GS raises and this reducer does not; the stage 04 report states this.
3. For i = 1..k add ln R_ii to the compensated accumulator of column i (tangent-core, "Accumulated logarithms"); lambda_i(n) = S_i(n)/n (D1).
4. Replace W by Q (tangent-core E7 with S = R, invariant I3); later reducers see Q, which is what `diagnostics-spec.md` D4 needs. Publish lambda_i(n), the window diagonal R_ii and G_j (D2; for k < d the singular-value ratio of R is reported only for information).
5. Iteration paths (E7). Diagonal path (H2, default): the stream carries k columns and W is the stream's matrix. Treppen path (H1): the stream carries d columns and the reducer replaces them by I_d after each handoff (tangent-core E7 with S = the window matrix, invertible); at the next handoff the stream matrix is the window product Y_win, the reducer forms W = Y_win Q_prev from its stored basis Q_prev (d x k, reducer state) and goes to step 1. Both paths factor the same matrix Y_win Q_prev, so in exact arithmetic they agree (derived); they differ in propagation work (d versus k columns) and rounding.
6. Parameters tau, k, the seeded initial orthonormal basis Q_0, T and the seed are tangent-core's. The reducer reads no state component, only W, so it is unchanged for the longer state vector of decision D28 (tangent-core Amendment, A5). Any tau is allowed; the guidance on tau_max(T) is tangent-core's (Algorithm, "tau"), and AC13 measures its effect here. Wrapped versus lifted state changes nothing in this module.

## Invariants

- I1 After every handoff Q^T Q = I_k within 8 k eps (max-abs, `diagnostics-spec.md` D4; orthogonality_tol = 8 with g = 1, GS Tolerances; (D30)), R is upper triangular with exact zeros below the diagonal and R_ii > 0, and W = Q R column-wise within A1.
- I2 In exact arithmetic S_i(n) depends only on n and Q_0 (E3): not on tau, not on the variant, not on the sign convention (flipping a column sign of Q leaves every |R_ii| unchanged: W D = Q R D with D = diag(+-1), derived).
- I3 For k = d, sum_i S_i(n) = ln|det Y_n| (E3 with i = d), which is 0 for symplectic windows (`diagnostics-spec.md` I2); for d = 2 also lambda_1 + lambda_2 = eps_sum = eps_pair.
- I4 Replacement transparency (tangent-core I3): the replaced matrix is Q, so the true deviation matrix is recovered as Q times the product of the R factors.
- I5 Orbit independence and bitwise thread and chunk invariance on the CPU backend, for every variant (single-threaded factorization per orbit; tangent-core I5).
- I6 No logarithm of a non-positive number is ever formed; no allocation in the hot loop after warm-up.
- I7 The result does not depend on the state length s (D28).

## Compute

Policy: `_config/architecture.md`, "Compute" (D17, D25, D29); owner decision, M2 kickoff: Double64 is the extended type, BigFloat only for short CPU oracle runs.

- Data parallel: independent orbits. Within one orbit the work is sequential (the stream in n, the factorization in columns). Per handoff cost is O(d k^2), counts in E9.
- Library path versus kernel path. This is the first reducer with a CPU-only implementation: a Householder factorization through LAPACK (via Julia's LinearAlgebra). LAPACK exists for binary32 and binary64 only, so in M2 the library path serves Float64 (and scales to d up to 200, `_config/architecture.md` "Scalability"); Double64 and BigFloat use the hand-written element-type-generic code (measured here: the generic factorization also accepts BigFloat and returns negative diagonal entries, so E5's sign fix is needed on both paths). Provenance records `implementation` = library or kernel, with `orthonormalizer` and the iteration path (value list: GS Algorithm item 3, (D30)).
- When the fallback reason "reducer is CPU-only" applies (D25, architecture.md "Compute"): when the reducer chain contains the library path, for any element type, because a library call cannot be fused into the GPU kernel. It is decided when the chain is built, before any chunk runs, holds for the whole run and for every chunk, and is recorded as the exact string "reducer is CPU-only". Proposed precedence (P9, (D30)): element-type reason first (BigFloat gives "kernel does not compile for the element type", `precision-spec.md` Algorithm 4), then "reducer is CPU-only", then device reasons ("CUDA.jl not loadable", "no functional device", ...). Reason: the string is then a property of the configuration, identical on CI and on the GTX 1050 machine. A `cpu` request records an empty reason. The kernel path never triggers this reason.
- GPU path by a hand-written small-dimension kernel: for fixed small d and k (target d <= 8, StaticArrays range) the same factorization (E5 or E8) is written as straight-line code over compile-time-constant loop bounds on isbits scalars, so the loops unroll and the scratch lives in registers or local memory; no library call, no allocation, no exception: rank loss and non-finite values set a per-orbit flag the host reads at chunk end, as in tangent-core ("Numerical health"). It is fused into the same KernelAbstractions kernel as the stream (one source for CPU and GPU, `_config/coding-conventions.md` "Code"), one orbit per work-item, so the CPU backend runs the very code that CUDA runs and is its verified reference. Larger d stays on the library path on CPU. Whether it is faster is measured, not assumed (K15, GTX 1050 cc 6.1, weak Float64; D25): `auto` picks the GPU for this chain and element type only after the AC14 record shows GPU time <= CPU time for the whole chain.
- Extra memory per orbit beyond tangent-core (derived upper bound, no in-place assumption): sizeof(T) (d k + 2 k + d) for reflector vectors, scale factors and the column workspace; Double64 is twice Float64. Worked: d = 2, k = 2, Float64: 8 (4 + 4 + 2) = 80 bytes on top of the 96 bytes of tangent-core (total 176); d = 4, k = 4: 8 (16 + 8 + 4) = 224 bytes on top of 240. Treppen path adds the stored basis d k scalars. Chunk size follows `chunk = floor(f free_bytes / (M + reducer bytes))` (tangent-core Compute).
- CPU worker threads over orbits with BLAS threads set to 1 when worker threads > 1, so each factorization is single-threaded and bitwise invariance is possible.
- CPU versus GPU agreement: never bitwise; compare lambda_i(n) (accumulated logs divided by n) at the tolerances of AC14, never per-step values (K16).

## Oracle

- Package suite (CI, CPU backend, small and self-contained): analytic Y5 shear and cat map, K = 0 (E12, E13); multi-block hyperbolic Y5 with known spectrum (AC6); the E3 identity on the Y5 maps against the Gram-determinant volume of the exact product at 2048 bits (the determinant of nearly parallel columns loses about 2 n (lambda_1 - lambda_i)/ln 2 bits, so 256 bits is not enough at n = 500; BigFloat is part of the language); a 256-bit run of the same estimator on the same stored orbit points (tangent matrices evaluated at the stored Float64 points, never a separate 256-bit trajectory, K18).
- Verification environment (stage 04, `verify/`, owner decision, M2 kickoff, D27): ChaosTools full-spectrum routine (catalog D2) on the same K and start, for the two oracle checks deferred from tangent-core (`tangent-core-verify-log.txt`, DEFERRED lines) in their k = 2 form (AC17); the k = 1 form belongs to lyap-benettin. ChaosTools facts and the lifted rule: `lyap-benettin` Oracle (it accepts the identical initial vectors (D30)); AC17 takes lambda_1 pathwise from `lyap-benettin` AC13 (S4(a)) and stays at long-horizon level for i = 2. TaylorIntegration BigFloat applies to flows (M5) and ForwardDiff to systems, neither here.
- Cross-estimator checks on the identical orbit, basis and tau against lyap-benettin, lyap-gram-schmidt, lyap-svd-stabilized and lyap-two-trajectory: AC8.
- K18: no published lambda(K) is a pass condition; AC18 only reports.
