# Spec: sali

Library layer: Indicators (`_config/architecture.md`, "Module map" row Indicators). Milestone M4 (decision D65). Status: approved at the stage 01 checkpoint, 2026-10-21 (D66): P1 candidate (a), P2 yes (the log form is retained), P3 at sali stage 05, P4 to P8 as proposed. Shared definitions are in `lyap-benettin-spec.md` (conventions and symbols S1, identical orbit S2, same-orbit rounding bound S3 as amended by its Amendment D35 including the amplification sum Psi_n, statistical rule S5, CPU-vs-GPU pointer S6, panel and starts S7, retained outputs S8, equations E1 to E14) and are cited as "L1 S3", "L1 E12" and so on, not repeated. The two-trajectory spec is cited as "L2" (T3, criterion 17). Spec-local equation labels are A1 to A16, open points P1 to P8. Element types: Float64, Double64 (effective epsilon 2^-100, precision spec Amendments) and BigFloat(256) for oracle runs; Float32 is out (D24). "n" counts map steps, handoffs fall at n = j tau (L1 S1); unit vectors are written u_i(n).

## Scope

- Catalog row I2 (`_config/catalogs/indicators.md`, section A): the Smaller Alignment Index of two unit deviation vectors, at every handoff, for the 2D symplectic maps of the P2 panel (Y1 standard map, Y5 linear maps). Row I3 (GALI_k, k = 2) is the next M4 module; A4 shows that its k = 2 value is the same quantity, so this spec fixes what `gali` will not have to re-derive.
- Prerequisites (confirmed `verified` on 2026-10-21): standard-map (catalog `systems.md` rows Y1, Y5; stage 04 report `standard-map-verification.md`, 90 PASS and 0 FAIL log lines), tangent-core (report `tangent-core-verification.md`, 62 PASS, 0 FAIL), precision (report `precision-verification.md`, 26 PASS, 0 FAIL), and the pathwise comparators L1 and L2 (`lyapunov.md` rows L1, L2 `verified`). The tangent-core stream, its seeded frame, health errors, scaled norms and compensated sums are used as they are, with the one question P1.
- The module is a reducer that never changes the orbit. It owns two deviation vectors (k = 2 columns of the stream matrix), normalises each column to unit length at every handoff, and reports SALI(n) and the floor-free log form of A7 (P2).
- Dimension: the definition (A1 to A4) holds for any dimension and any k-column frame pair; the relations A6 to A13 use d = 2 and det M = 1 and are labelled so. Higher-dimensional maps and flows are out of scope (M5).
- Out of scope: classification thresholds and the stopping rule (a study concern, `_config/numerics-policy.md`: thresholds are pre-registered per study, never hard-coded; Finding F1 below changes what a pre-registered threshold means for 2D maps), GALI_k with k > 2 (needs d >= 4, row I3), early termination of an orbit once SALI is zero (the module never stops an orbit: data-parallel kernels, equal cost per orbit, fair cost comparison in P2).
- Diagnostics: no diagnostics reducer is chained here; whether the diagnostics determinant residual applies to a column-normalised frame is not specified in the diagnostics spec and is not assumed (the determinant property enters through A7 and is tested by criterion 8).
- Keys: no `numerics-defaults.toml` key is proposed. tau stays `renorm_interval_tau`; default 1 (the sources normalise at every step). Only the overflow bound of the tangent-core spec (Algorithm, "Proposed renorm_interval_tau", item 1, with the k = 1 form of the override rule in item 3) limits an override, not its alignment bound (item 2): that bound protects orthonormalisers from losing the second column, whereas SALI normalises the columns separately and never separates them (derived; the information that is lost when the pair coincides is the signal itself, A14).
- Findings for the owner (details where cited):
  - F1. For 2D maps the regular-orbit statement of catalog row I2 ("fluctuates around positive value") and of the benchmarks row "SALI, regular orbit" does not hold. The sources say that for 2D maps SALI tends to zero for regular orbits too, by a power law (A9; Skokos2007 p.4, p.6). The rows are right for Hamiltonian flows and maps of dimension 4 and up. A study that classifies by "SALI below a pre-registered threshold at horizon n" must expect regular orbits to cross the threshold at a horizon set by the orbit (A9), so the classification is horizon dependent. Not edited here (Layer 3 files are outside this task).
  - F2. In Float64 the direct definition resolves a chaotic decay for only 10 to 18 map steps (A14). Any comparison with lambda_1(n) beyond that needs the log form (A7), which resolves it to any depth. This is why P2 asks for the log form.
  - F3. ChaosTools has GALI but no SALI routine (Oracle). Through A4 it is still an independent oracle for this module.
- Contract questions and open points: P1 (stream), P2 (log form), P3 to P8 at the end.
- Contract question for the owner (P1): the tangent-core stream hands every reducer one deviation matrix W and lets a reducer replace it by W S^-1 with S invertible (tangent-core spec E7); later reducers see the replaced matrix and an accumulated log belongs to the reducer that took the norm. SALI needs k = 2 and replaces W by the column-normalised matrix (S diagonal, allowed by E7). It needs only the directions of the two columns, which are unchanged by any per-column scaling (A1). Two conflicts follow. (i) A mixing reducer (Gram-Schmidt, Householder QR, stabilised SVD; S not diagonal) makes the columns orthonormal: placed before SALI it hides the alignment (SALI would read sqrt 2 forever), placed after SALI it receives unit-length columns and cannot recover the stretch factors its own sums need. (ii) A per-column reducer that needs the lengths (L1, and the later FLI, MEGNO) must run before SALI in the chain. Meanwhile `_config/architecture.md` (Indicators row) says all indicators are computed in ONE pass over the same orbit for a fair cost comparison, and P2 (D65) compares lambda_1(n), SALI, GALI_2, MEGNO, FLI/OFLI and RLI on the same orbits. Candidates. (a) Class-restricted chains, no contract change: per-column reducers (SALI, GALI_k, FLI, MEGNO, and L1 on a k = 1 run) share a chain in a fixed order; mixing reducers (the QR family) run as a separate pass; the orbit is bitwise the same in every pass (L1 S2), so comparisons stay pathwise, and the cost of a pass is reported per class. (b) Two named deviation blocks on the stream: each block has its own k and its own replacement, both evolve under the same tangent at the same orbit, reducers attach to one block; one pass, one orbit; cost: an amendment of the verified tangent-core spec (new memory formula, new carried state in the D44 relaunch), re-verification of tangent-core, and about a doubling of the tangent work (cheap against the orbit for 2D). (c) Recover the alignment from the R factors of a QR-family reducer: Y_n W_0 = Q_n P_n with P_n the product of the R factors, and the angle between the true vectors is the angle between the columns of P_n, so tan phi_n = abs(P_22)/abs(P_12), a ratio updated multiplicatively with no cancellation (drafted and checked, see Drafting checks); cost: the handoff must carry the R factor to later reducers, and Indicators would depend on the QR family's accuracy and on a sibling module (the Layering line puts Lyapunov, Indicators and Diagnostics at one level, so such an edge is not allowed as written; to be confirmed). This spec writes its criteria for the recommended default (a) and does not decide for the owner; criteria 7 to 14 and 16 to 18 hold under (a) and (b), only criterion 14(a) and the memory line of Compute change under (b).

## Equations

Notation. W_0 = (w_1(0), w_2(0)) is the initial pair, unit columns (the stream's seeded orthonormal frame, first two columns, L1 S2); w_i(n) = Y_n w_i(0) with Y_n the tangent product (L1 E1); u_i(n) = w_i(n)/norm(w_i(n)); theta_n the angle between u_1 and u_2 in [0, pi]; phi_n = min(theta_n, pi - theta_n) in [0, pi/2]; S_i(n) = ln norm(w_i(n)) is the sum of the log stretch factors of vector i (L1 E2, E3, E8; the initial norm is 1). ## Algorithm

Per orbit (ensemble member, orbit id j, run seed s), on the stream with k = 2, d = 2 (state length s >= d allowed, tangent-core Amendment D28: the tangent depends on the state only through cos x, so the module reads only the deviation matrix):
1. Initialisation. The pair is the first two columns of the seeded frame of the stream for (s, j), orthonormal, so SALI(0) = sqrt 2 and abs(det W_0) = 1. A supplied frame is accepted only if SALI(0) >= 10 B_meas(T); otherwise the run is rejected before any work with a numerical health error of kind initial frame (dependent vectors would give an absolute zero, A16, which reads as chaos).
2. At each handoff (step n = j tau), for i = 1, 2: alpha_i = Euclidean norm of column i, evaluated scaled so that it neither overflows nor underflows (tangent-core Algorithm, Scaled norms). If alpha_i is zero or not finite, raise a numerical health error naming the deviation matrix, with orbit id, column and handoff step (the finiteness check of the tangent-core health rule does not catch a zero norm). Add ln alpha_i to the compensated running sum S_i (tangent-core Accumulated logarithms). Replace column i by the unit vector (a diagonal S, tangent-core E7).
3. Direct form. d_- and d_+ from the two unit columns, each evaluated scaled (a plain sum of squares of a difference near the floor can underflow); SALI(n) = min(d_-, d_+).
4. Log form (P2). L(n) = ln abs(det W_0) - S_1(n) - S_2(n) (A7). Retained when P2 is answered yes (recommended).
5. Emit SALI(n) and L(n) at every handoff (storage schedule chosen by the caller, as L1 S8); at the end the final S_1, S_2 and compensation terms, SALI(0), and the floor B_meas(T) used.

Representation (the answer to "SALI goes to 0 exponentially"). (i) SALI itself, a number in [0, sqrt 2] stored as computed, including exact zeros; it is meaningful above B_meas(T) (A14) and an exact 0 or any value below B_meas means "below the floor". Narrowing to Float64 for the store never underflows for any of the three element types. (ii) The log form L(n) (natural log; log10 = L/ln 10 for display), finite and resolved to any depth: exp(L) would underflow Float64 at L about -745 (n of the order of 300 steps in a chaotic case), so L is stored, never exp(L). Conversion to SALI uses A4 and is valid for SALI >> eps. Neither form is clipped or replaced by a placeholder; a display of log10 SALI from (i) uses max(SALI, B_meas) and says so.

Parameters recorded per run: tau, element type, state mode (wrapped with counts, or lifted), seed, whether the frame is seeded or supplied, B_meas(T). Chain order (P1, default (a)): per-column reducers that need lengths before SALI.

## Invariants

- I1 Normalisation transparency (A1): SALI at a common n is independent of tau in exact arithmetic.
- I2 Range and algebra: SALI in [0, sqrt 2]; d_-^2 + d_+^2 = 4 within rounding; SALI is bitwise unchanged by a sign change of either column and by exchanging the columns (A3).
- I3 The orbit is unchanged by the module (L1 S2): orbit points bitwise equal across chains and across k.
- I4 Determinant identity (A7): for d = 2 symplectic maps GALI_2 = abs(det W_0) exp(-S_1 - S_2); SALI and L(n) agree through A4 above the floor.
- I5 Floor: computed SALI is within B_SALI(n) of the reference (A15), so no value below B_meas is reported as resolved.
- I6 Absorbing zero (A16): after the first exact 0 every later SALI is 0.
- I7 Orbit independence and execution invariance: orbit j's series depends only on (system, parameters, initial point, seed, j, tau, N, T, state mode), not on threads, chunk or position in the ensemble; across backends only to the criterion 18 tolerances. The hot loop allocates nothing after warm-up (BigFloat excepted, precision spec Compute).
- I8 Column independence: the arithmetic on column i does not read column j (the columns evolve independently, tangent-core E5), so the series S_i of the pair run equals that of a single-vector run on the identical orbit within the L1 bound (criterion 9).

## Compute

Policy `_config/architecture.md`, "Compute" (D17, D25). The module is per-orbit and data-parallel, fused into the stream's single kernel: per handoff two scaled norms, two logs, two scaled difference norms; no dense factorization, so no "reducer is CPU-only" fallback reason. Memory: the two log accumulators and two compensation terms are the k = 2 entries already counted by the tangent-core formula M(s, d, k, T) = sizeof(T)(s + d k + 2k) + 16, so SALI adds 0 bytes: s = d = 2, Float64: 96 bytes; s = 4 (wrapped state with counts), Float64: 112 bytes; s = 2, Double64: 176 bytes (L1 on its own k = 1 stream: 64 bytes). Under candidate (b) of P1 a second block adds sizeof(T) d k_b. Chunk size from free memory as in the tangent-core spec. GPU candidate for Float64 and Double64 (isbits); BigFloat is CPU-only with the reason "kernel does not compile for the element type" on a GPU request; GTX 1050, compute capability 6.1, weak Float64 (K15): the GPU is the default for an element type only after the benchmark records `gpu_faster` (tangent-core AC13; per-method record D54). ## Oracle

Package suite (analytic, invariant, cross-method, self-contained; D21): the closed forms A10 to A13 evaluated in BigFloat(256); the BigFloat(256) norm-form reference of A15 at the realized orbit points; the algebra of A2 to A4 on constructed pairs; tau invariance; the determinant identity; health; execution invariance. The closed forms are the primary oracle below the floor.

Verification environment `verify/`, driven from stage 04 (D21, D27): ChaosTools 3.5.4 (`verify/Manifest.toml`), installed source read on 2026-10-21 (`~/.julia/packages/ChaosTools/0SbsT/src/chaosdetection/gali.jl`, docs `docs/src/chaos_detection.md`, package test `test/chaosdetection/gali.jl`). Findings. (1) The package has `gali` (GALI_k) and no SALI routine (the package source contains no SALI code). (2) What it computes: it steps a tangent system with k deviation vectors, after every step normalises each column to Euclidean norm 1, takes the singular values of the resulting d x k matrix and records their product as GALI_k; the docstring says the implementation follows the singular-value method of a 2016 book chapter by Skokos and coauthors (its section 5.3.1; not in references.bib, not cited here) and not the original paper. For k = d = 2 the product of the singular values is abs(det) of the unit-column matrix, i.e. sin theta, which is GALI_2 of A4. (3) Settings: the step between normalisations is Delta t (default 1 for a map, so tau = 1); iteration stops at the first value below `threshold` (default 1e-12), so the default would end every chaotic run after about 10 to 25 steps and the threshold must be set to 0 to run the full horizon; the initial recorded value is 1 (assumes an orthonormal start); the Jacobian is automatic unless a prebuilt tangent system with a hand-coded Jacobian is passed (the route of the L1 verification script); the initial deviation matrix is a constructor argument and a restart accepts an explicit initial matrix (read in the source, not yet run in `verify/`), so the seeded frame can be supplied, unlike `lyapunovspectrum` (L1 Amendment D43). (4) Consequences. The comparison is made on GALI_2 through A4 (our SALI converted to g, or our log form exponentiated while above the floor). It is an independent code path (SVD against difference norms) and the same mathematical quantity (A4, Skokos2007 p.43). It shares the floor: the smaller singular value of a nearly rank-one matrix is known to an absolute error of about eps, so the oracle is blind below about 1e-15 in Float64. The orbit: with the lifted rule used in the L1 Oracle the orbit points should be bitwise those of the lifted stream (to be confirmed at stage 04); the tangent depends on x through cos x only, so a wrapped oracle rule would also give the same tangent. The two sides do their matrix-vector products and norms in different operation order, so pathwise agreement is to the sum of the two bounds, not bitwise. (5) Where it cannot help: below the floor, and for the log form beyond n_res; there the closed forms (A10 to A13) and the BigFloat reference are the oracles. Independent analytic oracles named: A10 (K = 0 shear), A11 (linear hyperbolic map), A12 (hyperbolic fixed point), A13 (elliptic fixed point and rotation).

Loose sanity lines (reported, never pass/fail, K18): the decay rate -L(n)/n over n in [n_a, n_b] after the transient against 2 ln(K/2) (Chirikov law, benchmarks row "Standard map largest Lyapunov exponent, large K": 1.83 at K = 5, 3.22 at K = 10) and 2 x 1.172 = 2.34 at K = 2 pi (row "Lyapunov exponent, single chaotic orbit", converted by L1 E9).

Drafting checks (not sources, not citable; throwaway Julia scripts in the scratchpad, never in the repo; to be reproduced at stage 04): (a) 2 sin((1/2) atan2(abs(det), abs(w_1 . w_2))) equals min(d_-, d_+) to 2e-16 on random pairs; (b) A10 with the seeded frame: SALI n^2 abs(b_1 b_2) = 0.574, 0.931, 0.993, 0.9993 at n = 10, 1e2, 1e3, 1e4; W_0 = I gives sqrt(2 - 2n/sqrt(n^2+1)) and 1/n; (c) A11: ratio to the asymptote 0.966 at n = 1 and 0.99999999 at n = 5, -ln SALI/(2n) approaching ln mu from below; (d) A13: SALI(n) alternates sqrt 2 and 0.2942 for one frame; (e) standard map K = 5, one start: in BigFloat(256) exp(-S_1 - S_2) equals the direct SALI to 4 digits from 7e-6 down to 2.6e-45 (n = 5..50), and the Float64 direct SALI reaches 0 at n = 20; K = 0.5 start (2, 0), Float64: SALI n^2 stays between 1.0e2 and 3.3e2 for n = 1e2..3e6 and agrees with exp(-S_1 - S_2) to 3 digits down to 1e-11; start (1, 0.3): n^2 SALI between 1 and 23, the two forms differ by 5e-13 at n = 3e6 (below the A15 bound); (f) the multiplicative route of P1(c) reproduces the same series to the accuracy of its orbit.
