# Test plan: lyap-householder-qr

Spec: `workspaces/module-build/stages/01-spec/output/lyap-householder-qr-spec.md` (D30: H2 diagonal path only, H1 and AC16 are out of M2), as amended by its Amendment D35 (unit theta_psi, criteria 7 and 8, 5(a), 5(c)). Bounds below are pointers to the spec; the plan derives nothing and has no tail rule. API contract: M2 Batch B `api-batch-b.md`, final (D34); names tested: `QRSpectrum`, `HouseholderQR`, `AugmentedQR`, `GivensQR`, `LapackQR`, `CGS`, `MGS`, `RGS`, `orthonormalize`, `QR_OK`, `effective_eps`, `reducer_provenance`, `NumericalHealthError.column`, `Benettin`, `SVDSpectrum`, `propagate_shadow`, and the Batch A names `propagate`, `propagate_orbit`, `ResidualMonitor`, `initial_deviations`, `StandardMap`, `WrappedStandardMap`, `LinearSymplecticMap`, `block_diagonal`, `hyperbolic_block`, `conjugated`, `resolve_backend`. Tests: `test/lyap-householder-qr/runtests.jl` including `hh_helpers.jl`, `hh_factor.jl` (AC1 to AC3), `hh_exact.jl` (AC4 to AC6), `hh_panel.jl` (AC7, AC13), `hh_cross.jl` (AC8), `hh_runtime.jl` (AC9 to AC12), `hh_backend.jl` (AC15). Helpers, test maps and reducers are prefixed `hh_` / `HH_`; nothing in `src/`, no top-level call into `Chaoslab.Lyapunov` (a missing module errors the testsets, not the run).

Conventions. Element types Float64 and Double64 (D24); every tolerance is in units of `effective_eps(T)` (Float64 eps, Double64 2^-100). Variants: V-H library (`LapackQR`, Float64 only, CPU only), V-H kernel (`HouseholderQR`), V-A (`AugmentedQR`), V-G (`GivensQR`) (`hh_algs(T)`, `hh_kernel_algs()`). Constants of the spec in one place: B_S of `lyap-benettin` S3 as amended (`hh_BS`, K = 0 default for the linear maps), theta_sum = 16 eps (G_max/tau + M_max) (`hh_theta`), theta_psi(n) = theta_sum + 8 eps Psi_n/n (`hh_theta_psi`, Psi_n from `lb_Psi` of `test/lyap-benettin/lb_psi.jl`, `hh_Psi` for a vector of orbits). References: 256-bit BigFloat QR with signs fixed to R_ii > 0 (`hh_ref_qr`), 2048-bit Gram determinants in AC6, exact BigInt Fibonacci products in AC5. Seeds: base 20261012 (`numerics-defaults.toml`) plus offsets 24000 to 24999 (`HH_BASE = 20261012 + 24000`), `hh_seed(ac, sub) = HH_BASE + 50 ac + sub` with sub < 50, so AC1 is 24050 to 24099, AC2 24100, AC4 24200, AC7 24350 to 24399, AC8 24400 to 24404, AC9 24450, AC10 24500, AC11 24550 to 24599, AC12 24600, AC13 24650, AC15 24750 (all inside 24000..24999; no Batch A offset below 20000 reused). Orbit ids are 1..N; the deviation matrix of orbit j is `initial_deviations(seed, j, Val(d), Val(k), T)`.

Test-side tools (`hh_helpers.jl`): `hh_Long` / `hh_Short` (state length 4 versus 2, same tangent; AC12), `hh_Degen` (identity tangent except for a chosen matrix at a chosen pre-step counter on flagged orbits; AC11), `hh_Dev` (reducer recording max |W - I| and the handoff count; AC4), `hh_FakeDevice` and `hh_with_device` (copy of the tangent-core pattern; AC15), `hh_regular_starts` (S7 screening of the regular class by a k = 1 `QRSpectrum` run, lambda <= 5e-4 at 1e5 steps; the replaced starts are counted), `hh_Mmax`, `hh_Gfirst` (largest tangent entry and G_first of the realized orbit).

## Deviations and ambiguities (owner decisions needed)

A1. Resolved by `lyap-householder-qr` Amendment D35. The stage 02 draft carried a neutral-orbit bound for AC8a and a sample-fraction tail rule (at most 6 percent of 50 orbits over, none by more than 16 times) for AC7, AC8a, AC8b, AC8c, because orbits that stick near an island exceed bounds linear in n. The spec amendment derives the reason (the amplification sum Psi_n of the orbit, not a rare event of the estimator) and replaces both with the unit theta_psi(n) = theta_sum + 8 eps Psi_n/n and the sharp 16 eps Psi_n/n; every orbit of every sample is now held to the bound. Owner questions: closed B_S form or the Psi form, and whether theta_psi moves to GS G13 (table, questions 1 and 3).

A2. AC5(c) and AC5(a). Resolved by the amendment: 5(c) is the exact E10 form (as the test already had it), 5(a) points to B_S of `lyap-benettin` S3 as amended (K_s = 0 for the linear map).

A3. Pathwise comparison with the two-trajectory estimator (AC8d): T5a of `lyap-two-trajectory` Amendment D35, no factor.

A4. Q11. The threshold of the zero-pivot case is not asserted in AC11; value 0.0 and column are.

A5. AC9, AC10 library paths. `LapackQR` is Float64 only and has a non-isbits workspace; the bitwise chunk invariance and the 0 byte requirement are tested in nested testsets that may fail legitimately. If so, the report to the owner is the result, not a change to the test.

## Package-suite size

Sample reductions for the 3 minute budget: AC7 Double64 uses 10 starts (the first 10 of the Float64 starts); AC8 is Float64 only (the unit is not measured for Double64, spec Amendment D35 evidence limits); AC10 bytes are measured per `reducer_step` (n = 100 for the orbit-level check). The full sizes (Double64 50 starts in AC7, Double64 in AC8) go to stage 04 `verify/`.

Time. The suite was run against a throwaway stub of `Chaoslab.Lyapunov` (kept in the scratchpad, not in the repo) that allocates heavily and runs unoptimised. Measured on that stub, one file per process on a shared machine, 4 threads: AC1 to AC3 3 min 15 s, AC4 to AC6 6 min 12 s, AC7 and AC13 3 min 38 s, AC8 3 min 16 s, AC9 to AC12 9 min 32 s, AC15 1 min 32 s (six to eight processes at once, so wall times are inflated; before the Amendment D35 edits, the Psi sums add one O(n) pass per orbit) (these include compilation of every (T, d, k, variant) specialisation). The test-side cost, measured separately with the stub's estimator removed: 256-bit references for AC1 about 20 s, AC7 about 20 s, AC6 under 1 s per case after compilation. AC10 (zero allocation) never passed on the stub: all 34 assertions fail there (112 to 10176 bytes per `reducer_step`, 22400 per `propagate_orbit`) because the stub allocates; the testset logic (inputs built outside the measured call, a warm-up call before `@allocated`) has been inspected, not exercised passing, so stage 03 is its first real run. AC9 (including the library path), AC11 and AC12 passed on the stub. The stub is far slower than a kernel with zero allocation, so the 3 minute budget is not proven by this run; stage 03 must time the real suite and report. If it is over, cut in this order: AC9 chunk 64 (the second chunk size keeps the check), AC7 tau 4, AC8b K = 10, AC1 200 W to 100 W.
