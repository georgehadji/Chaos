## Design

- Flow side (Systems). `AbstractFlow`, `AbstractSeparableFlow` (H = |p|^2/2 + V(q)); a separable flow gives `force` (grad V), `force_jacobian` (Hessian V), `hamiltonian` and the corrector pair; `drift`, `kick`, their increments and Jacobians, `hamiltonian_gradient`, `vector_field` follow once in `flow_core.jl`. Models: `HarmonicOscillator` (F1, `SVector` of frequencies), `Pendulum` (F4, `eps`), `Kepler` (F2), `HenonHeilesFlow` (T2), `TaoNonseparable` (T1, gradient, Hessian, vector field and its Jacobian). `CountingFlow` counts `force` calls (not isbits). The partial flows are `@noinline`: a stepper is compiled for every table length, so the flow operations are compiled once per flow and element type instead (the Double64 tangent of 120 method and flow pairs went from 56 s to 12 s of compile time).
- One stepper. `SplittingMethod{T,K,Name,Source}` holds two `NTuple{K,T}` (drift weights `c`, kick weights `d`) and order, palindromic flag, digit count; isbits. Name and source are Symbol type parameters so that the method can be a kernel argument. `advance_n` runs the stages with a pending kick weight: consecutive kicks with no drift between them, also across the step boundary, are applied as one kick (leapfrog KDK n + 1 evaluations, DKD n); `compensated = true` carries the updates as value and correction term per component (increments from the flow, two-sum, Hairer2008 Sect. 2). `tangent_advance` multiplies the elementary Jacobians stage by stage with `SMatrix` frames (heap-free); the elementary operations are `@noinline` for compile time. `IntegratorMap` is an `AbstractMap` (forward `advance`, inverse the adjoint table at `-tau`, tangent from the identity frame), consumed by `Tangent.propagate` bitwise as the serial iteration (API testset).
- Builders. `_compose` concatenates scaled pieces and merges the drifts between kicks (`_normalize`); `triple_jump` and `suzuki_jump` run in the element type with the real roots computed in it (Yoshida1990 Eqs. (4.12) to (4.14); `s = 1/(4 - 4^(1/(p+1)))`). S5 is `triple_jump(Leapfrog, 4)`; Yoshida, Kahan-Li and Sofroniou-Spaletta tables are palindromic compositions of the leapfrog with the printed step fractions. Palindromic tables are bitwise symmetric (the mirrored halves are the same floating point numbers).
- Constants. Decimal constants are strings in the source files, each with source key and equation or table number in the comment, parsed in the element type (`parse(T, s)`): Yoshida1990 Table 1 solution A and Table 2 solutions A and D (15 digits), KahanLi1997 Appendix A s7odr6, s9odr6a, s17odr8a (20), SofroniouSpaletta2005 Eq. (17) (32), Omelyan2002 Eq. (20) PEFRL (16), CalvoSanzSerna1993a Eq. (2.8) (18), Blanes2013 Tables 3 and 4 ABA864, ABAH864 (40), LaskarRobutel2001 Table II corrector decimals (40), Ruth1983 Eq. (31) (rational). Computed: Gauss coefficients, the triple jump and Suzuki roots, McLachlan z (Eq. (4.2)), SABA and SBAB (below).
- Near-integrable rows. SABA_n kicks at the n Gauss-Legendre nodes with the quadrature weights, SBAB_n at the n + 1 Gauss-Lobatto nodes (LaskarRobutel2001 Sect. 7 Eq. (47) derives them as the Gauss formula): computed in 320-bit arithmetic and rounded into the element type, so n = 6 and 10 need no table. `SABAC`, `SBABC` add the corrector kick `p += kappa grad W`, `kappa = tau^3 eps^2 c/2`, `W = |grad V|^2` (`CorrectedMethod`).
- Gauss. Coefficients from the Legendre nodes and the collocation conditions in 320 bits, split into a machine part and a remainder (Hairer2008 Eq. (3.1)). Fixed-point iteration from the Euler guess; stop when the largest stage increment is 0 or no longer smaller; update from f at the stages of the previous iteration; accepted when the final increment is at most `8 eps max(1, |Y|)`; otherwise an all-NaN state and `ok = false`. The status is a NamedTuple `(ok, iterations, increment, stages)`. The tangent differentiates the converged stage equations (dense `s 2n` system, heap, host only). `standard = true` is the Fig. 2.1 implementation (stop below 2 eps, rounded coefficients, update from f at the final stages).
- Tao. `TaoExtended(T, omega; order)` on the state (q; p; x; y); the three partial flows of Tao2016 Eq. (1), Strang Eq. (2), higher order by the triple jump with the exponent 1/(l - 1); elementary Jacobians written out (needs `hamiltonian_hessian`).
- Ensembles. One KernelAbstractions kernel over orbits, `advance_ensemble(m, flow, x0, tau, n; backend = :cpu, chunk)`, the orbit loop is `advance_n` (or the checked Gauss step), so the result of an orbit is independent of the chunking (bitwise, AC18). `:gpu` resolves through a hook `ensemble_backend(::NoDevice) = CPU()` which a device extension extends (stage 04).
- R1. `taylor_reference` is declared in Integrators; `ext/ChaoslabTaylorExt.jl` adds the method through `TaylorIntegration.taylorinteg` (order 40, tolerance 2^-200, `parse_eqs = false`) and the plain `sin` and `cos` for Taylor polynomials in the flows' guarded `_sin`, `_cos`. Pendulum T = 8 at 256 bits: 1.99 s, difference to Gauss s = 8 5.7e-43, energy drift 4.6e-63.

## Deviations from the spec and the plan

1. SABA and SBAB are computed from quadrature nodes, not copied from LaskarRobutel2001 Table I. Checked against the printed 36 digits (SABA6 and SABA10 c and d, SBAB7 and SBAB10 c and d, d1 = 1/56, 1/110, d6 = 32768/218295, SABA3 closed forms): largest difference 6e-37 (the rounding of the print). `table_digits` is 40, as the plan requires.
2. ABA864: the printed b_i of Blanes2013 Table 3 (arXiv v3, checked against the page image) sum to 1 only to 5.0e-31 (a_i and ABAH864 sum to 1e-39 or better; ABA104 not used). A construction with the sum condition at 40 digits rejects it. The central b4 is recomputed as `1 - 2 (b1 + b2 + b3)` (a change of 5.0e-31 in 0.065). The remaining conditions of the table hold to the accuracy of the print, which I did not check beyond the slopes (AC6: 3.998, 4.000 for ABA864, 3.9998, 3.99996 for ABAH864). To confirm in stage 04 with the published Table 3 or the REBOUND arrays (O8), which carry the same numbers.
3. Gauss default `maxiter`: 50 for Float64 and Double64 (spec), `precision(BigFloat)` bits for BigFloat. A contraction of 0.05 gains 4.3 bits per iteration; 256 bits need 59 iterations, so a 50 iteration cap makes the default 256-bit Gauss fail at tau = 0.1 on the pendulum, which AC17 uses without a `maxiter` argument. This change moves one AC7 assertion (see item 3 of the failing list).
4. Constructors without the element type exist for the explicit rows (`Leapfrog(:KDK)`, `Yoshida(6, :A)`, `KahanLi(8)`, `SABA(3)`, ...), as the plan says.
5. Yoshida1990 Tables 1 and 2: the text layer scrambles the columns of the tables (spec Sources); solutions A (6th and 8th) and D (8th) are the labelled, single-column lists w_1..w_7 of the left column, read from the text layer; they are confirmed by the table identities and the orders (6th A equals KahanLi s7odr6 to the print, AC3b; slopes 5.99 and 7.97 to 7.98, AC4). The page images were not read for these two.
6. PEFRL parameters of Omelyan2002 Eq. (20): the text layer lost the Greek letters; the three values are in the order (xi, lambda, chi) of the equation and the roles follow the algorithm Eq. (22) (drifts xi, chi, 1 - 2 (chi + xi), chi, xi; kicks (1 - 2 lambda)/2, lambda, lambda, (1 - 2 lambda)/2). Confirmed by the measured order 4.00 (AC4).
7. Corrector coefficients, Table II: the text layer prints SABA3 as `(54 - 13 sqrt(5))/648`, which is 0.0385 and breaks the monotone sequence; the coefficient is `(54 - 13 sqrt(15))/648` (0.0056346). I checked every closed form and decimal for n = 1 to 5 (both families) by the BCH series of the quadratic Hamiltonian (mpmath, 80 digits, matrix logarithm of the F1 step, coefficient of eps^2 tau^2 q^2 divided by 2): agreement to 8 printed digits for all ten (script `ic03/chk_corr.py`). The corrector sign `p += kappa grad W` follows from the same series (the modified Hamiltonian carries `+ c tau^2 eps^2 |grad V|^2`); AC6d passes (error ratio at least 10, slope near 4).
8. `perturbation_scale` of flows without eps is the integer 1 (type-neutral).
9. `advance_ensemble` explicit methods report `ok = true` always (a non-finite orbit gives a non-finite state, no flag); Gauss reports the status per orbit.
10. The compensated update of `advance_n` is for the table methods and Gauss; corrected methods and Tao reject it with `ArgumentError`.
11. The Taylor extension defines `Chaoslab.Systems._sin`, `_cos` for Taylor polynomials (a method on a library function for a foreign type, not piracy).

## Limits and open items

- Tao's tangent frame lives in the extended space (open point P11: no physical projection defined).
- The Gauss tangent builds a dense `s 2n` system on the heap (host only; not for the kernel).
- `advance_ensemble` on a device is not exercised here (stage 04, G); the kernel is the CPU kernel with the orbit loop of `advance_n`.
- ABA864 b4 (deviation 2) and the Yoshida A and D columns read from the text layer (deviation 5): stage 04 confirms against the images and the REBOUND arrays.
- Compile time per table length (one specialization per method type): a study that builds many distinct tables pays about 25 ms per table for `advance` in Float64.
- `energy_error_tol` (P13): no value written.
