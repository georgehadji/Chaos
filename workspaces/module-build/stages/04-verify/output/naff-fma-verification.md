Working tree of branch `main`, nothing committed; the code verified is the final working tree (uncommitted: the stage 03 fixes of the independent review M1 to M3 and the LOW items, the later regression fix in `reducer_init` / `check_run`, and the new tests `test/naff-fma/nf_review.jl`). Criterion 15(b), the one FAIL, in words. The gate as set for this stage (D68 form: CPU against GPU only on the orbits whose final states agree within 1e-9) fails on the regular class: the largest ratio is 17.6, and the orbits that agree to 1e-9 still differ by up to 3.8e-10 in state. The causes are in the log: (i) each backend alone passes the bound B_E2 of criterion 10(d) with a ratio of at most 0.052 against its own BigFloat evaluation (the actual criterion text of 15: "pass if every orbit's two frequencies lie within B_E2"), so the algebra of the device reducer is correct on its own samples; (ii) fed the same samples, the device reducer and the CPU pure function agree within 0.033 (regular) and 0.062 (chaotic) of 2 B_E2; (iii) the CPU and GPU orbits are different orbits (a rounding-level difference of the device sin, grown linearly by the shear), and a regular orbit changes its frequency with its torus: the largest gap divided by the state distance of its orbit pair is 0.029 over the 1024 orbits (frequency change per unit of state difference, the sensitivity of the regular class), and among the 696 orbits with a state distance of at most 1e-11 the largest gap is 0.63 of B_cpu + B_gpu. B_E2 bounds the algebra at given samples; it carries no orbit-divergence term, and a selection by 1e-9 in the state lets in orbits whose frequency differs by more than B_E2 for that reason alone. This is a defect of the gate as formulated for this stage, not of the module; the sali, rli and stretching forms carry such a term or a tighter selection. The form the orchestrator proposes as Amendment D82 (proposed, not approved): the module-owned check is the device reducer against the CPU pure function on identical samples (0.033 of 2 B_E2, a real gate in `verify/gpu.jl`), and the orbit-level CPU-GPU frequency gap is reported with its measured ratio, not gated. The row stays FAIL here, as measured under the gate set for this stage. The spec text of criterion 15 itself (each backend within B_E2) is met.
