## Oracle lines (criteria 12 and 13), as the spec states them

Criterion 12(a): the Poisson form WB17, f = cos 2 pi (1/7 + j (sqrt 2 - 1)), the weighted average of window 1 of the module's function in BigFloat(256) against the mpmath 30-digit evaluation (quadrature of W(s) in one subinterval per unit of s, the sums over the terms with abs(s) up to 500; the values 3.6e-7, 1.7e-10, -1.2e-14 are 10 to 14 orders above the gate, so the 1e-18 gate resolves them): 7.4e-33, 1.5e-33, 1.3e-33. The spec dry run (1.9e-21, 2.1e-30, 3.8e-43) was limited by its truncation; the agreement here is limited by the 30 digits.
