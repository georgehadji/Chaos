# Verified annotated bibliography: Hamiltonian chaos, chaos indicators, symplectic maps and integrators, Solar System dynamics

Verification method: each entry checked against Crossref (DOI resolves, title, authors, volume, pages) by script (check.py), plus arXiv/OJS pages and full texts where benchmark values were read. Entries tagged [verified-secondary] have no DOI and were confirmed only through independent reference lists or a library/registry record. Nothing is cited from memory alone; items I could not confirm are in UNVERIFIED.

Totals: 180 entries (173 verified by DOI/arXiv/DataCite record, 7 verified-secondary without DOI), 14 UNVERIFIED/caveat items.

## 1. Maps and model systems

- **Chirikov1979** B. V. Chirikov (1979). A universal instability of many-dimensional oscillator systems. *Physics Reports* 52(5), 263-379. doi:10.1016/0370-1573(79)90023-1 -- Resonance-overlap criterion; origin of the standard map and of the large-K logarithmic law for its Lyapunov exponent.
- **Greene1979** J. M. Greene (1979). A method for determining a stochastic transition. *Journal of Mathematical Physics* 20(6), 1183-1201. doi:10.1063/1.524170 -- Residue criterion; golden-mean torus breakup in the standard map defines the benchmark K_c.

- **Henon1969** M. Hénon (1969). Numerical study of quadratic area-preserving mappings. *Quarterly of Applied Mathematics* 27(3), 291-312. doi:10.1090/qam/253513 -- The quadratic area-preserving (Hénon) map: second 2D symplectic test map besides the standard map.
